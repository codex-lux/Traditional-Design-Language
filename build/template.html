Reading the chart

Each bar is one taxon, drawn across the years it was actively built. The time axis is deliberately non-linear — antiquity is compressed so the last four centuries can breathe. Segment rates are printed on the ruler.

A thin ticked line under a bar marks a later revival — the years someone deliberately built the style again after it had stopped.

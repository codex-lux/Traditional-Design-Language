# OQ 54 — the heuristic places rooms below their band and nothing says so

*Status: RULED AGAIN 26 AUG 2026 · Raised in: From the real solver (WP-2.3, 24 Aug 2026)*

**RULED AGAIN 26 Aug 2026 — the critic reads the drawn house after all, in one layer and only one.** Lucas reversed the August ruling on reading Lucas's review of two rendered sheets. The 24 Aug ruling below deliberately took option (b) — report the under-band placement in `geometry_report` — and deliberately refused option (c), teaching `plan_check` to read `room.geometry`, as "a larger and separate change every plan layer would feel". `workbench/server/evaluate.py` recorded the same position as "a settled fact, not an oversight".
**What that cost, measured on the two shipped plans.** While it held, the critic scored the house the record DECLARED and the sheet drew the house the solver PLACED, and nothing compared them. So: a landing drawn entirely clear of its own stair produced no finding; a kitchen drawn at 63% of its declared area produced none; twenty rooms drawn off their declaration on one plan produced none, the worst by +303%; and three habitable rooms whose only declared doors the placement could not realise — a library, a breakfast room and a chamber bath — produced none, because the DECLARED door graph was fine. Every one of those is a defect a reader saw on the sheet within a minute.
**What was built (WP-6.2).** A `drawn` layer in `build/plan_check.py`, the ONLY layer permitted to read placement; every other layer stays geometry-blind, which is what keeps the original ruling's real concern intact. Three states throughout: a record nobody has placed reports COULD NOT EVALUATE and takes no drawn finding at all, so a hand-authored plan is never failed on facts about a placement that does not exist. It reports reachability (fatal), a room joined to nothing inside the house, drawn-against-declared in both directions, the landing over its stair, passage clear width, and fixtures that will not fit. **The under-band report the 24 Aug ruling asked for is kept** — it is the solver's own account of itself and this is the critic's; they are different questions and both are now answered. *The original ruling follows, and it is worth reading: it was right about the cost and wrong about the balance.*<br><br>**RULED 24 Aug 2026 — report it in the geometry report.** `geometry_report` gains an explicit under-band list, the way `build/solver.py`'s `unmet_requirements` already works. The search keeps its freedom to trade a room's size against everything else, which is what a heuristic is for, and stops doing it silently. Deliberately NOT the deeper fix of teaching `plan_check.py` to read `room.geometry`: that would have the critic score the drawn house rather than the declared one, which is a larger and separate change every plan layer would feel. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**OPEN — the heuristic places rooms below their band and nothing says so.** `build/geometry.py` puts the spec Colonial's dining room at 91 sf against the 122 sf floor of its own catalog band — 26% short — and it does so on every one of the six seeds WP-2.3 tried. It can, because `level_score` charges a flat 12 points for a room under its band and a candidate can win the search while paying it. What makes this hard to see is that the plan *record* still declares 12 × 12: only the placement shrinks the room, and `plan_check.py` never reads `room.geometry` at all, so no layer of the critic is looking at the number that changed. `build/solver.py` refuses to do it — the room minimum is the one requirement it will not trade away, and the refusal is what turns an over-stuffed brief into a named conflict instead of a bad plan — but the heuristic remains the default engine everywhere, so the corpus's usual placement still does this silently. Three fixes suggest themselves and the choice is a judgment: (a) make the heuristic refuse too, rejecting any candidate with a room under its band, which is the most consistent but may leave some plans with no candidate at all and no explanation of why; (b) have `geometry_report` carry an explicit under-band list the way the solver's `unmet_requirements` does, which keeps the search's freedom while ending the silence; or (c) teach `plan_check.py` to read `room.geometry` when it is present, which is the deepest fix — the critic would then score the house that was drawn rather than the house that was declared — and much the largest, since every plan layer currently assumes the record's declared dimensions are the truth. Worth noting that (c) is the only one that would also catch this class of defect from any *future* engine. Surfaced by WP-2.3; nothing was changed on the strength of it.

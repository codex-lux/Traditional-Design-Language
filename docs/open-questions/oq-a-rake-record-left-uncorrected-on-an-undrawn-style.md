# oq/a-rake-record-left-uncorrected-on-an-undrawn-style — folk-victorian inherits a rake its own kit contradicts

*Status: OPEN · Raised in: WP-16.9, the roof, the rake and the deeper cornice (1 October 2026), on T8, taken as recommended under Lucas's standing instruction of 1 October 2026 and never put to him*

**folk-victorian leaves its `rake_condition` open, and reads american-farmhouse-vernacular's.** That record
states *"Plain rake trim 6-8 in, occasionally carrying a single jigsawn gable ornament -- one piece of
applied decoration, never a full profiled rake or bargeboard."* WP-16.9 transcribed its 6 to 8 in
into a parameter (B5), so folk-victorian now reads a drawable plain trim of 7 in.

**folk-victorian's own kit permits a pierced bargeboard.** Its `gable_treatment` makes
`pierced-bargeboard` permitted, noted *"The oldest millwork layer, inherited from Gothic Revival"*,
and its lineage names *"pierced bargeboard and the single pointed gable sash"* as its Gothic layer.
The inherited rule's *"never ... bargeboard"* contradicts it. The contradiction predates WP-16.9.

**Why it was not corrected (T8).** B5 corrects an inherited rake record a style's own words
contradict, in the style's own kit. A folk-victorian record would be the record
new-urbanist-traditional reads ahead of the farmhouse's. new-urbanist-traditional is drawn, on
bad-03, and folk-victorian is not. So the correction would change which record a shipped plan
reads, by an answer nobody chose. The farmhouse parameter's note names it.

**Not swept.** B5's adjudication read the 41 drawn styles. Whether other undrawn styles inherit a
rake their own words contradict has not been measured.

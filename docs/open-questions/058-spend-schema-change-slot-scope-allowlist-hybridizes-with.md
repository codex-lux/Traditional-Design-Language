# OQ 58 — spend the schema change: a slot-scope allowlist on `hybridizes_with`

*Status: RULED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: Ruled with the rest, 24 August 2026*

**RULED 24 Aug 2026 — spend the schema change: a slot-scope allowlist on `hybridizes_with`.** The problem, found in WP-4.2 and patched twenty-six times at the node rather than once at the mechanism: an edge drawn to carry one narrow aspect of a donor's practice transmits that donor's *entire* kit, because the cascade cannot partition a donor's bindings by which aspect the edge was drawn for. Greek Revival's hybrid edges bring Greek Revival's whole cornice apparatus with them whether or not the edge was about cornices. The ruling is to add an optional slot-scope allowlist to the edge, so `hybridizes_with` carries only the slots it was drawn for and carries everything only when no allowlist is given. That fixes the class rather than the instances, makes the twenty-six node-level patches removable one by one as each edge gains its scope, and — the reason it is worth a schema version — stops the next kit author rediscovering the same thing from scratch. The alternative considered and rejected was narrowing `hybridizes_with` to carry no kit content at all, like `references`: that would be cheaper but would lose the genuine partial inheritance the edge exists to express, and the corpus does have edges that legitimately transmit several slots. Costs a schema bump and a re-resolve of every kit with a hybrid edge in its chain.

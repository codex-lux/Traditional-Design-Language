# OQ 56 — there is no half-storey, so the split-level cannot be expressed

*Status: RULED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: From partis to full coverage (WP-4.5, 24 Aug 2026)*

**RULED 24 Aug 2026 — add `level_offset_ft`.** `level` stays an integer and keeps its current meaning, driving stacking, vertical alignment and storey heights; an optional `level_offset_ft` sits beside it, defaults to 0, and is read only by the section and the stair check. Every existing parti and every consumer works unchanged. The split-level parti becomes writable once it lands. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**OPEN — there is no half-storey, so the split-level cannot be expressed.** `schema/parti.schema.json` types `rooms[].level` as an integer and every consumer reads it as a level index — `compose.py` maps `{0: "ground", 1: "upper", -1: "cellar"}`, `geometry.py`'s `prepare_rooms()` keys its working dict by it, `solver.py` inherits that. A split-level Ranch, which `PLAN-OF-ACTION.md`'s WP-4.5 text names among the partis to add, is a house whose defining property is that two of its floors are half a storey apart, and there is nowhere in the record to say so. It was left unbuilt rather than faked: the type unblocks **zero** nodes on this package's own criterion (its two `common` styles are already covered by `ranch-tripartite`, and no style has `split-level` as canonical), so spending a schema change to reach it would have been paying a real cost for no coverage. The concrete proposal for whoever rules, so the next person does not re-derive it: `level` stays an integer and keeps its current meaning, and an optional `level_offset_ft` is added beside it, defaulting to 0 — the level index continues to drive stacking, alignment and the storey heights, and the offset is read only by the section and the stair check. That keeps every existing parti and every consumer working unchanged.

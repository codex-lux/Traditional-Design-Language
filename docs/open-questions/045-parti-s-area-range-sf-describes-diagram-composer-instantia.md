# OQ 45 — A parti's `area_range_sf` describes the diagram and the composer instantiates a fixed room list

*Status: CLOSED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: From the real solver (WP-2.3, 25 Aug 2026)*

**CLOSED 24 Aug 2026 — the four ranges corrected, the type's true size moved to prose, and a check so it cannot recur.** Measured with every room at its catalogue band top: **seventeen of twenty-one partis reach their own declared maximum comfortably** and four could not — `courtyard-and-portal` at 43% of a stated 9,000 sf, `great-hall-h-plan` 82% of 12,000, `living-hall-picturesque` 96%, `tower-villa` 97%. One bad case and three near misses, not a systemic problem, and all four corrected for the same reason rather than the small ones left because they are small. `area_range_sf` now means what the AUTHORED room list can be built at; the fact that a real hacienda runs to 9,000 sf — by having more rooms and a second court, which is exactly what the composer cannot do — is in each parti's description, naming the figure it replaced so the correction records what it corrected. `check_partis.py` gains check 11, which computes the ceiling the way `compose.instantiate` actually builds (rooms marked `repeats_with_bedrooms` counted once per bedroom at the top of the parti's own `bedroom_range`, because a bound computed against a room list the composer never instantiates would be measuring the wrong house). Partis missing their own midpoint by more than 12% went from three to one. The larger option — letting the composer add rooms from the parti's `groupings` when the target demands it — was NOT taken: it means the composer inventing plan content rather than instantiating it, which is the thing it is built to refuse, and it needs its own package and its own rules about what it may invent. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**A parti's `area_range_sf` describes the diagram and the composer instantiates a fixed room list.** Surfaced by OQ 40, which made the gap measurable rather than creating it. `courtyard-and-portal` declares 1,800–9,000 sf and its seventeen rooms reach about 3,200; `great-hall-h-plan` declares 2,800–12,000 and reaches about 6,500. Both figures are honest about the TYPE — a hacienda really does run to 9,000 sf — and neither is reachable by the only mechanism the composer has for growing a house, which is making the same rooms bigger until each hits its catalogue band. The one thing that does add rooms, `repeats_with_bedrooms`, adds bedrooms and nothing else. So a large brief against a large-range parti returns a house that misses its target by 40% and a decision log correctly saying the diagram grows by having more rooms — with no way to act on it. Three ways out: mark rooms in a parti as `repeats_with_area` so a courtyard gains a second court range and a manor a second wing at stated thresholds; declare the range honestly as what the AUTHORED room list reaches and record the type's true range separately as prose; or let the composer add rooms from the parti's `groupings` when the target demands it, which is the most useful and the largest, since it means the composer inventing plan content rather than instantiating it — the thing it is currently built to refuse.

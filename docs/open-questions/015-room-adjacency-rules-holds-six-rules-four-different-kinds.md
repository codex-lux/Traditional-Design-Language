# OQ 15 — `room_adjacency_rules` holds six rules of four different kinds in one flat set

*Status: CLOSED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: From authoring the Georgian kit*

**CLOSED 24 Aug 2026 — the six are now two, and the slot has a typed shape.** (The slot is `room_adjacency_overrides`; the entry's old title named it by the parameter inside it.) A `rules` array joins the kit schema for any slot the ontology types `value_type: rule` — there are 17 — each entry carrying a **kind** (topology, axis, sequence, daylight, element-placement, hierarchy, orientation) and an **effect** against the universal room records (adds, restricts, suppresses). The one-sentence `rule` string stays: prose stays beside the test. **Of the Georgian six:** two were about the adjacency graph and survive as typed rules, the second of them folding in a clause that restated the first. Three were duplication of slots that already say the same thing and are DELETED rather than moved — 'the best room is at the front, away from the service end' is `public_private_gradient`'s own rule sentence, 'no principal room is a through-route to another' is that slot's 'no room is entered through another', and 'windows on two walls of every principal room' is `daylight_strategy`'s `walls_lit_per_principal_room: 2` verbatim. One was not about adjacency at all — where the chimney breast sits in the room — and moved to `hearth_position`. The other seven populated kits are classified in place. **And the executable half:** the slot's own note has said since ontology 0.4.0 that it holds 'only what a style ADDS or SUPPRESSES', and nothing read it — a suppression existed as prose while the validator went on enforcing the rule the kit said should not apply. `plan_check.kit_suppressions()` now reads it across the whole inheritance chain, and `check_kits` refuses a suppression with no machine-readable target or one naming a rule that is not there. **No style declares a suppression yet, and that is stated rather than assumed:** all eight prose statements in the corpus restrict or add, and inventing one to exercise the code is exactly what this corpus forbids. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**`room_adjacency_rules` holds six rules of four different kinds in one flat set.** No schema change fixes this; it needs the room catalogue. (The room catalogue now exists — `rooms/`, 58 types — so this is unblocked and should be revisited; not yet done.)

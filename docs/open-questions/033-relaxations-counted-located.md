# OQ 33 — relaxations are counted but not located

*Status: CLOSED 26 AUG 2026 · Raised in: From the workbench (WP-5.2, 25 Aug 2026)*

**CLOSED 26 Aug 2026 — P7 is met: a compromise is counted AND drawn where it falls, in both engines and both renderers.** A relaxation is no longer a bare float. `build/geometry.py`'s cuts each record `{off_ft, axis, at_ft, from_ft, to_ft, level}` — the axis the cut runs across, where the line sits, its extent, and which storey — and `geometry_cp.py`'s counter, which already knew the edge coordinate and threw it away, records the same shape. `geometry_report.relaxations.marks` carries them beside the count that was always honest.
Both renderers draw them: `build/render_plan.py` and the workbench's `Sheet.jsx` put a hollow triangle on the line itself, dashed, **in ink and never in colour** — colour in this drawing names a material and does not flag a condition. Verified end to end: the Tidewater plan reports 11 cuts and the SVG contains exactly 11 marks, which is P6 and P7 in one assertion — the number on the sheet is the number in the record, not a number the renderer chose. A mark from the CP engine carries no extent, because an edge there is a wall line shared by however many rooms abut it and inventing a span would be a drawn claim nobody measured; it gets a short centred tick instead.
**A near-miss worth recording.** The first version tested `if round(d, 2)` where the original tested `if d`, which swallowed a sub-half-inch miss and took the pinned relaxation count from 11 to 10. Nothing failed loudly; the number simply changed. It was caught because WP-2.3 had pinned 11 as proof that its footprint-helper extraction changed no numeric behaviour, and the pin is now also a test in `tests/test_measurement_honesty.py`. A count that moves silently is the whole reason these numbers are pinned. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**OPEN — relaxations are counted but not located.** `geometry.solve()` reports `relaxations = {count, max_off_grid_ft, note}`; the individual cuts' positions, levels and kinds are computed and discarded inside `slice_rect`. P7 says compromises "appear on the drawing, at their location" — the workbench can currently render only the honest tally ("11 cuts off the bay line, worst 4.8 ft") at the plate caption, not a marker where each cut falls. Recording `{level, x_ft, y_ft, off_ft}` per relaxation is additive to the report and would let both the workbench and `render_plan.py` mark them in place. Surfaced by WP-5.2 (the design mockup had invented positioned markers, tagged `[G]`, precisely because the real data lacks them).

# OQ 67 — the composer now recommends different plans, and that was a side effect rather than a decision

*Status: OPEN · Raised in: From the candidate score (26 Aug 2026) — reissued 66-68 at the 26 Aug merge*

**OPEN — the composer now recommends different plans, and that was a side effect rather than a decision.** Replacing the demerit total with the composite changed the returned SET on both shipped briefs, not merely the order they are read in. `tests/test_composer.py`'s two identity pins cover position 1 and passed throughout, which is how it went unnoticed until an adversarial audit measured the whole set. The two rankings cannot be reconciled at any weighting: `demerits` charges 1 a minor without limit while an axis charges half a room however many minors land on it, so a plan with many minors concentrated on few rooms is treated very differently by the two. A sweep of fidelity's weight from 18 to 60 reproduces neither brief's old set at any value. **What was done:** fidelity's weight was set from a measurement — the observed 18.0/22.0-point spread of the whole non-fidelity subtotal between clean candidates — so that being the right diagram can decide, which is WP-4.5's ruling; at the original 18 a tidewater-georgian brief returned an octagon and a tower villa over the native side-hall town house. The resulting sets are more native than the demerit total's own answer on both briefs (fit 7.0/7.0/3.6/2.0 and 7.0/3.6/3.6, against 7.0/3.6/2.0/3.2 and 7.0/2.0/2.0). **What is open:** nobody has ruled that the new sets are the right ones. They are defensible by WP-4.5's own criterion and they are not what the composer said yesterday, and only Lucas can say whether a diagram like `five-part-palladian` belongs in a 3,200 sf family brief's four. The cheap test is the same one OQ 64 wants: rank a dozen candidates by hand across several briefs and see which ordering agrees. Until then this is a stated, measured, guarded change and not an endorsed one — `tests/test_score.py::test_the_returned_set_is_native_dominated_not_merely_tidy` fails if it drifts further.

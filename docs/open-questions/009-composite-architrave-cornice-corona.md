# OQ 9 — The Composite architrave and cornice corona

*Status: OPEN · Raised in: From the proportion layer*

**OPEN — The Composite architrave and cornice corona** in Vignola are the weakest data in the order corpus — three transcriptions all say only "as the Corinthian, fewer and larger details, no brackets." Encoded as two fascias on that authority, with a three-fascia reading recorded as defensible.

# OQ 13 — The entablature is scattered across three slot groups

*Status: CLOSED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: From authoring the Georgian kit*

**CLOSED 24 Aug 2026 — the cross-references exist, and they now enforce the thing they were for.** WP-1.3 added `derives_from_module` to the slot schema and populated it on five slots pointing at `entablature` as the root (`cornice`, `frieze`, `modillion_dentil`, `crown`, `chair_rail`); no regrouping was done, as ruled. `build/validate.py` has checked since then that each reference resolves and that no slot references itself. What nothing checked was the ruling's actual purpose — that a chair rail's module derives *from the same run* as the exterior cornice rather than being independently invented. `build/check_kits.py::check_derived_module_family` now does: within a kit, members of a family that both record a `computed_at` key must agree on its value. It compares only shared keys, so a member derived from the ceiling height alone is not held to be in conflict with one that also knew the opening width — a partial context is not a contradiction. The measured state: only `georgian-colonial-american` binds the family with computed parameters at all, and its two contexts are subset/superset (`ceiling_height_in: 108.0` in both), so the corpus passes today. The check is there for the kit that will not. Ontology stayed at 0.5.0 — nothing about this changed the slot data. Pinned by `tests/test_ontology.py`. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**The entablature is scattered across three slot groups** — `cornice` and `frieze` in Envelope, `crown` and `chair_rail` in Interior, `entablature` and `modillion_dentil` in Classical Apparatus. They are the same object at different scales and three of them derive from the same module. **Ruling: add `derives_from_module` cross-references between the six slots; do not regroup them.** The lighter-touch option — every existing id and downstream kit binding stays valid, and the proportion engine gains a cross-reference it can use to enforce that a chair rail's module actually derives from the same run as the exterior cornice, rather than being independently invented.

# oq/the-wall-at-the-corner-is-ruled-by-nothing — the pier at the end of a run, and the doorcase at the corner

*Status: OPEN · Raised in: WP-16.6, the pier and the alignment (1 October 2026), carrying the residual of `oq/the-placer-seats-windows-a-foot-apart-and-the-corpus-states-the-pier-three-ways`*

**R5 rules the wall between two windows. Nothing rules the wall between the last opening on a face
and the corner of the house.** The ruling of 29 Sep 2026 left it open in so many words: *"The corner
pier at a doorcase is not ruled and stays open here."* That question is closed by WP-16.6, so the
residual is carried here.

**Two cases, each measured on the heuristic placement of the shipped plans.**

1. **A doorcase at the corner.** On `spec-builder-colonial` the entrance doorcase stands
   **2.737 in** from the corner of the house (`elevation.doorcase_piers`, its left side, "no
   opening stands between the doorcase and the corner"). facade-classical's rule for the wall
   beside the doorcase speaks of the flanking WINDOWS, so the elevation measures that side and does
   not judge it. WP-16.6 did not move it.
2. **A window at the end of a run.** The pier fault's own secondary states it:
   `corner_return_pier_width_in / adjacent_opening_width_in` at-least 1.0, *"The pier at the end of
   a run, which the ganged exceptions do not licence."* Nothing supplies the measurement, so it is
   could-not-evaluate. Over the drawn elevations, **7 of 68** windows ending a run stand closer to
   the corner than their own width, down to 0.3 of it (good-05's upper E face, 18 in beside a 60 in
   window). That was 6 of 68 before WP-16.6. Seating from the centre out (R5a) moves the outer window
   toward the corner, and nothing stops it there.

**What is wanted.** Whether the wall at the corner has a floor, for a window and for a doorcase, and
whether it is the fault's 1.0 x the opening beside it. And whether the placer refuses a window that
cannot keep it, as R5 refuses one that cannot keep the wall to its neighbour.

*Taken as recommended under Lucas's standing instruction of 1 Oct 2026, never put: raised and left
open, carrying the ruling's own residual rather than answering it.*

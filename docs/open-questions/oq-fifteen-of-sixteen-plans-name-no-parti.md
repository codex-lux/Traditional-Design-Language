# oq/fifteen-of-sixteen-plans-name-no-parti — the facade layer is silent on 94% of this corpus

*Status: OPEN · Raised in: WP-11.7, building the derived facade (5 September 2026)*

**`facade.rhythm` derives a bay rhythm for exactly ONE of the sixteen plan records in this tree.**
The other fifteen name no `parti`, so their circulation diagram is unknown, so the rhythm is
`could-not-evaluate` and every finding below it is unjudged. Measured, not supposed:

| verdict | records |
|---|---|
| `derived` | 1 — `tidewater-georgian-careful` |
| `could-not-evaluate`, *"the plan record names no parti"* | **15** — every record in `plans/reference/`, both `good-` and `bad-`, **and `spec-builder-colonial`** |

**This is the sibling of `oq/fourteen-of-sixteen-plans-name-no-massing`, one field over, and the
two together are the same fact about this corpus**: the plan records were authored before the
layers that read these fields existed, so each new check arrives on a corpus that mostly declines
to state its own precondition. WP-11.2 authored `parti` on the Tidewater record and nothing has
authored it since; `spec-builder-colonial` — the OTHER shipped reference plan, the one both
engines are benchmarked on — is among the fifteen.

## Why this is not simply a data-entry backlog

**The refusal is correct and must not be traded away.** A record that names no parti has not said
what its organising move is, and `oq/the-facade-is-a-result-not-an-input` is explicit that the
facade follows that move: deriving a rhythm without one would be composing something plausible,
which is the trap that ruling names by name. So the fifteen are honestly unjudged, and the
question is what to do about a corpus where honest silence is the normal answer.

**And the obvious fix is the one the massing question already forbids.** Its entry says *"do not
close it by deriving the massing from the parti: that would make all three checks live on a fact
the author declined to state."* The mirror of that applies here — do not derive the parti from the
massing, the style, or the room list. A plan whose author did not say is a plan that did not say.

## What must be ruled

1. **Is `parti` authored on the fifteen, or is silence the permanent answer for a reference
   record?** The seven `good-` and seven `bad-` plans exist to exercise the CRITIC, and several
   are deliberately not any coherent diagram — `bad-06-open-concept-render` has no circulation
   parti in any real sense, and giving it one would be authoring a fiction to make a checker
   speak. `spec-builder-colonial` is the one that plainly should have one.
2. **Should a plan carry a `circulation_parti` DIRECTLY**, rather than only through the parti it
   names? A record can be a coherent house without being an instance of one of the twenty-one
   diagrams, and today there is no way for it to say "centre-door" without claiming to be a
   `centre-passage-double-pile`.
3. **What should the sheet say?** Today the plate draws a front composed from
   `facade-classical.json`'s formula on all fifteen and says nothing about the rhythm being
   underived, because the finding is `info`. A reader looking at the drawing cannot tell a facade
   that follows the plan from one that does not.

**Do not close this by lowering the finding's severity or by removing it.** An `info` on fifteen
records is already close to invisible; the count is the deliverable, and it is here so that the
next package cannot quote the facade layer's silence as agreement.

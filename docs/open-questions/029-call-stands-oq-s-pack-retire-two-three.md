**CONFIRMED 24 Aug 2026 — the call stands, and OQ 30's pack will retire two of the three. RESOLVED, with a recorded tension — 129 of 132 buildable nodes bound, not 132.** `PLAN-OF-ACTION.md`'s acceptance line for WP-4.1 says "132 of 132 buildable nodes bound," but the task text two sentences earlier says "where a needed pack does not exist... list it — do not bind the wrong pack." `egyptian-revival`, `moorish-andalusian`, and `mudejar` each have a real, checked reason no combination of the 36 packs applies (trabeated/battered-wall Egyptian construction with no arch; Islamic geometric setting-out with no order or module at all). Binding any of them to hit the literal count would have meant forcing a pack the corpus's own text rules out. Ruling made in the moment rather than escalated: honor the "do not bind the wrong pack" instruction over the numeric target, name the exception explicitly (`DELIBERATELY_UNBOUND` in `build/check_pack_bindings.py`, with the reasoning duplicated in `docs/reports/wp-4.1-proportion-pack-bindings.md`), and keep `--strict` wired into `check_all.py` so a fourth unbound node — a real regression — still fails the build. If a future package (WP-4.6) builds an Islamic/Moorish system and an Egyptian order, this drops to 0 automatically once those three are bound for real; until then it's a permanent, documented 3, not a punch-list item quietly carried forward.

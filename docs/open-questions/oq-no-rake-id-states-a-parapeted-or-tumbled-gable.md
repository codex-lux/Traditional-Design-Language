# oq/no-rake-id-states-a-parapeted-or-tumbled-gable — the town gable has no word in the rake vocabulary

*Status: OPEN · Raised in: WP-16.9, the roof, the rake and the deeper cornice (1 October 2026), on T6, taken as recommended under Lucas's standing instruction of 1 October 2026 and never put to him*

**No `rake_condition` id states a parapeted, stepped or spouted masonry gable, or a raking edge
finished with tumbled brick.** The ids in use are `boxed-rake-with-return` (The Cardboard Gable's
own rake), `bargeboard-full-rake-pierced`, `kneelers-and-raised-coping`, `timber-barge-board` and
`close-verge-with-rake-mould`.

**hudson-valley-dutch describes the town gable in its own words.** Its description reads: *"In the
towns the same culture builds in brick with the gable to the street, stepped or spouted, its raking
edge finished with mouse-tooth tumbling"*. B28's record for dutch-colonial-american, which reaches
hudson-valley-dutch, states the same town gable in its parent's words and leaves it to the rule.
So hudson-valley-dutch's words contradict nothing it inherits, and no record was written (T6).

**What is not ruled.** Whether the vocabulary gains ids for a coped or stepped parapet gable and a
tumbled raking edge, what each would draw, and which records would use them.

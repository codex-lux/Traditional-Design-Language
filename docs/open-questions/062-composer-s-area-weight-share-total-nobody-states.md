# OQ 62 — The composer's `area_weight` is a share of a total nobody states

*Status: CLOSED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: Ruled with the rest, 24 August 2026*

**CLOSED 24 Aug 2026 — a real share, with partial coverage allowed.** A room WITH an `area_weight` now takes that share of the brief's target, clamped to its own catalogue band, and is **frozen before the scaling loop runs** — a real share is not renegotiated by a global factor, and that is the whole of what changed. Rooms without a weight split whatever is left, exactly as before, so the **eleven of twenty-one partis that state no weights behave identically to yesterday**. A void's weight is a share of the same number and not of a gross the brief never states: the court is 0.18 of the house that was asked for, and that the house also has a court is what makes the block bigger than the brief (OQ 33) — sizing the court against the block would be circular.
**Measured, against each parti's own `area_range_sf` midpoint rather than one arbitrary brief:** eighteen of twenty-one land within 12% of target and most within 1%. Three do not, and each says something true. `courtyard-and-portal` misses by 41% at a 5,400 sf brief — **and missed by exactly 41% before this change too**, so it is not a regression: its seventeen rooms cannot be a 5,400 sf house at any weighting, because that diagram grows by having more rooms and the composer instantiates a fixed list. `great-hall-h-plan` went from −0.4% to −12.1%, which **is** the cost of the ruling: freezing weighted rooms at their bands leaves less for the unweighted ones to recover. `hall-and-parlor` moved 12.0% → 14.1%.
**A weight its own band cannot honour is now reported, not split quietly.** The composer logs a JUDGMENT naming every room, what its weight asked for and what its band gives — *"Sala wants 756 sf, band gives 480"* — so the area miss that follows is an explained number rather than a mysterious one, and it ends with what the reader needs to know: the brief is asking this diagram for a house it does not grow into by making its rooms bigger.
**The alternative reading, considered and not substituted:** treat the weights as RELATIVE shares and scale them as a group to hit the target, which would keep the ratios the author meant and recover the 11.7 points `great-hall-h-plan` lost. The ruling was the absolute reading — that share of that target — and implementing something else because it measured better would be answering a question that was not asked. Recorded here so the choice stays visible. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**The composer's `area_weight` is a share of a total nobody states.** Found while fixing the target-is-heated-area bug (OQ 33/39). `instantiate()` sizes each room from its catalogue default, nudges it 10% if the parti gives it an `area_weight` at all, and then scales everything by one factor to reach the brief's target — so a room's `area_weight` never actually sets its share of anything. It is read as a *boolean*: has a weight, or has not. The courtyard parti's weights (0.18 court, 0.07/0.07/0.04/0.04 corredor, 0.14 sala…) read as a considered distribution and are not one; they were tuned in this package against band capacities and the tuning had to be done by measuring the output, because nothing consumes the numbers. Either make the weights real (each room's target area is its weight times the brief's, clamped to its catalogue band) or stop writing them as fractions and say in the schema that the field means "this room is one the diagram sizes deliberately". The first is the honest reading of what a parti author thinks they are writing; it changes the sizing of every parti in the corpus and needs measuring before it is chosen.

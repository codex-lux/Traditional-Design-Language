# OQ 60 — The roof over a court is stated but not derived

*Status: CLOSED 24 AUG 2026 AS A STANDING DISCLOSURE · Raised in: Ruled with the rest, 24 August 2026*

**CLOSED 24 Aug 2026 as a standing disclosure — ruled: leave it stated.** Same standing as the wing/hyphen simplification this file already carries. The record says what it does not model, which is the discipline working rather than a gap in it; revisit when roof geometry is the subject rather than a dependency. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**The roof over a court is stated but not derived.** `build/roof.py` computes one ridge over one rectangle. Over an open court it does not: the real roof is ranges around the void, each with its own eave, meeting in valleys, and in the Spanish and Mediterranean cases falling inward to drain to the court. OQ 33 put the hole in the record with its own dimensions and labelled every height figure as computed for a roof that spans the whole block — which over the court it does not — so nothing is silently wrong. But `grade_to_ridge_ft` for a courtyard house is now a number that is explicitly not describing the building. The same is true, less visibly, of `elevation.py`'s silhouette. Deriving ranged roof geometry is a real package (valley lines, four eaves, the inward-pitch case) and it should be ruled whether it belongs to Phase 5 or is left standing as a disclosure the way the wing/hyphen simplification already is.

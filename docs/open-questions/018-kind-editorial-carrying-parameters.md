# OQ 18 — `kind: editorial` is carrying 166 of 402 parameters

*Status: HALF CLOSED 25 AUG 2026 · Raised in: From authoring the Georgian kit*

**HALF CLOSED 25 Aug 2026 — every editorial parameter now says it is one; the SOURCE half is environment-blocked.** The entry's complaint was two things and only one of them needed a library. All **162** parameters that were editorial with neither a source nor a note now carry a note: it states plainly that no source is recorded, quotes the basis the SLOT states for itself, and says which of four forms the figure takes. Nothing was given a source it does not have. The count of silent editorial values in this corpus is **0**.
**The audit before the writing is the finding, and it caught two errors of mine.** A first pass reported "57 mis-tagged placeholders" with `value: null` and I nearly rewrote them as unpopulated. They are nothing of the kind: **55 carry a `range` and 2 a `set`**, and my script had only looked at `value`. That is the **ninth** count in this work package to change once it was read, and the first that would have DAMAGED correct records rather than merely mis-sized a job. The audit was not in the ruling; doing it anyway is the only reason 57 good records survived.
**What the 162 actually are — RECLASSIFIED 25 Aug 2026 after an audit, because the first census was wrong in the direction that matters.** It classified a record as a figure whenever it had a `value` key, and called **82** of them "a bare single figure with no band … it reads as precise and is not". Seventy-two of those 82 are not measurements at all: `french-vernacular.wing_strategy.growth_unit` is `"bay-or-building"`, `georgian-colonial-american.frieze.treatment` is `"plain, or pulvinated (cushioned) in the Gibbsian manner on the richest work"`. No citation would sharpen those into numbers, and pointing a researcher with library access at them as the priority would have wasted the scarcest resource this entry has. The true shape: **23 a bare single figure** — the real weakest form, and the real place to start; **82 a categorical call**, which needs a source showing the tradition makes the choice, not a figure; **55 a band**, the honest form for a judgment since the width IS the uncertainty; **2 a set of permitted values**, an enumeration rather than a band (a source would extend the list, not narrow it), which the first pass also mislabelled.
**And the quoted basis was truncated at a hard 150 characters — mid-word in 148 of the 162.** "…this is the entablature's own m". The note presents that fragment as the reasoning the figure rests on, and several lost their operative clause. It now cuts at a sentence or clause boundary and marks every elision with an ellipsis; 17 are still elided and all 17 say so.
**And all 162 sit on a slot that already carries a rule or a note.** The reasoning was never missing — it was attached to the slot and not to the parameter, so a reader of the parameter saw a number with nothing behind it. That is what the notes fix.
**The source half is ENVIRONMENT-BLOCKED**, with OQ 7 through 11: closing it needs measured surveys and period manuals, `loc.gov`, `archive.org` and `hathitrust` all fail to connect from here, and **none of the 162 may be given a source from a secondary work or a modern redrawing.** The 23 bare figures are where a person with library access should start — NOT the 82, which the reclassification above shows are categorical calls rather than measurements.

**Where it actually stands: 1,556 parameters — measured 1,161 (74.6%), derived 161 (10.3%), editorial 199 (12.8%), invented 25 (1.6%), code 10 (0.6%).** The number that matters is not `editorial`: an editorial call carrying a note saying what it rests on is the rule working as designed, since 'sources or `kind: editorial`' makes the second half a legitimate answer. What cannot be defended is **editorial with neither a source nor a note — 164, 10.5% of all parameters**. Nothing is unlabelled.
**156 of those 164 are on one kit,** `georgian-colonial-american`, spread across 67 slots with no slot holding more than 7. That is the deepest-authored kit in the corpus carrying the cost of having been authored first, not a systemic problem across 159 kits — which is a materially different work list from the one this entry has implied since it was written.
**Two things were fixed rather than described.** (i) Every unsourced editorial number was matched against every numeric threshold its own style states, on the constraint's *expression name* as well as its value. **Exactly one matched**: `water_table.measured_band_in` [24, 36] is `georgian-colonial-american.c05`'s own water-table band, so it was a sourced fact wearing the label for an unsourced one, and it now cites it as `measured`. One hit out of 166 is itself the finding: these numbers are genuinely unsourced, not sourced-and-uncited, and no amount of cross-referencing inside the corpus will retire them. (ii) **Eleven were mislabelled in kind.** `garage_strategy`'s own ontology note says in its first sentence that no historical precedent exists, and the schema distinguishes `editorial` ('our considered convention') from `invented` ('no precedent exists'). A number about the garage door itself cannot be a convention drawn from tradition, because the tradition has no garage. Reclassified to `invented`; the ones left editorial on that slot are the ones carried from the dependency tradition — subordination, ridge ratio, pitch match, cladding continuity.
**What remains cannot be done from inside this repository, and that is the point.** The 164 want a real second pass against real sources, one number at a time; anything else is laundering a guess as a measurement, which this project's own rules call the worst thing that can be done to the corpus. The honest interim position is the one now in force: every one of them is labelled as our own convention, the count is computed, and it is 10.5% and falling only when somebody sources something.

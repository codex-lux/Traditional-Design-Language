# OQ 19 — `determined_by` is declarative only

*Status: CLOSED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: From authoring the Georgian kit*

**CLOSED 24 Aug 2026 — three enforceable meanings, and they found four things.** The field said what decides a slot and nothing resolved it, because the schema never said what the determination MEANS. It now means three things `build/check_kits.py::check_determined_by` holds the corpus to. **(1) The determiners must be bound.** A slot that says it is whatever the order requires, on a kit whose `order` is empty, is unjudged — and it read as specified, which is this corpus's first discipline inverted. One real case: `scandinavian-log-vernacular.roof_material` is determined_by `roof_pitch`, which is empty, so the choice between sod-on-birch-bark and split-shingle is decided by nothing — and is not a free choice either, since sod needs a shallow pitch to stay on and a shingle a steep one to shed. Now stated on the slot; the warning is left standing because it is true. **(2) No cycles.** None found. **(3) A determined slot may not state a dimensional number as an independent claim** — the schema's own note has always said so ('specifying it separately either restates the order or contradicts it'), so a number must be `derived`, or carry a source or expression, or say in its own note why it is genuinely independent. 17 numbers failed that. Working through them found two data defects rather than missing notes. **`reveal_masonry` and `reveal_frame` on the Georgian kit were byte-identical:** OQ 12's split divided the slot in two and copied the whole parameter set into both halves rather than dividing it, leaving each carrying the other trade's numbers with the per-parameter `applies_when.construction` doing the discrimination the split was supposed to do. **Tidewater's `reveal_frame` was a verbatim copy of its `reveal_masonry`** and therefore stated the MASONRY answer under the frame id — a 13.5 in wall and a 4-8 in reveal — so a frame Tidewater house would have been drawn with a brick wall's reveal. It now extends the parent and states nothing of its own, because nothing in the corpus sources a Tidewater departure and inventing one is what the copy already did once. Also **recorded and deliberately not resolved:** `construction_type.wythe_step_at_floor` reads 13.5 in, which is that slot's own masonry wall thickness repeated, while `reveal_masonry.step_at_floor_in` reads 4.5 in and the rule sentence says a half brick. Correcting a number on a guess is worse than recording that two slots disagree; both now say so. The remaining eleven are notes: which of restatement and independence each number is, and why. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**`determined_by` is declarative only.** `porch_ceiling` says it is determined by the order and the entablature, which is better than prose, but nothing resolves it because the schema does not say how the determination works.

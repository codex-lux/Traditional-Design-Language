# OQ 59 — Five of twenty-one partis carry fatal findings against their own native style

*Status: CLOSED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: Ruled with the rest, 24 August 2026*

**CLOSED 24 Aug 2026 — all five repaired, the check that would have caught them added, and two of the five were not the partis' fault.** `build/check_partis.py` gains check 10: every parti is instantiated against the first style in its own `styles` array and run through `plan_check`, and a fatal is an error. It runs in about six seconds for the whole catalogue and now reports **21 of 21 composable**. Everything else in that file checks a parti is well FORMED; this checks the diagram WORKS.
**The check is differential, and had to be.** Three of `cape-central-chimney`'s four original fatals fire identically for *every* parti composed for `cape-cod-colonial` — they are facts about that style's kit and elevation. A check that blamed the parti for them would generate false accusations against exactly the files it exists to protect, so each parti is compared against a control diagram run on the same style, keyed on the requirement rather than the measured value.
**What was actually the partis':** `charleston-single-piazza` had no door between its two principal ground rooms (a single house is one room wide with the stair hall on the piazza side, so they share the wall between them and are connected through it); `creole-gallery`'s repeating `chamber` generated `chamber1`/`chamber2`, the ids of two chambers it already named, so every Creole brief for more than two bedrooms built a plan with duplicate room ids; `ranch-tripartite`'s family room reached the kitchen only across the eat-in area, and the validator counts a hop only through CIRCULATION; `five-part-palladian` typed both hyphens as `back-hall`, whose own hard rule is must_adjoin kitchen by direct door — so both wings demanded a kitchen at their own end and only one wing has one. That last is an unsatisfiable requirement produced by naming a room wrongly rather than by drawing it wrongly: a hyphen is a connecting gallery, and it is now `gallery-corridor`, with the service rooms regrouped into the one dependency that has the kitchen.
**Three validator facts had to change with them, each a rule asking for something a correct plan structurally cannot have.** `landing` → `stair-hall` is now `vertical_ok` (a landing is the top of a stair and the stair hall is a storey BELOW it, so read as a same-floor rule only a house with a second stair hall upstairs could satisfy it — OQ 35 built the mechanism, nothing had used it). `butlers-pantry` → `kitchen` gains `via: [back-hall, gallery-corridor]` (where the kitchen is in a dependency the pantry is in the block and the kitchen is in another building; without it the rule asked the pantry to be in two buildings at once — the same principle the room already embodies at the other end of its own run). And `centre-passage` joins the `entrance-hall` EQUIVALENT group: the front door opens into it and every principal room opens off it, which is the definition that group holds. That last removed 2 and 3 spurious *minor* findings from the two shipped plans with fatal and serious unmoved, which is what says it removed noise and not signal.
**Two of the five were the corpus's own first discipline inverted — unjudged reported as evaluated-and-failed**, and those are the more dangerous kind. `roof.py` models gable-end stacks only and every branch that returns an empty position list says in its own note that it did NOT model the case; `elevation.py` reported that as `visible_chimney_count: 0`, which handed the fault corpus an evaluated measurement where it had none, and `faults/chimney-omitted.json` duly fired **fatal on a parti named cape-central-chimney for having no chimney**. It is None now, and `plan_check` drops a None measurement rather than letting the evaluator throw on it. Separately, a fault finding quoted `results[0]` — a fault with secondary tests can have its primary PASS and a secondary fail, and the report then printed the passing number as the evidence: *"The House With No Fire: 2 against at-least 1"* on a Cape with two chimneys, a sentence in which every number is right and the claim is nonsense. `core.check_measurements` now returns `failing` beside `results` and the finding quotes the test that failed.
**Outcome:** a Charleston brief returns the Charleston single, a Creole brief the Creole gallery, a Cape brief the central-chimney Cape. A `ranch-style` brief still returns `bungalow-open-linear`: the ranch parti carries 15 serious findings against the bungalow's 3 and no fatal is involved, so that is the composer's weighting working as designed on a diagram that is genuinely rougher, not a defect of this kind.

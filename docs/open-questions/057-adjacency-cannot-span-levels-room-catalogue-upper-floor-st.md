# OQ 57 — adjacency cannot span levels, and no room in the catalogue is an upper-floor state room

*Status: RULED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: From partis to full coverage (WP-4.5, 24 Aug 2026)*

**RULED 24 Aug 2026 — let adjacency rules declare a vertical relation.** Add `relation: "above"` / `"below"` to adjacency rules and teach `plan_check.py` to evaluate them across levels. `schema/parti.schema.json`'s own `adjacencies` array already anticipates exactly this and `five-part-palladian` already uses it for its stair. Both of WP-4.5's workarounds come out: `rooms/overlook.json`'s hall rule goes back to `strong`, and the Tudor great chamber stops being typed `library` to dodge a ground-floor rule it can never satisfy. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**OPEN — adjacency cannot span levels, and no room in the catalogue is an upper-floor state room.** Two findings from WP-4.5 with one cause. First: `rooms/overlook.json`'s defining relationship is that it is open to the hall on the storey *below* it, and `plan_check.py` evaluates adjacency within a level, so a `should_adjoin` written for that relationship can never pass on a correct plan — it reported `serious` on the one arrangement the room exists for, and was held at `preferred` rather than `strong` to stop a right answer being called a defect. Second: all thirteen public and living rooms in the catalogue carry a ground-floor adjacency rule — `drawing-room` must reach a dining room, `parlor`/`best-parlor`/`morning-room` an entrance-hall, `sitting-room` a parlor, `hall` a cross-passage — so the great chamber of a Tudor H-plan, which is the best room in the house and sits over the parlour, can satisfy none of them; typed `drawing-room`, which is what it most nearly is, the plan trips a **fatal** for not reaching a dining room a storey beneath it. It is currently typed `library`, whose rule is `strong` rather than `hard`, so the same impossibility reports as serious and the diagram stays composable — a workaround, and recorded as one. Both would be answered by the same change: letting an adjacency rule declare that it is satisfied vertically (`relation: "above"` / `"below"`, which `schema/parti.schema.json`'s own `adjacencies` array already anticipates and `five-part-palladian` already uses for its stair) and teaching `plan_check.py` to read it. Whether the room catalogue should ALSO gain an upper-floor reception room is a separate and smaller question that this one probably subsumes.

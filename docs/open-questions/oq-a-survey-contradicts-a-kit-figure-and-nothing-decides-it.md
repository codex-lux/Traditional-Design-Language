# oq/a-survey-contradicts-a-kit-figure-and-nothing-decides-it — 17 `measured` figures a survey disproves, three by the node's own exemplar

*Status: OPEN · Raised in: WP-11.5, after Ruling A (5 Sep 2026)*

**OPEN — and this is the precedent bench's main product, sitting inert.** Two tranches read 155
surveys against the kits and returned **240 verdicts: 196 silent, 27 agree, 17 CONTRADICT.**
`oq/a-surveyors-prose-may-source-an-envelope-figure` was ruled on 5 Sep and settled what a survey may
SOURCE. It said nothing about what a survey that DISPROVES a figure obliges, so all seventeen are
recorded and none is acted on. Nothing in the corpus decides them.

**Ruled 5 Sep 2026 by Lucas: name all seventeen here and decide none of them yet.** The reason is
that they are not one kind of thing, and a single rule would force one remedy onto three problems:

- **A band that is simply wrong.** `southern-federal.depth_and_pile.max_depth_ft` is 20 ft and
  Woodlawn's central block is *"56' x 45'"* — forty-five feet of depth against a maximum of twenty,
  on a building the node itself names.
- **A parameter that PRESUPPOSES something its exemplar does not have.**
  `southern-federal.chimney.standoff_min_in` is 4 in and Belle Grove has *"four inside chimneys"*. An
  interior stack has no standoff from a gable wall at all, so the parameter cannot be SATISFIED
  rather than merely being exceeded. Widening it would be meaningless; it is the wrong question for
  that house.
- **A band with no stated DATUM, where the disagreement may be an artefact.**
  `appalachian-log-house` is contradicted on both ends of one slot by ONE survey — an interior
  15'2" below a 16 ft floor and an interior 19'9½" above an 18 ft ceiling — and HABS TN-161 says
  INTERIOR twice while the slot does not say whether its band is interior or exterior. The gap could
  be wall thickness. A slot that does not state its own datum cannot be convicted.

**The three options, with the cost that argues against each, recorded so the ruling is not re-derived
from scratch.** (1) *A contradiction obliges a widened band, cited* — direct, and it makes the bench
immediately change what the machine does; but a band widened to fit one building carries one
building's authority, which is what `oq/the-proportion-band-forbids-the-square` had to unpick from
the other direction. (2) *A contradiction obliges a FAULT, not a band change* — the corpus
disagreeing with itself, stated rather than reconciled, which is how
`oq/a-grouping-rule-and-a-room-record-can-disagree` was handled; it needs a checker and makes the
count of known self-contradictions a permanent published number. (3) *Case by case, by a reader* —
honest at seventeen and not at seventy.

**The trap, stated in advance.** Do not reconcile any of these by picking the stricter or the looser
number to make a checker green. In several the source is on one side only and it is not consistently
the same side; in at least one — `ranch-style`, twice — **there is no survey at all** and the
contradiction rests on secondary sources, which is a weaker claim than the others and is marked as
such below.

**And a disclosure that belongs with the list.** Several of these exemplars were ADDED to their node
by Tranche 2, so the kit's band was written without that building in view. That does not make the
contradiction unreal — it is still two records in this tree disagreeing — but it means "the node's
own exemplar disproves the node" is a sharper sentence for a pre-existing exemplar than for a new
one, and the difference is not tracked.

## The seventeen

1. **`gothic-revival-american`** — `roof_pitch.pitch_min 12 / pitch_max 18 rise_in_12 (both kind: measured)`
   *HABS GA-222, Green-Meldrim House, Savannah: 'EXTERIOR FEATURES OF NOTE Flat roof with crenallated
   parapet, four end chimneys flush with exterior walls, cast iron entrance porch, cast iron
   verandas...' A flat roof is not inside a 12:12 to 18:12 band and cannot be brought inside it.
   Disclosure: this exemplar is one I added to the node, so the kit did not have this building in view
   when the band was written; the contradiction is nonetheless real and is between two records in the
   tree.*

2. **`dutch-colonial-american`** — `chimney (same slot; the forbidden variants are the point)`
   *John Ackerman House, HABS NJ-298 Page 1: "Inside brick chimneys". And, from the Steuben Estate
   Complex National Register nomination, of the Campbell-Christie House: "The front parlours have large
   fireplaces connected to separate internal stack."*
   Reading: contradicts, weakly and reportedly. Both phrases describe interior stacks in Bergen gambrel
   houses; neither says whether the stack sits inside a GABLE wall (canonical) or inside the house
   flanking the ridge (forbidden). Recorded on both precedent records and not resolved — the words do
   not decide it, and no kit was edited

3. **`stick-style`** — `roof_pitch.pitch_min = 9:12 and roof_pitch.pitch_max = 14:12 (both kind: measured, binding specified)`
   *HABS NJ-746 (Emlen Physick House, Architectural Data Form): '60' x 52 1 with 22' x 35' rear wing;
   2-1/2 stories; gable, mansard and hip roofs; hip-roofed porch across portions of northwest front and
   northeast side'.*

4. **`queen-anne-american`** — `belt_course.position_pct_wall_height = 60 to 75 percent (kind: measured), and its rule, which presupposes ONE texture change`
   *HABS CA-1160 (Haas-Lilienthal House): 'The first story (main floor) is covered with horizontal wood
   siding about eight inches wide; the second story is covered with shingling, but with strong
   horizontal divisions about four inches apart. The third story is covered with scale-like shingles,
   which extend up into the multiple gables of the roof.' Two changes on a three-storey wall, the first
   at the first-floor line -- roughly a third of the way up, not 60 to 75 percent. No height is printed,
   so the percentage itself cannot be computed.*

5. **`neoclassical-revival`** — `porch_depth.depth_typical_range_ft, range [10, 14] ft, kind measured`
   *HABS FL-224 (Whitehall), Part II.B.5.a: 'a two-story portico measuring 101'-6" across the front and
   18-0" deep'*

6. **`french-eclectic`** — `roof_pitch.pitch_constancy, value 'constant within 1:12 across all faces', kind measured`
   *HABS DE-283 (Nemours): 'a three-story main block ... noted by its hipped roof with dormers ... and
   the pair of two-story, mansard-roofed pavilions that flank it', plus a one-storey sun-room wing with
   a roof balustrade*

7. **`french-eclectic`** — `roof_pitch.eave_to_wall_ratio, range [0.6, 1.0], with the note 'So the roof reads as 40-50 percent of the elevation'`
   *HABS RI-344 (The Elms): 'An eight foot high blind balus- trade conceals the third story, the
   servants quarters ... Slate shingles cover the inside wall of the balustrade ... Copper sheets are
   used as roofing of this story.' The roof reads as none of the elevation*

8. **`jacobethan-revival`** — `chimney.cluster_shaft_count (range 2-4, measured, 'Hard constraint c05')`
   *HABS OH-50, Stan Hywet Hall, Part II.B.3 -- 'The numerous chimneys are designed in a variety of
   ways, sometimes clustered and sometimes standing individually.' A stack standing individually is a
   one-shaft stack, which falls outside the range [2,4] on a building this node names as its own
   exemplar. Stated as a contradiction rather than a silence because reporting it smaller than it is
   would be the wrong direction; the qualification, which belongs with it, is that the parameter may be
   scoped to clusters only, in which case a single stack is out of the rule's scope rather than in
   breach of it -- and the kit does not say which.*

9. **`southern-federal`** — `depth_and_pile.max_depth_ft 20 (measured)`
   *Woodlawn (HABS VA-337): '5-part plan with: central block - 56' x 45'; 2 stories'. Forty-five feet of
   depth against a kit maximum of twenty, on the node's OWN exemplar -- and the node's second diagnostic
   tell is 'One-room-deep main block with a through-passage, where a Northern Federal house of the same
   status would be double-pile'. The kit is not edited here.*

10. **`southern-federal`** — `chimney.standoff_min_in 4 (measured)`
   *Belle Grove (HABS VA-259): 'four inside chimneys'. An interior stack has no standoff from a gable
   wall at all, so the parameter cannot be satisfied rather than merely being out of band. The node's
   FIRST diagnostic tell is 'Exterior-end chimney stacks standing away from the gable wall'.*

11. **`italianate-villa`** — `roof_pitch.pitch_min = 3 rise_in_12 (and pitch_max 5), inherited through `extends``
   *HABS AL-765, Kenworthy Hall: "From the finished first floor to the slight peak of the flat roof the
   height of the building is 37'." A flat roof with a slight peak is below a 3-in-12 floor. The only
   numeric roof statement in the cluster, and it falls outside the band.*

12. **`prairie-school`** — `roof_pitch (specified: hipped, 3:12-4:12; hard constraint c01)`
   *HABS IL-1099 Frank Lloyd Wright Home and Studio: 'The house roof is a steep gable covered with black
   composition shingles. ... The studio has several levels of flat roofs.'*
   Reading: contradicts -- a steep gable and several flat roofs on a building the node now names as an
   exemplar; recorded, and the exemplar's standing is documentary for exactly this reason

13. **`arts-and-crafts-american`** — `chimney (specified: canonical exterior-masonry-mass; rule 'rough, unpainted masonry -- fieldstone, river rock, or clinker brick -- ... the heaviest, most textured object in the composition'; thin-multiplied-accent FORBIDDEN)`
   *HABS CA-1981 Gamble House: '5. Chimneys: Three - brick with cast concrete caps with gunite finish.'*
   Reading: contradicts -- three stacks against a singular rule whose own slot note cites this very
   building as building 'the chimney' as 'the single heaviest object on the elevation', and cast
   concrete caps with a gunite finish are outside the rule's own material list

14. **`ranch-style`** — `roof_pitch.eave_overhang_range_in = 18-36 in (measured, source ranch-style.c03)`
   *NO HABS SURVEY EXISTS FOR ANY BUILDING IN THIS CLUSTER. Save Our Heritage Organisation on the
   O'Leary House of 1932, the building this node dates the type from: 'A traditional Spanish Colonial-
   style cornice caps the walls and conceals the rafter tails.'*

15. **`ranch-style`** — `depth_and_pile.overall_length_range_ft = 50-80 ft (measured, source ranch-style.typical_ratios)`
   *No survey. Three secondary sources give the 1949 Levitt ranch model as 32 by 25 feet (Keri Murray
   Architecture; thehistoryoflevittown.weebly.com; Digital History's '25-by-32-foot house'), and
   levittownbeyond.com says that model is 11,447 of Levittown's 17,447 houses. A 32 ft body is 18 ft
   short of the band's floor.*

16. **`appalachian-log-house`** — `depth_and_pile.pen_width_ft, range [16, 20] ft, kind measured`
   *HABS TN-161 Henry Whitehead Place: "Old cabin 15'2\" x 14 ,9\" (int. dimensions)"*
   Reading: contradicts -- 15.17 ft is below the 16 ft floor. Caveat that matters: the survey says
   INTERIOR twice and the kit slot does not say whether its band is interior or exterior, so the gap

17. **`appalachian-log-house`** — `depth_and_pile.pen_depth_ft, range [14, 18] ft, kind measured`
   *HABS TN-161: "New cabin 17-1/4\" x 19' 9-1/2\" (int. dimensions)"*
   Reading: contradicts -- 19.79 ft is above the 18 ft ceiling, again interior. The companion width

## Tranche 3 added three more (5 Sep 2026), and one of them is a band two exemplars break

18. **`cotswold-vernacular`** — `window_lite_pattern.light_count`, range [2, 5], `measured`.
    *Owlpen Manor, Historic England: 6 lights in the central gable's ground floor. Grevel's House,
    a different building surveyed by a different hand: a two-storey Perpendicular bay 'containing 6
    lights with tracery', beside '4- and 6-light mullion windows (with king mullions)'.*
    **Two of the node's seven exemplars exceed the ceiling of 5, independently.** Three other
    exemplars sit inside the band, so citing those while these two contradict would be
    cherry-picking, and all five candidates on this parameter were refused for that reason.
    A band its own buildings break twice is the clearest case in the list for the band being wrong.

19. **`german-fachwerk`** — `height_proportion.jetty_projection`, range [200, 400] mm, `measured`.
    *Rathaus Wernigerode: 'etwa 50 Zentimeter'.* 500 against a 400 ceiling — and it SATISFIES the
    bracket condition the node's own constraint c03 attaches to exceeding it, so the kit half expects
    this. **It cannot even be recorded as a measurement**, because the `measurements[].unit` enum has
    no millimetre while the kit's parameter unit does; the record carries the figure with no parsed
    `value`. A contradiction the mechanism cannot see, for a reason that is neither party's fault.

20. **`english-georgian`** — `composition_parti.bay_count`, a SET of [3, 5, 7], `measured`.
    *Royal Crescent, Bath: 'Nos 14-17, to centre, have four-window fronts.' Kedleston Hall: 'Centre
    block of eleven bays.'* Four and eleven are not in the set, on two buildings the node now names.
    This is not a band that is too narrow but an ENUMERATION that is incomplete, which is a fourth
    kind of contradiction the three above do not cover — and `kit_source_agrees` cannot see it at
    all, because it compares a `range` or a scalar and a `set` is neither
    (`oq/a-parameter-has-one-source-field-so-a-building-cannot-corroborate-a-reasoned-figure`).

**That the count went 17 -> 20 by researching one more continent is the thing to carry forward.**
Nobody knows the true number; what is known is that it rises with every tranche, and that the four
kinds now visible — a wrong band, a presupposition the exemplar does not meet, a band with no stated
datum, and an incomplete enumeration — each want a different remedy.

## What is NOT here

The 27 AGREEMENTS are not a mirror of this list: most agree in KIND and are silent on the NUMBER, and
six of them became citations under Ruling A (`docs/precedents.md`). And the **196 silences** are the
larger finding — an archival survey states materials, dates and condition and rarely a pitch, a
ceiling or a porch depth — which is why more of the same series will not, by itself, dimension the
generator. Those belong to `oq/a-measured-parameter-with-no-source-is-not-metered`, not here.

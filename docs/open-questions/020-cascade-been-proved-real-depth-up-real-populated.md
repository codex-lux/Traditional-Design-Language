*Status: ANSWERED WITH CORPUS WIDE DATA · Raised in: From authoring the Georgian kit*

**ANSWERED WITH CORPUS-WIDE DATA (24 Aug 2026, WP-4.2, wave B) — the cascade has now been proved at real depth (up to 34 real, populated levels on a spot-checked variant) across all 129 previously-empty style/variant kits, with a real count of merge problems.** Originally: not a decision so much as a test the kit fill must run; WP-4.2 was written to surface it. **23 Aug 2026 (WP-4.2, wave A)** built the mechanism this test depends on — family nodes had no kit files and no `lineage` edges, so could never appear in `_cascade` before this — and got one real four-level chain with zero merge problems found (`tidewater-georgian → georgian-colonial-american → american-colonial → english-georgian`), but explicitly left the corpus-wide count open until wave B ran. **24 Aug 2026 (WP-4.2, wave B):** all 129 remaining style/variant kits now carry real content (159 of 159 kit files corpus-wide have at least one non-`open` slot; 1,556 `specified` + 330 `extends` + 223 `forbidden` bindings total, up from 256/18/75 before wave B). A spot-check on `appalachian-log-house` (a variant, the deepest kind of cascade) shows a 34-node chain in which every single ancestor now resolves real content — the acceptance criterion PLAN-OF-ACTION.md names directly ("`resolve_kit` shows multi-level provenance on every variant"). The merge-problem count: **~26 real problems found and fixed across 129 nodes (roughly one in five)**, all traceable to three distinct causes, none a mechanism failure: (1) deep classical content (`antique-classical`'s strict-bilateral symmetry, `renaissance-classical`'s harmonic-mean ceiling rule) leaking into 15 non-classical nodes across five families via a real but narrow lineage edge that, once populated, carries the donor's entire binding rather than just the trait the edge exists for; (2) `hybridizes_with` edges built to carry one narrow aspect of a donor's practice instead transmitting that donor's whole kit (9+ nodes in the `continental-baroque-neoclassical`/`british-picturesque`/`low-countries-vernacular` cluster alone, plus `north-german-hall-house` and `swiss-chalet`) — a real, general mechanism limitation, not fixed at the mechanism level in this pass, only patched node-by-node; (3) one genuine cross-batch collision (`french-eclectic`'s `garage_strategy`, authored against `tudor-revival` as the nearest ancestor in sub-wave 1, broken when sub-wave 2 concurrently gave a nearer ancestor its own binding) — the specific risk of authoring `extends` deltas in parallel across a shared cascade, caught by `check_kits.py`'s dangling-diff check and fixed by converting to a full override. Full detail, per-node list, and the case for a future slot-scope allowlist on `hybridizes_with`: `docs/reports/wp-4.2-wave-b-kit-fill.md`.

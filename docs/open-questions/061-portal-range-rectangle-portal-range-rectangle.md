# OQ 61 — A portal range is one rectangle, and a portal range is not one rectangle

*Status: CLOSED 24 AUG 2026 · Raised in: Ruled with the rest, 24 August 2026*

**CLOSED 24 Aug 2026 — four loggia records, as ruled.** `partis/courtyard-and-portal.json`'s walk is now `walk-s`, `walk-w`, `walk-e`, `walk-n`, doored to each other so the ring is continuous, to the court, and to the rooms each range serves. What is lost is the true statement that it is ONE room; what is gained is a diagram whose circulation reaches its own rooms. `courtyard_slice()` reads the assignment rather than guessing it: the i-th walk takes the i-th band (the parti lists them in the engine's own band order, S W E N, so the range holding the entry passage is the one on the street) and every other room goes to the band of the walk it doors to, a room with no walk door following the room it does open off. **Two things had to change with it.** The ranges are not equal — a full courtyard's street and rear ranges run the block's whole width while the side ranges run only the court's depth, so they hold about twice the area — and four rooms in a side range made that corredor swell to 385 sf to fill a band its rooms could not; the rooms are now distributed against the band areas. And the four weights sum to 0.22, not the single record's 0.12: a corredor that actually rings the court is its perimeter times its depth, about 4·√(0.18·G)·7 for a house of gross area G, which is 0.21–0.22 across this parti's whole area range. The old figure was the weight of a walk along ONE side, and understating it does not make the corredor smaller — the ring geometry gives it the band area its rooms do not fill either way — it makes every room in the ranges smaller. *Original entry follows.*<br><br>**A portal range is one rectangle, and a portal range is not one rectangle.** `partis/courtyard-and-portal.json` gives the covered walk a single room record (`walk`, a `loggia`), and the parti's own note says that walk IS the house's entire circulation: "every room is entered from it and from nothing else." A continuous roofed edge around four sides of a court cannot be one rectangle, so the placement engines put it along one side and the other three ranges are entered from nothing. The plan validator does not catch it because the door graph is satisfied by the record, not by the geometry — `plan_check.py` never reads `room.geometry` (the same blind spot OQ 32 named). Three ways out and none of them is obviously right: give the parti four loggia records and lose the fact that it is one room; let a room carry more than one rectangle, which is a schema change with reach into every consumer; or accept it and say so on the drawing. Raised rather than decided, because the first option changes what a room IS in this corpus.

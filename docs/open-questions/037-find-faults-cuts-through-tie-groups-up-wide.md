# OQ 37 — `find_faults` cuts through tie groups up to 104 wide

*Status: OPEN · Raised in: From the deployment audit (25 Aug 2026)*

**OPEN — `find_faults` cuts through tie groups up to 104 wide.** `mcp_server/core.py`'s sort key is `(severity_index, frequency_index)`: two coarse enums over 209 faults, so tie groups are enormous, and `out[:limit]` with a default of 25 then keeps an arbitrary slice of them. Measured: for `queen-anne-american` the cut lands inside a tie group of 104 and drops 103 faults the function's own ranking calls exactly as important as the one it kept. It is now *deterministic* — the glob sweep saw to that — but deterministic is not the same as principled, and the caller is told `matches` without being told the cut was arbitrary. The fix `pick_partis` took (expand through a narrow tie, cut a wide one) does not transfer: a 104-wide tie means the ranking has no opinion at all, and the honest response is either a finer key (frequency and severity are not the only things that distinguish faults) or saying in the response that the remainder is unranked rather than unimportant. Both are product decisions about what a search result means, so neither was taken unilaterally.

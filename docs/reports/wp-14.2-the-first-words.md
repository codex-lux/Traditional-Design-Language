## II. Two batches were authored twice, and the choice between them is stated

Two agents were started on Batch B in one worktree, and two on Batch D in another. **This was my
dispatch error, not theirs**: I sent a message to a workflow-spawned agent, which resumed a second
copy of it in the same tree. Each pair wrote over the other's files in the shared working tree. Both
second authors saw it, did not touch the first author's branch, and committed beside it:
`wp-14.2-b-alt` as a child of the first B commit, `wp-14.2-d-alt` (`8e87734`) from the same base as
the first D.

- **Batch B: the second authoring is taken.** It binds, orders, senses and pairs exactly as the first,
  and it is better evidenced: each worked example quotes its kit **under a key path**
  (`kits/georgian-colonial-american.kit.json roof_form.variants[cross-gable].note` and twelve more),
  so the checker verifies the quote under that key and not merely somewhere in the file. It also
  declares three pairs inside the batch that the corpus itself treats as easily confused
  (descends-from / references, member-of / descends-from, editorial / invented).
- **Batch D: the first authoring is taken.** The two carry the same 27 ids with the same terms,
  kinds, orders and senses and no record byte-equal. Both pass the checker. The first was committed
  first, was already checked against WP-14.1's in-progress checker over the seeds, and states the
  cascade exactly the way `docs/inheritance.md`'s table and the data do. `wp-14.2-d-alt` is left
  unmerged and intact as the fallback, record by record, should a later reading prefer one of its
  definitions.

**The lesson for the next dispatch: never message a workflow-spawned agent.** It resumes a
duplicate rather than steering the one you have, and the duplicate writes into the same tree.

## III. What the records found that the docs do not say

Each finding below was made by an author reading the corpus to write a definition, and each is
recorded here rather than fixed, because the file it names is outside this package.

1. **`member_of` is not inert, and three surfaces say it is.** Since WP-4.2 `build/build.py` walks
   `member_of` to put each style's family into its kit cascade, so a family's kit DOES reach the
   styles filed under it. Measured by Batch B on Prairie School: its own kit leaves the classical
   apparatus `open`, and all eight slots resolve `forbidden` from `american-arts-and-crafts`, its
   family. `docs/model.md` says `member_of` "does not carry inheritance", `README.md` says it is "for
   browsing and nothing else", and **`EdgeGlyph` prints "browsing only — carries no inheritance"
   under every `member_of` edge**. The records `member-of` and `rank-family` say what the builder
   does. The EdgeGlyph sentence is WP-14.11's to remove; the two docs are named for it too.
2. **`docs/inheritance.md` misdescribes `extends`**, saying it "stops the cascade like `specified`",
   where `build/resolve_kit.py` walks on past it to find the base it merges into. The same table
   lists an `inherited` binding the schema enum does not have, and the file opens "95 slots in 8
   groups" against an ontology of 97. `binding-extends` follows the schema and the resolver.
3. **`docs/model.md`'s prose has Colonial Revival "reference" Georgian building; the data edge is
   `revives`.** The edge records take their examples from the edges as the data states them.
4. **`schema/brief.schema.json` promises "The composer will not drop these." of must-have rooms**,
   and the composer returns candidates without them and says so — the register's
   `oq/the-composer-returns-a-set-that-satisfies-neither-must-have-room`. The Brief record quotes
   `docs/compose.md`'s "reported, not satisfied" instead and does not repeat the schema's promise.
5. **`build/typefacts.py`'s `FACT_TITLES` still titles the bearing fact "bearing continuity and span
   capacity"**, and a refusal prints that title at the head of its line, although since the 16 Sep
   ruling only continuity refuses. `judgment-refused` names bearing continuity alone, from the code.
6. **No citation kind lands on the Family tree with a style, or on Drawings, Export or Trace a
   drawing at all**, and `room:`, `grouping:` and `massing:` land on a searched banner. Those
   surfaces' `try` links are therefore a style, a fault or a `term:` cite, which resolve once
   WP-14.3 serves the glossary.

## IV. What the contract got wrong, left frozen

The ids, terms and families were frozen so four authors could work at once, and the authors used
the frozen strings verbatim where the contract fixed them. Four places where the contract is too
narrow, each softened in the definition rather than contradicted:

- **`rank-variant`'s sense, "the finest rank, where a kit is buildable", reads as exclusive**, and it
  is not: buildable nodes include styles, and families carry kits too. The definitions say
  "specific enough to build".
- **`exception-refused`'s sense, "an exception whose condition this style does not meet", is
  narrower than the verdict**: `core.grant_exception` also refuses on the house's date outside a
  licence's range. The definition says both.
- **`pack-kind-order-system` is glossed "classical order", and the kind also holds `moorish-arch`**,
  whose own notes say it is filed there for want of an arch-system kind. The definition says so.
- **`pack-kind-module-system` is glossed "material module", and the kind also holds framing and
  geometric modules** (the storey graduation, the timber bay). The definition says "most often".

## V. What a basis cannot say, and two hazards now in the README

`GLOSSARY_REC_RE` admits no file under `docs/reports/`, `docs/prd/` or `docs/open-questions/`. So a
page Phase 14 invents — the front door, the rail groups, the Glossary, the guided example, the
assistant's name, the "drawn upright" foot line — has nothing admitted to rest on except the
content it introduces. Each such basis says so in its own words and quotes `VISION.md`,
`README.md` or `docs/` for the content, and the two figure records describe the wall face and how a
casing is measured from `trim-classical`'s own member notes and claim nothing about plate
furniture. **The assistant's definition does not say it cannot see the page**, because no admitted
file states it; it avoids implying that it can (`oq/the-assistant-is-blind-to-the-page`).

Two hazards the authors met are now written into `glossary/README.md`:

- **A quotation carrying a corpus count goes stale without a sound.** `check_counts.py --fix`
  rewrites the figures in `README.md` and `docs/`, and the next corpus change breaks the quotation
  rather than the definition. Batch A cut three such quotations.
- **Every path the checker finds in a basis becomes the record's served `reads` provenance line**,
  quoted from or not. Quoting a command line that named `build/compose.py` put that file in the
  guided example's provenance though nothing was read there.

The short-quotation mispairing Batch D met (a quotation under twenty-five characters makes
`_QUOTE_RE` read the author's own prose between two quotes as one quotation) was already in the
README, WP-14.1 having written it down from the same finding.

## VI. What was deliberately not done

- **No cross-batch `confusable_with`.** §B.0 forbade it, so four authors could work without seeing
  each other. The natural pairs across batches are listed by the authors and are the next package
  to declare: member (C) with member-of (B) and section-members (A); derived-rule (C) with
  constraint (D) and section-rules (A); proportion-pack (C) with surface-proportions and
  section-proportions (A); authority (C) with bibliographic-source (D); pack-kind-trim-system's
  "trim family" (C) with rank-family (B); on-the-bench (D) with surface-workbench (A), whose aka is
  "the bench"; slot-variant (B) with slot (D); binding-* (B) with binding (D); rank-* (B) with rank
  (D).
- **The families `model` and `house` carry no `order`.** Each is shared between two batches, and a
  partial order would sort one batch's records after every ordered one. Both sort by id until one
  commit orders a whole family.
- **No record is sourced.** That is a measurement of where the corpus's bibliographies stand, not a
  shortcut: none of these definitions is a sentence a bibliography already carries.

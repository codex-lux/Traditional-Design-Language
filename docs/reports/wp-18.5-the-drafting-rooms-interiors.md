# WP-18.5: the drafting room's interiors

*Status: BUILT (27 Nov 2026) · Phase 18 · follows `docs/reports/wp-18.1-the-rest-of-the-rooms.md`*

WP-18.1 to 18.4 redrew the twelve remaining surfaces and listed, in its §V, the views inside them
that were still as Phase 17 left them. This report covers those views, and the rendered-contrast
read of the paper pages that the same list named as not run.

## I. What was asked

Nothing new was asked. Lucas's reply of 27 Nov 2026 to the first tranche was *"Looks good! Keep
going"*, and the closing message of WP-18.1 to 18.4 listed what was left. This package is that
list.

**Pull request #48 was opened from the interface and merged while this was being worked.** It
carried the branch as far as `3d66f1a`, which is WP-18.1 to 18.4. This package is on the same
branch, after that merge, and is in no pull request.

**One decision in this package is this session's own and was not put to Lucas.** A rendered read
found the affinity ladder's words below 4.5 : 1 on paper. They are bound to the paper inks, on the
same reading of his reply that WP-18.4 records. It is five lines in `tokens.css`, twice.

## II. What was built

**The candidate set with candidates in it.**
- **A column's order.** A column ran: name, standing, the score, eight rows of the score's
  arithmetic, a note on what could not be judged, three counts, a list of fault names, and only
  then *why* this diagram is here and what it *trades away*. It runs now: name, standing, the
  score, why, trades away, how it scores, the counts, the facts, the warnings, the way on to the
  bench.
- **What did not change.** The score still leads and still shows its working, unfolded. The trade
  still stands next to the score (the column's own first rule). A disqualified column is still
  scored, and its band still stands under the number.
- **The fatal faults nobody judged.** Their mark, their word and their count stay in view. Their
  names ran on under the mark, a dozen lines in every column; they are one click under it now,
  each on its own line.
- **The reading face.** The score is a display numeral where it was 26 px Courier. An axis is
  named in the reading face and its figures stay in the data face. The counts, the facts, the
  standing line and the strip's tally and act are in the reading face.
- **The way on.** *open in the workbench* is ruled off at the foot of a column with an arrow.
  A click on it no longer also selects the column.
- **A diagram's name is set as a name** where the pool's passed-over list and the dropped list set
  it in 12 px Courier.

**The finding rows on the plan page.** A row was four columns: the severity, the layer at 76 px
of Courier, the statement in what was left, and a room's id. The list is grouped by layer under a
heading, so the layer column said again what the heading had just said. The severity, the layer
and the room are one narrow side column now, stacked, and the statement takes the rest with its
two tags run on after it. An opened row's *why* and *fix* align under the statement. *show on
drawing* is a link in the reading face.

**The export page.** It leads with its own record's line. The four cards are one grid of equal
columns with their titles at display size; they were a wrapping row whose fourth card stood alone
at another width. The strip keeps the face picker and no longer repeats the page's name.

**The Drawing Set's strip.** It printed the plan's id and its style's id in Courier until it ran
out of pane. The sidebar names the house, so the two ids are a record's note here, shown when ids
are asked for. The captions under a sheet are in the reading face.

**A tracing draft.** The side panel's labels, fields, notes and gap list were Courier at 11.5 and
12.5 px. They are in the reading face at 15 px. The canvas and its tools are unchanged.

**The family tree's name column.** WP-18.1 widened it to 260 px and recorded the cost: with the
assistant open at 1280 px the chart scrolled sideways 95 px inside, where it had scrolled 45. The
column is a share of the chart it stands in now, between 150 and 260 px
(`surfaces/phylo/labelWidth.js`, a leaf). At that width the chart no longer scrolls sideways.

**The affinity ladder's words, on paper.** See §III.1.

## III. What was found on the way

1. **Forty-nine words on paper read below 4.5 : 1, and no token table had read them.** WP-18.4
   bound the word duties on paper to the paper inks and emptied both lists of known rows. A
   rendered read of twenty-eight paper pages then found the affinity ladder's words: *canonical*
   at 4.09, *common* at 3.78 and *forbidden* at 3.15 on its own field. `grounds.test.mjs` §10
   holds every ladder duty on the instrument. Nothing held one on paper. The affinity rungs have
   one reader, `VariantPill`'s map, which sets each as a word, so they are bound to the paper
   inks at `:root` and under vellum, and `rooms.test.mjs` holds them. **This is the same finding
   Phase 17's audit made on the dark ground: read contrast off the rendered page.**
2. **The other ladders were read and are not changed.** The variant and binding ladders are a
   swatch in the rung's pigment beside a word in a text duty, and the read found no word of
   theirs under 4.5 : 1. `--bind-open`, `--judge-unjudged` and `--edge-claims` are set as marks.
3. **The only words left under 4.5 : 1 on the paper pages are four disabled buttons** on the plate
   viewer (2.26). A disabled control is not held to the threshold.
4. **The first read was wrong on one page.** It reported 210 words on the elements index at an
   opacity of 0.72. The page was read part-way through its entrance. Read alone after seven
   seconds, and again in two whole runs, it is clean. The reader waits 2.6 s a page; a page that
   is still arriving at 2.6 s reads as faded.
5. **A selection row's floor was held to a face it is no longer set in.** The strip's act has a
   row with a floor of two lines, in `em`, and a test derived the 2.9 from `--type-data-s`'s line
   height. The row is in the reading face now. The test reads the line height the row itself
   declares, so a row re-set at another line height fails there.
6. **A name button's baseline is its last line.** The rank beside a column's name was aligned to
   the name's baseline, so beside a name that wrapped it stood on the second line. It stands on
   the first.
7. **The first cut of the finding row cost a quarter of the list.** It put the layer, the tags
   and the room on a line under the statement. Measured, the statement went from 212 to 325 px
   wide and a row from 66 to 86 px tall, so a screen held a quarter fewer findings. The
   component's own first sentence is that the findings must fit. The side column keeps a row at
   66 px with the statement at 311.
8. **Pull request #48's CI found one red that is this phase's own.** `tests/test_svg_census.py`
   refuses a file that emits SVG and is not in its registry. WP-18.0 drew the product's mark as an
   inline SVG in `Chrome.jsx` and ran no test under `tests/`, and neither did WP-18.1 to 18.4. It
   is registered out of scope, with its reason. **Two reports of this phase say what was not run;
   neither said that a test under `tests/` reads the app's source.**
9. **`docs/fidelity.md` could not be regenerated here.** Regenerating it added the one new row and
   also rewrote four DXF rows as could-not-evaluate, because this container has no `ezdxf`. The
   four rows are left as the doc had them and only the new row is added, by hand. The census
   tests pass on that.
10. **A guard of this package read past the end of its own subject.** The finding-row test sliced
    the source up to a marker it looked for with `indexOf`. The source reader strips comments, the
    compiled `/*#__PURE__*/` among them, so the marker was never found and the slice ran to one
    character short of the file's end. The mutations it was checked against were still red. It was
    found when the row was re-cut and the same lookup was used as a premise.

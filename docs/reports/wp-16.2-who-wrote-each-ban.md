# WP-16.2 — Who wrote each ban, and the corrections in the styles' own kits

*Phase 16, 29–30 September 2026. R3: fix each wrong inherited ban in the style's own kit, case by
case; then refuse what the resolved kit forbids, naming the node that wrote the ban.*

## I. What was asked

Lucas's ruling R3 of 29 September 2026, asked as a direct question
(`oq/an-inherited-ban-decides-what-the-elevation-may-draw`):

> Decision 4 stands. First, each inherited ban that strips a style wrongly is corrected in that
> style's own kit, case by case (for example, colonial-revival binds its own water table, belt,
> frieze and doorcase). Then the elevation refuses what remains and names the node each ban
> comes from.

This package is the first half: the attribution (stage 1) and the corrections (stage 2). The
refusal itself is WP-16.4.

## II. Stage 1: a ban is credited to the node that wrote it (`a2aacc7`)

**The defect.** Every reader of "where does this ban come from" read the resolved slot's
`_source`. That field names the NEAREST node to touch the slot: the nearest `extends` delta, where
one was merged. Where a style extends a slot an ancestor wrote a ban in, the ban read as the
style's own. colonial-revival's doorcase was the case: gothic-revival-british wrote
`pilasters-and-entablature` forbidden, colonial-revival extended the slot with its own figures, and
census V2 said "doorcase (door_surround: own)".

**What `resolve_slots` records now.**
- `_bound_by` on every slot: the node whose record set the binding. A delta cannot change a
  binding, so it is the base the deltas merged onto; `null` for an open slot.
- `_written_by` on every variant row: the node whose record or delta wrote it. A `replace`
  credits the replacer.
- `_source` is unchanged, because it answers a different question: which record to open.

`resolve_kit.forbidden_by(rec, words)` is the one reader of who forbids a feature. It returns the
writers, sorted, or `None`; a feature one of whose matching rows is not forbidden is not forbidden.

**Three readers moved.**
- **Census V2.** Over the 21 styles that draw something their kit forbids, the split went from
  2 own / 14 inherited / 5 both to 2 / 17 / 2, as the planning pass predicted. Six rows moved in
  their attribution text alone.
- **The elevation's dormer line** credits the variant to the node that wrote the row, and says
  whether the style extends the slot or binds it nothing.
- **The Dossier's slot page** served the nearest kit FILE's variant list whole. Where that file
  extends the slot, the list is the delta alone, so the page hid every inherited row, the bans
  among them. It serves the resolved ladder now, each row with `written_by`, and `bound_by`.

**Found.** The gable-return ban on nine shipped plans is gothic-revival-american's bargeboard
rule, and on bad-03, good-04 and good-06 american-farmhouse-vernacular's; not colonial-revival's.

**Checked.** `tests/test_ban_writers.py` (15) and two server tests; 16 mutations over the new
guards, each asserted to land and restored by sha256, all red.

## III. Stage 2: the corrections, in the styles' own kits

**How each case was decided.**
- **Tabled.** Every ban reaching a style the elevation draws was grouped by (writer, feature).
  Each was read against the ban's own note and the receiving style's own record.
- **Where the records decided.** The style's own kit was corrected on WP-8.3's template,
  colonial-revival's `transom_sidelight`:
  - the style binds the slot itself;
  - every row quotes its own record, or cites Lucas's answer and is marked `judgment: true`;
  - a dated note names the ban it replaces and the node that wrote it.
- **Where they did not.** The question went to Lucas directly, in three rounds (§III.3).

**1. The thirteen (drafted 29 Sep; bound 30 Sep).**
- Nine edits the adjudication proposed. The independent check upheld six and amended three.
- Four companion records the check wrote, where a descendant's own words contradicted what the
  nine would hand it: garrison-revival's `cornice` and `door_surround`,
  modern-farmhouse-traditional's `cornice_return`, and neo-eclectic's `cornice`.
- Each record's one date stamp was moved to the day it was bound. Nothing else in any record
  changed.

**2. The twenty, on answers A1, A2, A4, A5, A7 and A8.** A second independent check upheld seven
and amended thirteen. It verified every quotation it wrote, word for word, against the tree.
It also wrote four companions:
- cape-cod-revival's `frieze`, forbidden on its own c05 (a **soft** constraint: "Cornice returns,
  modillions, friezes, pediments over windows, and full-width porches are forbidden."), and
  diagnostic_tells[3];

**3. The questions (A1 to A10).** They were asked in three rounds, at 22:37 UTC on 29 Sep and at
01:00 and 02:31 UTC on 30 Sep. They were answered at 00:48, 01:29 and 12:03 UTC on 30 Sep, and

**4. A10's record, checked by a third independent check.** neo-eclectic's `belt_course`:
- specified, a `none` row permitted, nothing canonical, `judgment: true`;

**5. Corrections made to the checked records before they landed, each asserted in the apply
script.**

  mediterranean-revival's `door_surround` each said the ruling reaches the node "by descent

  renaissance-revival-american's `transom_sidelight` were bound `forbidden` whole, with no
  governing pack. Now `opening-mullioned` governs each, bound on french-renaissance-chateau and
  jacobean respectively.

  - These two now do too, in the same words.

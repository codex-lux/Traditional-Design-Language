# WP-14.3 — a convicted instrument reports and does not disqualify

*Phase 14, package 3. 20 September 2026. Ruling of the same day: **a fatal from an instrument
the corpus has convicted REPORTS, in full, with its figure, and does not disqualify.** Cite this
report by filename.*

## The headline is the measurement, and it is that this buys almost nothing

**One fatal finding of 158 across the sixteen shipped plans. Zero plans change disqualification
status. Zero composed candidates change.** `engine="heuristic"` throughout, deterministic.

    good-03-parlor-drawing-room-house    fatal 8  ->  disqualifying 7   (still disqualified)
    every other shipped plan                          unchanged
    16 of 16 plans disqualified, before and after

The approved plan said, in advance: *"If the answer is near zero the ruling has been executed and
the problem is WP-14.5's; say so rather than widening the list until the number looks better."*
It is near zero, and this report says so rather than widening it.

**The reason is in the census, not in the list.** Of 158 fatal findings over the sixteen plans,
**131 are not fault findings at all** — 93 `unreachable` and 38 `adjacency`, both the PLACEMENT's
— and only 27 come from the fault corpus. No excusal of a fault instrument can reach the other
131. The composed candidates split the opposite way and reach the same conclusion: both of
`briefs/family-georgian.json`'s returned candidates carry *only* fault fatals, and neither is
convicted (below).

## The one entry, and it is a good one

`good-03-parlor-drawing-room-house` is `greek-revival-american`. `column-without-entasis`
measures `upper_shaft_diameter / lower_shaft_diameter` at **1.0 against a band of 0.75–0.87**,
and the fault's `severity_by_style` makes it FATAL for that style with an excellent reason:

> The Greek Revival column is the whole building. Benjamin's Grecian Doric diminishes to 0.783 —
> the most pronounced taper in the pack — and a parallel Greek Doric is not a simplification of
> the order but a different object.

**And the 1.0 is not a fact about the house.** `critique/suspects.json` has recorded it as
`cs-parallel-shaft` since WP-9.1, with a basis quoting `build/elevation.py`'s own comment —
*"upper and lower diameter are genuinely identical"*, beside the note that no entasis rule exists
anywhere in this pack or this codebase — and `build/check_critic_suspects.py` verifies that
quotation against the file it names on every run.

So a `good-*` reference plan was being disqualified by a number the generator states about
itself, in a file the corpus already wrote down. **The same fault reads `serious` on eight other
plans at exactly the same value of 1.0**; only Greek Revival escalates it, which is why this was
invisible until somebody counted fatals by fault id.

## What was refused, and this is the more important half

**Three candidates were examined and rejected, each by execution rather than by reading.**

**`one-bay-symmetry-break` — refused, and it is the only fatal on the composer's own returned
leader.** Excusing it would have taken `side-hall-townhouse` to zero disqualifying fatals and
made the card read clean. `oq/the-facade-layer-counts-a-dependencys-windows-as-bays-of-the-front`
convicted this measurement in September — and **WP-13.8 executed the ruling on it**, so
`axis.front_openings` returns the main block's own population and the wing's separately. What
fires now is the main block's own front, honestly measured, and WP-13.8's report says in its own
headline that the fatal does not clear. The instrument was convicted and then fixed; the finding
that survives is the house's.

**`truss-flattened-pitch` — refused, and the question that would have warranted it is wrong about
its own mechanism.** `oq/the-depth-a-roof-needs-is-known-and-cannot-be-enforced` says
`good-05-lobby-gallery-mansion` is *"convicted FATALLY by this fault today, through a licence
that names its sibling"* — the licence naming `italianate-american` where the plan's style is
`italian-renaissance-revival` — and cites
`oq/a-licence-matches-the-style-id-exactly-and-never-its-descendants`.

**Run rather than read: `italian-renaissance-revival` has no `descends_from` at all.** It is a
root node, `member_of: eclectic-revivals`, while `italianate-american` is `member_of:
romantic-revivals`. They are not parent and child, and they are not siblings — they are in
different families. **The licence would not reach that plan under any of the fixes that question
proposes**, because that question is about DESCENDANTS. The conviction does not apply, so the
excusal does not either. That correction is carried back into the question's own file.

**`cornice-that-is-a-fascia` — refused**, and it is the largest fault population in the corpus
(7 of 16 plans). Its two-rival-secondaries defect was OQ 84 and OQ 84 is **CLOSED**. A settled
question is not a live conviction.

## The mechanism: no second list, and a two-part warrant

The ruling asked for *"a named list, never a heuristic … `critique/suspects.json`'s precedent"*.
**The precedent is the answer: that file already IS the named list**, so nothing here creates a
second one. `build/critic_suspects.py` gains `convicted()` and `convicts(finding)` beside
`editorial()` and `why_suspect()`, and both `plan_check` and `compose` come through them.

An entry now has two powers and they are gated differently:

| | warrant | power |
|---|---|---|
| `basis` (existing) | the GENERATOR admits the measurement is its own, quoted and verified against the file | blocks `build/revise.py` from sending a generator to fix it |
| `basis` **and** `question` (new) | and the CORPUS has taken a position on it, in an OPEN register entry naming the fault | **also** takes its fatals out of `disqualified` |

**An entry with a basis and no question keeps the move block and excuses no verdict**, and
`cs-stair-at-the-band-edge` is exactly that today — 3 editorial, 2 convicted. The absence is a
state, not an omission, and a test asserts `convicted()` is a STRICT subset of `editorial()`,
because otherwise the gate is not a gate.

**Narrow by construction on three axes.** It matches the `(fault, EXPRESSION)` pair and never the
fault alone, so a fault's other tests are untouched. It reads the hand-authored entries and never
`why_suspect`'s AST hits — 41 ratcheted literal names and 6 ratios, a population that grows
without anyone ruling on it. And the finding is **untouched**: still emitted, still `fatal`, still
carrying its figure, still in `counts`. Only `disqualified` and `_sort_key`'s primary key stop
reading it.

**Measured: the named list and the AST heuristic agree today, at one fatal each.** That agreement
is a property of this corpus on this date and is not a reason to take the wider one; it is
recorded in the code so a later reader does not "simplify" one into the other.

## Three states, because two would be a fake pass

`plan_check` returns `None` — not `[]` — when `critic_suspects` will not load. `None` means
*could not evaluate*, nothing is excused, the count stands, and `fatal_excused_unjudged` says so
on the card. An empty list means the sweep ran and found none. A reader who cannot tell those
apart is being told "nothing is wrong" by a run that checked nothing.

## The checker

`check_critic_suspects.py` verifies every cited `question` three ways, and each covers a
different way a citation rots:

- **it exists** in the register — catches a slug typed wrong;
- **it is OPEN** — catches a question ruled since, because the power this field grants is *the
  corpus has not settled this*, and a settled question would withdraw it in silence;
- **it names the fault in its own text** — catches the citation that resolves and is about
  something else, which is `oq/a-source-that-agrees-numerically-may-be-the-wrong-quantity` one
  layer over.

It reads `build/check_ids.py::read_questions()` rather than globbing, so the status vocabulary is
spelled once. All three were driven and each produces its own message. The run's OK line now
states how many entries carry a question, **including when that is none**, because a green tick
over a gate that never executed and one over a gate that verified two entries are otherwise the
same tick.

## Verification

Eleven new guards in `tests/test_convicted_instrument.py`; the app suite 226 → **227**.

**Six Python mutations and five JS ones, each asserted to have LANDED, each file restored
byte-for-byte. Two Python mutations were BLIND on the first run and one of them was the ruling
itself.**

Restoring `disqualified = bool(fatal)` left all ten guards GREEN. **No plan in this corpus has a
convicted fatal as its ONLY fatal** — `good-03` has seven others — so on every shipped record
`bool(fatal)` and `bool(disqualifying)` return the same answer and the whole ruling is
unobservable. That is WP-11.15's *a fixture where both branches return the same number guards
neither*, and the branch left unguarded was the one the package exists for. It is DRIVEN now,
with its premise asserted, and the mutation bites.

The second blind one is a genuine **equivalent mutant**: `len(conv or [])` and
`len(conv) if isinstance(conv, list) else 0` return the same number for all three states the key
can hold. The defensive spelling is kept, with the reason written beside it — the neighbouring
`_clear_on_a_constant` returns a DICT for its could-not-evaluate case, and if this key ever grows
that shape the `or []` form would silently excuse one fatal per dict key.

**Two JS mutations were blind for one reason: the selector matched the field somewhere else in
the same file.** Each of the three names appears twice in `CandidateColumn.jsx` — once in the
condition that decides whether a block renders and once in the body that renders it — so
replacing a condition with `false` left a bare `c.<field>` match green, and dropping one
`data-fatal-excused` attribute left the other one matching. Re-cut against the render CONDITION
and against both attribute sites, all five bite.

Checkers: `check_critic_suspects` OK (and now naming the two convicted entries),
`check_citations` 0 dangling over 696 files, `check_ids` clean, `check_counts` 0 stale.

## What is deliberately not done

- **The list is not widened to make a number move.** Three candidates were examined and all three
  refused, with the evidence above.
- **Nothing is reclassified as unjudged.** An excusal that dropped or downgraded the finding would
  be the fake-unjudged direction; the row is still there, still fatal, still carrying 1.0.
- **`severity_by_style` is not touched.** Greek Revival is right that a parallel shaft is fatal on
  a Greek Revival house. The defect is that the generator cannot draw a diminished shaft, and the
  fix for that is to model entasis — which is `oq/thirty-five-measurements-the-elevation-states-as-literals`'s
  work and not a threshold to move.

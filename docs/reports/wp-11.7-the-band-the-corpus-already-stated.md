# WP-11.7 — the shape band, and the ranked ladder that had to be inverted

*5 September 2026. The second package of the B line. Ruled by Lucas on one question put to him
with the measurement in hand: the room's own proportion band outranks the record's declared
`exterior_walls`.*

`PLAN-OF-ACTION.md` asks for *"per-room side bounds at an authored tolerance, the spanning
passage pinned on the block axis, the ladder generalised from `wall_keys` to a ranked list."*
Two of those three are built. The third was built, measured, and refused. And the tolerance is
not the one the plan asked for.

---

## I. The baseline, which is the reason for the package

`plans/tidewater-georgian-careful.json`, `engine="auto"`, before:

```
OPTIMAL (hard-only) — kept hard-only phase A
serious 79 · minor 81 · diverged 14 · relaxations 11 · stacks kept 0 of 5
```

and the drawing behind those numbers:

| room | declared | drawn |
|---|---|---|
| `hallbath` | 8 × 11 | **39 × 4** |
| `chamber3` | 13 × 16 | **45 × 7** |
| `primary` | 17 × 20 | 45 × 12 |
| `passage` | 12 × 34 | 22 × 19 — not spanning |

**Fifteen wall pins had been downgraded, among them `L0 passage N` and `L0 passage S`** — the
pair this engine's own docstring calls *"the spanning rule"*. So the centre passage had stopped
spanning, and `plan_check`'s entrance-axis census read `passage_has_no_through_axis: 1` and
compared nothing.

---

## II. The tolerance is the corpus's own band, not an authored τ

The plan asks for `w, h ∈ [(1−τ)·d, (1+τ)·d]` with **τ authored once, editorial**. That was not
built, and the reason is the project's own rule about inventing numbers.

The corpus already states the shape rule: every room record carries `dimensions.proportion`, the
band `plan_check`'s drawn layer already convicts a room for leaving. Measured before choosing —
**all 23 dimensioned rooms on the Tidewater record declare a shape INSIDE their own type's
band.** So binding the band constrains the drawing and contradicts no authored record, and there
is no τ to source.

It is also the rule the critic tests, which satisfies `vertical_score`'s own standing
requirement: the search and the arbiter must not convict and acquit the same house. One
spelling, `GEO.shape_band`, which the soft aspect term already read.

The FLOOR is deliberately not stated: every band's floor has been 1.0 since the 3 Sep ruling and
`max ≥ min` holds by construction, so a floor constraint would be a branch that can never bite.

**Two rooms have no band at all** — `terrace` and `landing`. Their drawn shape is unconstrained
here and unjudged by the drawn layer too. That is stated in `reqs.notes` rather than left silent.

---

## III. The two authored facts that cannot both be hard

Built, and the model went INFEASIBLE at every footprint to ten bays. The diagnosis took three
measurements and the third is the one that mattered.

**Coverage is not the blocker.** The ladder gives up 14 or 15 of the 15 shape pins at *every*
coverage floor:

| `COVERAGE` | 0.97 | 0.90 | 0.80 | 0.70 | 0.60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| shape pins given up (of 15) | 15 | 14 | 14 | 14 | 15 |

So the plan file's *"`COVERAGE = 0.97` lowered and `derive_footprint` given a wall-and-slack
allowance"* would not have helped, and `oq/the-placement-carries-no-wall-bands` is not what is
in the way. **Packing is not the blocker either:**

```
shape band held, all 22 wall pins released  ->  OPTIMAL
shape band held, wall pins held             ->  INFEASIBLE
```

The first conflict core says it in three literals: the Back Hall's declared **N** wall, its
declared **S** wall, and its 7 × 16 ft programme. A room declaring an opposite pair must span the
40 ft depth; at 112 sf that is 2.8 ft wide, which is 14 to 1 against a band of 5.

**CLAUDE.md has carried the reason since WP-2.2:** *"A plan's `exterior_walls` are aspirations,
not rectangle edges. Three Tidewater ground rooms each declare opposite walls, so each would have
to span the full depth of the house. They are weights, at the 14 points `exterior_score` charges.
**Do not promote them to constraints; the corpus does not mean them that way.**"* This engine
promoted them anyway, from the day it was written. The shape band is what made a stated
contradiction into a measured one.

**Ruled 5 Sep 2026: the band outranks the pins.** `_RANK = ("wall", "axis", "shape")`, lowest
authority first, and the wall goes first. A released wall pin is not discarded — it keeps the
heuristic's own 14 points in the objective, which is what that note always said it was worth.

---

## IV. The ladder

It read `wall_keys = [key for _t, k, key in core if k == "wall" and key]` — one kind,
hard-coded. So every requirement added since would have been either un-downgradable (and would
have taken the whole placement to INFEASIBLE) or soft, which on this plan means invisible:
`_finish_feasible` keeps the hard-only phase A placement whenever the polish times out, and the
objective never runs.

It walks `_RANK` now and releases the first kind with a live pin. Two decisions inside it are
measurements rather than taste:

**A round releases the WHOLE of the lowest-ranked live kind, not the pins the core names.** The
narrow version was written first and swept: it needs EIGHT rounds, and the intermediate states —
some walls released, the band still held — are the expensive ones, so at a 6 s per-round cap
round 2 comes back UNKNOWN and the ladder never converges inside any budget the bench can spend.
Releasing the rank whole reaches the same end state in ONE round. It over-releases, and the file
already answers that: `_reinstate` restores each pin alone at the footprint being drawn and keeps
the ones that hold. Its own docstring — *"a solver core is SUFFICIENT, not minimal"* — is the
same reasoning one round larger. Measured on the shipped plan, `_reinstate` wins one of the 22
back inside budget and says of the rest that they were carried, not proven.

**`downgraded` holds two shapes of key now** — `(level, room, wall)` and `(level, room)` — and
three readers unpacked three names from every one of them. `downgraded_wall_pins`,
`downgraded_shape_pins` and `downgrade_rounds` are on the record; `_reinstate`'s attempt label
would have raised IndexError inside the reinstatement pass, where nothing in the traceback would
have named the ladder.

---

## V. `_absorb` undid the proof, for the third time

CP proved every room inside its band. The post-solve absorb pass then grew three of them
straight back out of it — the **library to 1.69 against a ceiling of 1.6, the powder room to
2.66 against 2.2, a closet to 4.21 against 4.0**, all three with their pin still HELD.

That function's own docstring already records the same defect twice: an uncapped absorption
stretching a 2.8 sf linen press to 8 sf ("a drawing quietly violating its own proof"), and OQ 55's
upper room grown across a courtyard ("a guarantee proven and then undone by a post-pass"). It
takes `ratios` now beside `caps` and `keepout`, and a room whose pin the ladder RELEASED is
deliberately absent from it: the record says its band could not hold, so there is nothing to
preserve.

**It was caught by a test, not by reading** — `test_no_room_is_drawn_outside_its_own_band_when_
the_pins_hold` failed on its first run and named all three rooms.

## VI. The axis: the spanning half holds, the centring is refused

`kind="axis"`, ranked between wall and shape. It states that the spanning circulation room runs
through the house.

**Which room, and the first answer was wrong.** "Every circulation room declaring an opposite
pair" selects TWO on this record — the Centre Passage and the Back Hall, which is circulation and
declares N and S as a *hyphen*. Two rooms pinned to one centre line cannot both hold, so the
ladder released both axis pins and the passage went back to running across the house. An axis
rule that names two rooms names none. It is the room the FRONT DOOR OPENS INTO now — the relation
`plan_check`'s own entrance-axis census walks — and where that picks out no room or more than
one, the axis is UNJUDGED and nothing is pinned.

**The centring is refused with its measurement.** `2x + w == W` is what the plan file asks for.
Built and measured: with the bands held and all 22 wall pins already released — the most
permissive model this engine can offer — it is **INFEASIBLE in 0.9 s**. The arithmetic is a fact
about the record, not the solver: the passage declares 12 × 34, spanning the 40.08 ft depth puts
it 10.2 ft wide, and centred on a 60 ft front that leaves two strips of 24.9 ft holding about
2,000 sf of programme in 1,992 sf of floor — an exact tiling of both strips by rooms that must
each sit inside their own band. There is no slack anywhere in it, which is
`oq/the-placement-carries-no-wall-bands` measured from the other side. A symmetric passage needs
the footprint to gain slack, not a harder constraint. `test_the_centring_equality_is_refused_
with_its_measurement` keeps the refusal from quietly becoming a reinstatement.

## VII. What it does to the drawing

`plans/tidewater-georgian-careful.json`, `engine="auto"`:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| fatal | 0 | 0 |
| **serious** | **79** | **56** |
| minor | 81 | 83 |
| diverged (±10% area) | 14 | 18 |
| relaxations | 11 | 13 |
| **stacks kept** | **0 of 5** | **3 of 5** |
| shape pins given up | — | **0 of 15** |
| worst room against its own band | `chamber3` 6.4:1 vs 1.35 | **none outside its band** |
| the passage | 22 × 19, not spanning | **9 × 40, spanning** |

Every room is drawn inside the band its own record states, and the passage runs through the
house again — which is what WP-11.6's stacking claims were waiting for: **stacks kept 0 → 3**.

`diverged` rises because the metric is on AREA at ±10% and the band governs SHAPE; the area band
is [0.88, 1.20] and is untouched. Taking `diverged` to zero means tightening the area band, which
is a different package and a different number.

**EVERY FIGURE IN THIS SECTION IS AN `auto` FIGURE AND `auto` IS NOT REPRODUCIBLE**, which
CLAUDE.md records for a different sweep ("the same sweep returned 130, 132 and 133 on one
unchanged tree"): CP-SAT under a wall-clock budget is not deterministic under load. Two runs of
the unchanged baseline gave serious **79** and **75** on this plan. So read `79 → 56` as a
movement of about twenty rather than of exactly twenty-three, and **ratchet none of it** — the
deterministic figures in this repository are the `heuristic` ones, and the heuristic is untouched
by this package.

## VIII. The cost, and it is on the other shipped plan

`plans/spec-builder-colonial.json` solves to OPTIMAL in **29.6 s** against a 25 s budget. It is
not infeasible — given 90 s it proves — so the plan falls back to the heuristic and loses its
proof, by five seconds. fatal 4 → 10, serious 132 → 97; six more fatals and thirty-five fewer
serious, and the plate says which engine drew it.

`PLAN-OF-ACTION.md`'s own WP-11.7 text anticipates the choice — *"raise the budget on the
reference plans or state the fallback"* — and this package **states the fallback**, because
`/api/plan/evaluate` is the route the infrastructure audit measured as the whole server's bound
and 40% on its worst case is not a cost to spend without a ruling.
`oq/a-shipped-plan-needs-thirty-seconds-and-the-budget-is-twenty-five` carries the numbers and
the three options.

**And the obvious speed-up is slower.** `max/min <= c` rewritten as `w <= c*h AND h <= c*w` needs
no reified auxiliaries and reads as strictly cheaper: measured, spec-builder 29.6 → **48.2** s and
tidewater 11.3 → **20.4** s. CP-SAT's max/min propagators beat two reified linear constraints
here by about 1.7x. The numbers are in the comment above the constraint and in a test, because
the rewrite will otherwise be attempted a third time.

**That measurement nearly went the other way, and the reason is worth carrying.** The first
attempt at it reported `exit code 144`, did not land, and the timing run that followed compared
the max/min encoding against ITSELF — 29.9 s against 29.6 s, "no difference", which is exactly
what a null result looks like. It was caught because a `grep` guarding the edit printed nothing
and the shell's `&&` swallowed the message. **Read the exit code of the edit, not only the output
of the run after it.**

## IX. What this package does NOT do

The heuristic is untouched — every figure of the search engine is unchanged, and the three pinned
relaxation counts do not move. `COVERAGE` is not lowered and `derive_footprint` gains no slack
allowance: the sweep above shows neither would have helped, so
`oq/the-placement-carries-no-wall-bands` is left for the package that can act on it. The area
band is untouched. No `τ` is authored anywhere. The entrance-axis census still reports
`passage_has_no_through_axis` because it needs the passage's N and S *doors* placed, which is the
openings layer and not this one.

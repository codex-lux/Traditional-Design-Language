## What it is for

A style had two pages, and each was reached by its own address:

- the **Style Record** (`#/style/<id>`), one long page;
- the **Kit** (`#/kit/<id>/<slot>`), which reached the same style by a different address.

Neither page could be reached without a style. A bare `#/style` opened Tidewater Georgian, and so
did a bare `#/kit`, because both surfaces carried a `DEFAULT_STYLE`. So a link somebody pasted
opened a record the link did not name.

The package puts everything about one style in one citable place, the **Style Dossier**. It is
read section by section, in the order the corpus is consulted:

1. identify
2. filed under this
3. lineage
4. kit
5. proportions
6. plan types
7. constraints
8. faults
9. evidence

The dossier draws only the section in view and omits every empty one. A bare `#/style` is an
outline to recognise a style by, and never shows a record. `#/kit` becomes an alias for the
dossier's kit section.

## I. What was built

### The place

The place is decided by `dossier/sections.js::placeOf`, and nowhere else. It follows the PRD's
order (§E.2):

- **A style is present:** the dossier. With no section named, that is `identify`.
- **No style, the kit section and a slot:** the slot panel, at `#/style/-/kit/<slot>`. This is one
  slot across every style, on the `/api/slots/{id}` route nothing had used.
- **Anything else:** the Styles index.

### The dossier (`surfaces/StyleDossier.jsx`)

**The head.** It carries:

- the rank, as a `Term`;
- what the style is filed under, as a link;
- its years;
- its name, with its id as a margin note;
- its tradition's swatch;
- a `StylePicker` whose pick writes the URL;
- "Design a house in this style →" to `#/brief?style=`, for buildable ranks only;
- "place in the phylogeny →".

**The strip.** The strip is the dossier payload's own section list:

- It is in `DOSSIER_SECTIONS` order, whatever order the rows arrive in.
- Each section is labelled by its `section-<id>` glossary record.
- Each section carries the payload's count.

`listedSections` holds the server's zero-omission rule a second time on the way in. So a section
ever served at zero is still not offered.

**A section the strip does not list.** A URL may name such a section. It is REFUSED by name ("This
record has no Kit section"), and the page draws identify rather than an empty section.

**The style in hand.** `prefs.setStyleInHand` is called when the resolved payload is for the id the
page names. That is the only thing the dossier does with it:

- the store is written here;
- the index offers it as a link;
- nothing reads it into a render.

### The sections (`dossier/*.jsx`)

Each section is EXTRACTED from `StyleRecord.jsx` or `KitSurface.jsx`, not rewritten.

**Identify.** It shows, in order:

1. summary cards, one per listed section, each with its payload count;
2. the description;
3. the diagnostic tells;
4. `distinguished_from`, as links;
5. the defining characteristics.

**Members.** It lists `members` and `buildable_at` from the payload, each with a rank `Term`.

**Lineage.**
- The lineage block of `StyleRecord` is extracted with its `EdgeGlyph` call exactly as it was.
- Descendants are links now, where they were buttons that swapped the record in place.
- It adds the cascade ladder, lifted into `relations.cascadeRows` so that the kit section and
  lineage read one spelling.

**Kit.** This is `KitSurface` embedded. `KitSurface` no longer has:

- a `DEFAULT_STYLE`;
- a picker;
- its own style state.

The dossier owns the style and the URL owns the open slot. The side panel's packs and massing
affinities went to the Proportions and Plan types sections. The cascade ladder stays in
`PANES.kit`, which is kept.

**Proportions.**
- The record's proportional-system prose comes first.
- Then `/api/styles/{id}/packs`, as its five provenances: bound, opted in, delivered, withheld and
  declined. Each group has a `pack-*` `Term` heading.
- The nearest ancestor's delivered group is shown. The farther ones fold behind the reader's own
  `delivered` fold in `state/prefs.js`.

**Plan types.** A LIST only (`oq/a-brief-cannot-name-a-parti`):

- massings, as `VariantPill`;
- partis and groupings, named with their ids and not linked.

**Constraints.**
- Each row's state is `judgment.constraintStateOf`, and its name is that state's glossary record.
- A judgment row keeps its hatched mark.
- `?constraint=` marks the row and scrolls to it.
- The row's own id is a link to exactly that place.

**Faults.** It reads the dossier payload:

- the verdict here;
- the faults licensed through the lineage;
- a link to the universal count, for style and variant ranks only.

**Evidence.** It shows the exemplars and sources, extracted. It adds the image records from
`/api/assets?style=`, each with its harvest status.

### The Styles index (`surfaces/StylesIndex.jsx`)

The index is `styles/styleTree.js`'s outline:

- traditions come in the server's order, and siblings by floruit;
- an orphan is listed rather than dropped.

`?q=` narrows the outline and keeps each match's filing chain above it. The index never fetches a
record.

### The relations panel (`components/RelationsPanel.jsx`)

The panel shows four things:

- Filed under;
- Filed under this;
- Neighbours, which is `relations.siblingsOf`: filing, not lineage;
- On the bench.

It sits in `PANES.kit` beside every section but the kit. On the kit section the pane is the kit's
own ladder, as it was when the Kit was a surface.

### The router and the citations

**`#/kit/<style>/<slot>` is an alias.**
- It is listed in `router.js::LEGACY_PATHS` and READ positionally, with the same `-` placeholder.
- It is never WRITTEN: `formatHash` answers the canonical address.
- `state/nav.js::canonicalize` rewrites the address bar by `replaceState`, as it already did for
  `#/cite/`.
- A bare `#/kit` is the index, because it named no style.

**`kit:` and `slot:` citations route into the dossier.** `kit:<id>#<slot>` opens the kit section on
that slot, and `slot:<id>` opens the slot panel. `citeFor` is the inverse of both.

**`names/names.js` takes the one constraint-id reader.** It is `citations.js::constraintStyleOf`,
where it had a local `lastIndexOf('.')`. A guard refuses the local copy.

**The palette's "The Kit" entry is merged into the style surface's entry.** The kit's words stay in
its haystack, so "kit" and "slots" still find it (`search-unit`).

### Tests

**`src/dossier.test.mjs`** has 24 tests over the two pure modules. They cover:

- the three places, driven from `parseHash` addresses;
- the strip's order, zero omission and deduplication;
- the refusal;
- the section address round trip;
- neighbours, pack groups, the fold, the ladder and the bench;
- that every word the dossier names is a glossary record.

It also holds the surfaces to five source facts:

- the style in hand is written once and read nowhere;
- the index fetches no record;
- no file on the style path names a default style;
- no new file sets text in `--ink-4`;
- every section component is drawn only under `view.id`.

**`router-unit` is 124 checks.** The alias table is driven in both directions, including a cold
load that must leave the address bar rewritten.

## II. What was found

### Three pages said something the corpus does not say, and a guard is behind each now

**1. The bare addresses showed a record the URL never named.** `StyleRecord` and `KitSurface` each
defaulted to `tidewater-georgian`. A pasted `#/style` opened a record nobody chose, and the W5
check could only look for the absence of one word in the first 400 characters. Two things replace
that:

- the structural check (the index is on screen and no dossier head is);
- `placeOf`, which has no branch that opens a record without a style.

Two mutations are red:

- `placeOf` opening the dossier on a bare `#/style`;
- the dossier filling a missing style from the style in hand.

**2. StyleRecord told three styles they were unbound.** It told `egyptian-revival`,
`moorish-andalusian` and `mudejar` they were *"deliberately unbound to any proportion pack — a
named exception, not a hole (OQ 29)"*. The claim came from a list written into the page. Measured
against `/api/styles/{id}/packs` today, **all three bind packs of their own**:

| style | packs it binds |
|---|---|
| `egyptian-revival` | 1 (`facade-peristyle`, OQ 49's scoped binding) |
| `moorish-andalusian` | 3 |
| `mudejar` | 3 |

The list went stale when OQ 49 closed. The Proportions section reads the packs payload, so it
cannot say this.

**3. The constraints column carried *"295 of 660 constraints are that kind"*.** That is a
corpus-wide count typed into JSX: *a figure typed into a page is true the day it is typed*. It
is gone. The copy ratchet's two baseline rows for the retired files are removed, so the ratchet
only shrinks.

### The kit table had no room for the column it exists for

The table has four fixed columns: group 132, binding 74, source 190 and faults 52 px. Beside the
ladder pane at a laptop's width, the slot column collapsed to one character ("a…"). The same
layout was on the base, where the Kit surface's side pane was `PANES.kit` at its 340 px default.

Two changes answer it:

- the table keeps a 640 px floor and scrolls sideways;
- on the kit section the relations panel steps aside.

`components/SlotRow.jsx` was not touched.

### Every count on the dossier reconciles with the payload it came from

Measured on `tidewater-georgian` against the API:

| section | on the strip | the payload beside the section |
|---|---|---|
| Kit | 94 | `/api/kit/tidewater-georgian?only_specified=true`: 94 slots of 97 |
| Proportions | 35 | the packs payload: 4 bound + 4 opted in + 26 delivered + 1 withheld + 0 declined |

`north-american` lists 9 members and 85 buildable styles, which are the dossier payload's own
lists. The walk holds each of these to the API rather than to a literal.

## III. What was deliberately not done, and what was done outside the listed files

- **`Chrome.jsx`: one line removed, the rail's "The Kit" item.** 14.13 owns this file.
  - With `kit` gone from `SURFACE_PATHS`, the item's click writes `#/style`.
  - The walk's rail-reachability loop then fails it: its address is the one the Style Record
    item reaches, and no rail item can be marked current for a surface that no longer exists.
  - The removal is the smallest edit that keeps the walk honest. 14.13 should know it is there.
- **`e2e/walk.mjs`: one line outside the listed blocks.** In the filter round trip, `visit('#/kit')`
  became `visit('#/style')`. `visit` refuses an address the router does not WRITE, and it no
  longer writes `#/kit`, so that line would have failed on this tree alone. No `engineClaim`-pinned
  line was touched.
- **`Overview.jsx` is not touched.** Its "The Kit" door (`nav.go('kit')`) goes through
  `formatHash`'s alias and lands on the Styles index. The door is still labelled "The Kit", which
  names a place that is now a section. That wording is the front door's (14.14).
- **`EdgeGlyph.jsx`, `Phylogeny.jsx`, `FaultCard.jsx`, `Proportions.jsx`, the journey, the brief
  intake, the candidate set and `session.js` are not touched.** The lineage section's `EdgeGlyph`
  call is byte-identical to `StyleRecord`'s, at `workbench/app/src/dossier/Lineage.jsx:38`, so
  14.11's reconciliation has one line to find.
- **Two things were left as they were extracted:**
  - `KitSurface`'s slot-detail fetch still builds `/api/kit/${styleId}/slot/${openSlot}` without
    `api/client.js`'s `seg()` encoding;
  - `SlotRow`'s group column is set in `--ink-4`.
  Neither is new in this package. Each is one line for whoever owns those components.
- **No new open question.** Every finding above was fixed by construction, or belongs to a package
  already named.

# WP-14.13: every page says where it is

*25 September 2026. Branch `wp-14.13`, taken from `0ad8b49`. Scope: PRD
`docs/prd/phase-14-the-dossier-and-the-journey.md` §F (the site map, the crumbs, the title and
§F.4's rule), §I.11 (the assistant's name and the narrow fold), §I.12 (the shell's other
attachments) and the rows of §J that name this package. Every figure below was measured in this
worktree or in a control worktree of `0ad8b49` made for the purpose. Cite this report by
filename: the number names two packages in two other phases already, and may in this one.*

## What it is for

A reader of the workbench could not tell where they were. The tab title never changed. The
masthead's only persistent content was the bench plan's id and style as bare text. The rail was
a card catalogue of eleven surfaces in the order they were built, each labelled in words
`Chrome.jsx` wrote. The palette's `short` lines and the Overview's DOORS labelled the same places
in different words again. A link handed to a colleague opened on a slot's figures inside a kit
inside a style inside a family, with nothing on screen naming the product or the way back.

This package makes each page say three things: where it is (the crumbs and the title), what it is
(its page head, from its own glossary record), and what comes next (the rail as a site map with
the house's steps numbered). It does all of that from records and the API, and writes no word or
figure of its own.

## I. What was built

**`nav/navModel.js`, the site map.** It is one pure module and holds ids only. `NAV` is §F.1's
frozen table: four groups (start, styles, a house, the library) and thirteen items, each naming
its surface and the glossary record it is called by. `navModel({lookup, counts, glossaryCount,
inHand, dossier, journey, place})` returns §F.2's shape exactly.

- Every label is that record's `term`. A record the glossary lacks comes back as `label: null`
  and `missing: <id>`, and the rail prints `noEntry(id)`.
- Every meta is the API's figure or a journey word. The count beside Find a style is
  `counts.styles`. Beside Proportions it is the sum of `counts.proportion_packs`, beside Faults
  `counts.faults`, and beside the Glossary the glossary's own `count`. Beside a house step it is
  `journey/journey.js`'s words for that step, and beside a dossier section it is that section's
  `count`. A test fails if any numeric literal in the module's code is other than 0 or 1.
- Step numbers are `JOURNEY`'s index plus one. Trace a drawing is not a step and has none.
- The style in hand is a link to that style's dossier. Its sections are listed only while that
  very dossier is the page shown. At most one item is current, and it is the one §F.2 names.
- The four §F.2 exports (`NAV`, `navModel`, `headTermFor`, `guidedExampleStyle`) keep exactly
  §F.2's signatures, as the lead asked. Six helpers are added beside them: `wordFor`,
  `normalizePlace`, `sectionOf`, `stylePlaceKind`, `inHandFrom` and `flatItems`. They are
  additive, and the rail, the crumbs, the palette and the walk read them rather than keeping
  their own copies.

**`nav/crumbs.js`, the trail and the title.** `crumbsFor(place, {lookup, dossier, names,
styles, journey})` returns §F.3's table, place by place.

- The style chain follows `member_of` and never lineage. It is taken from the served
  `dossier.chain` when that dossier is this style's. Before the dossier arrives, it is
  `chainOf(styleId, styles)` over `/api/styles`, whose `in` field is the same fact. So the trail
  is whole on the first render, and not only once the dossier fetch answers.
- `titleFor(crumbs, lookup)` reads the non-group labels backwards, joins them with ` · `, and
  appends ` — ` and the `about-tdl` term. On the front door it is that term alone.
- The PRD's own example is held to `styles/*.json` and `elements/slots.json` by a test: *Styles ›
  North American › American Colonial › Georgian Colonial American › Tidewater Georgian › Kit ›
  Main cornice*.

**The shell** (`Chrome.jsx`, `App.jsx`):

- **The rail.** Items are `<a data-nav href aria-current>` inside the existing
  `nav[aria-label="surfaces"]`. Each carries `useTermDescription(termId)` and the step number or
  meta from the model. The shell writes no label.
- **`CrumbStrip`.** A `<nav aria-label="crumbs"><ol>` between the masthead and the pane row, with
  the last item marked `aria-current="page"`.
- **`PageHead`.** WP-14.8's component is mounted once, in the centre column above `<main>`, with
  `headTermFor(place)`. The Glossary surface's own mount is removed, so no page has two heads.
- **The masthead.**
  - The wordmark is a link to `#/`.
  - The "the workbench" eyebrow and the inert Export and Settings icons are gone.
  - "On the bench: *name*" links to `#/workbench`. Beside it are fatal, serious and unjudged as
    three counts, each word a `Term`, and a count the check did not state reads "not counted" and
    never 0. Before a plan is evaluated it says so instead.
  - A "Keys ?" button opens the existing `ShortcutCard`.
  - The tab title is `titleFor`'s, and `index.html`'s static title is the product's name.
- **`lastEval` belongs to its plan.** It clears when `plan.id` changes, unless the evaluation in
  hand is already of that plan. The bench loads a revised record and sets its evaluation in the
  same tick, and a blanket clear would have thrown that evaluation away.
- **A dead server is said, not blanked.** A failed `/api/health` renders
  `role="status" data-boot-status` with "Cannot reach the server · Retry".
- **`data-reflow`** is set on `#root` for the Overview and the Glossary, which releases WP-14.8's
  1380 px floor on those two pages only.
- **`ColdLinkBanner`** shows once per browser, when the first place loaded is not the front door
  and `prefs.seen['front-door']` is unset. It shows:
  - the `about-tdl` term and definition;
  - the crumbs;
  - a Front door link and a Guided example link, each named by its record;
  - a Dismiss button.

  Dismissing it, or visiting the front door, sets the flag.

**The assistant** (§I.11):

- **Pane head.** `AiRail` takes a `title`. `RailHost.assistantName(glossary)` passes the
  `assistant` record's term, or `PANES.rail.label` while the glossary loads, or
  `noEntry('assistant')` if the glossary answered without the record.
- **Folded spine.** The spine carries the same name, and its label stays `PANES.rail.label`.
- **Greeting and off-state copy** now say it is an AI assistant. `rail.py` and what it is sent
  are untouched.
- **The narrow fold.** `state/layout.js` exports `NARROW_FOLD_PX = 1500`. `readStored` records a
  stored choice exactly as §I.11 defines one: an own boolean `collapsed.rail`. The store's first
  `clampAll` folds the assistant below that width when there is no choice, and publishes the fold
  with `save = false`. No later resize folds or unfolds anything.

**The palette and search:**

- **Options.** Each carries `data-id` and, for a record, `data-cite`. The opening hand is the site
  map's order, front door first. `KIND_LABEL.term` is "glossary terms", and `kit` is gone from
  `KIND_ORDER`, which gains `term` last.
- **Surface entries.** `staticEntries(lookup)` derives the surface entries from `NAV`. Each entry
  is named by its record's `term`, or shows its id where the record is missing. The kept synonym
  haystacks go with each entry, and the kit's (`elements`, `bindings`) are now the style entry's.
  The app-written `short` lines are gone.

**The copy:**

- **Three stale fault counts.** "209 faults" appeared three times against a corpus of 210. The
  Fault Corpus now reads the length of its own list, and Export reads `counts.faults`. Export's
  download filename is kept.
- **Quiet eyebrows.** Four quiet eyebrows in `RevisionPanel` take the default tone.
- **The copy ratchet loses six rows** and gains none: the masthead's unjudged tooltip, the rail's
  "9 sections" and "4 formats", and the three "209 faults".
- **`match.js`.** Its comment no longer pins 665. The index this tree serves holds 762 entries,
  measured through `corpus.search_index()`.

## IV. Findings

**1. The shell's page head broke the walk at check 90, and 120-odd checks behind it never
ran.** The first walk on this tree died on `waitForSelector('text=compressed')` on the
Phylogeny. The head now sits above `<main>` on every place. After the first visit it folds
its "How to read this page" list, and that list is kept in the DOM with `hidden`. On the
Phylogeny one hidden line mentions the compressed axis, it is first in DOM order, and a global
`text=` wait resolves to the first match. The wait timed out on a hidden `<li>`. Being a
top-level wait with no `try`, it took down every check after it. That is WP-13.9's finding about
a top-level wait, met again.

The damage was swept rather than assumed. Of the walk's nine global `text=` waits, a page head
can answer exactly two:

- `compressed`, on the Phylogeny;
- `dishonest`, on the Fault Corpus, where the wait is soft and would only have waited fifteen
  seconds in silence.

In both, the word is only in the hidden list. The sweep read every `glossary/surface-*.json`'s
term, lede, read list and try, case-insensitively, as Playwright's unquoted `text=` matches.
Both waits are `main >> text=` now, which is what each was written to prove. The other seven
cannot collide today. **They will collide the day a surface record's read list takes one of
their words**, and four of them are in blocks other packages own. Scoping every walk wait to
`main` is the durable fix. It is named here rather than done across other packages' blocks.

**2. Any layout stored before this package reads as a choice to keep the assistant open.** This
follows from the ruled rule and is not a defect in it. `persist` writes the whole layout object,
so every browser that ever dragged or folded any pane has `collapsed.rail: false` stored. Under
§I.11 that is a stored choice. The narrow fold therefore reaches only a browser with nothing
stored: a new reader, which is the reader the ruling was for. An existing reader on a 1280 px
laptop keeps an open assistant they never chose. The PRD states the whole-object write in its own
words ("that is a stored choice from then on"). The part it does not say is that the rule
applies to layouts written before the rule existed. It is left as ruled.

**3. The style in hand is always the guided example on this tree.** The shell reads
`prefs.styleInHand` and never writes it. The write belongs to the dossier (WP-14.12's
`StyleDossier`), which is not on this branch. So until the two merge, the in-hand item is the
guided example's style, correctly labelled as such. That is the behaviour §F.2 specifies for
"prefs holds none".

**4. `lastEval` outlived its plan.** Loading a different plan left the previous plan's fatal,
serious and unjudged counts in the masthead, beside the new plan's name. This was found by
reading, and fixed as above. The obvious fix, clearing on every plan change, would have discarded
the evaluation the bench sets in the same tick as a revised record.

**5. A failed `/api/health` rendered nothing.** `locked` stayed `null` for ever, so a stranger
handed the URL during a slow boot saw a blank page. The walk now aborts `/api/health` in a fresh
context and asserts the status line and its Retry.

**6. The two readings of "the fault count" had drifted a third way.** The copy said 209 in three
places, the corpus holds 210, and the Fault Corpus's own list is the population it is about. Each
now reads its own source. No number is written in its place.

**7. My own screenshot harness waited on a selector that carries two states.**
`[data-bench-counts]` holds the three counts or the words "not yet evaluated". A wait on it
therefore resolved before the evaluate answered, and the first bench screenshots showed the
unevaluated masthead. The harness waits for `[data-count]` now. It is the selector class this
repository keeps meeting, met in a harness that is not a check.

## V. Deviations from the brief and the PRD

- **`walk.mjs` outside the permitted blocks, three times.**
  - The two scoped waits in Finding 1 are in the Phylogeny and Fault Corpus blocks. They were
    needed because this package's head caused the crash.
  - The rail-reachability loop is WP-14.7's §J.1 loop, not one of the four blocks named for this
    package. It was re-cut because the rail's items are anchors now.
    - It judges each item by its `data-nav` id and its own `href`, not its position.
    - It allows `in-hand:identify` as the current mark on the in-hand style's own dossier.
    - Its pre-visit, which moves off a current item, never lands on `style` or `kit`, because
      that would mark a different item current.
  - None of the three touches an `engineClaim`-pinned line or the full-screen `goto('#/kit')`.
- **`layout.test.mjs`'s "a narrow window borrows" states an open assistant.** Its fixture is
  seeded with `{collapsed: {rail: false}}`. Otherwise the narrow fold, a different property with
  its own cases, would fold the pane that test measures. Its assertions are unchanged.
- **`glossary.test.mjs`'s premise is re-cut.** It asserted that `Glossary.jsx` ships a literal
  `<PageHead termId>`. That mount moved into the shell, where the id is a variable, so the
  premise now reads `RailHost.jsx`'s `assistant` record. That is the one literal id the shell's
  own files ask the glossary for.
- **`search-unit.mjs`'s `elements`/`bindings` synonyms now point at `style`.** §J.1 gives that row
  to WP-14.12. It had to move here because the site map has no `kit` item, so the old assertion
  had nothing to find. The change is the same as the one 14.12 specifies.
- **`useStyles` maps `member_of` from `/api/styles`' `in`.** `crumbsFor` takes `styles` beside
  §F.3's four options, so the trail is whole before the dossier arrives. Both changes are
  additive.
- **`normalizePlace` reads the legacy `kit` surface as the kit section of that style's dossier.**
  Until §E.1's retirement lands, the rail, the crumbs and the head then agree with the address the
  reader is sent to. Once WP-14.12 makes the router answer `style` for every kit address, this
  branch is dead and may go.
- **The chrome writes three bits of furniture that are not records.** They are "Dismiss", "You
  are at:" and "Cannot reach the server · Retry". The last is the PRD's own wording. None of the
  three defines anything, and each is named as furniture in its component's header.

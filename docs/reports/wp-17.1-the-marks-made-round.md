*Unjudged is a form, not a colour*.

## I. What was built

**The judgment marks are discs and rings** (`marks.js` `MARK_FORMS`):
- passed and failed: a filled disc;
- unjudged: an open ring hatched at 45°, the same size;
- yours to judge: an open gilt ring;
- refused: a heavy brick ring.

These were squares until now. Not applicable stays an em rule, and loading stays its word.

**Three journey marks**, each a `--mark-*` duty token with exactly one glossary record:
- `--mark-journey-done`, a vellum disc;
- `--mark-journey-current`, a gilt ring round a gilt dot;
- `--mark-journey-not-yet`, a dashed ring.

The records are `mark-journey-done`, `mark-journey-current` and `mark-journey-not-yet`. Their
basis quotes the handoff note and the token comments. None borrows a judgment's form, which
`marks.test.mjs` already holds.

**On the instrument** the unjudged ring is edged in `--ink-4`, the gilt ring takes `--gilt`, and
the loading word and the not-applicable rule take `--ink-4`. Each is restored on vellum. The
ground test reads all four.

**Four ladders in one leaf, `chips.js`** (source, binding, variant, severity), drawn by

## II. What was found

1. **A word cannot sit in paper on the board's strong fills.**
   - `--paper` reads 3.78 : 1 on `--green-deep` and 3.50 on `--brick`, and `--coal` 3.94 on
     `--brick`.
   - So where the board fills a chip in green-deep, green or brick, the pigment is a **swatch**
     beside the word, and the word is in a text duty.
   - Where the fill is pale (measured, editorial, derived, a specified binding), the fill is the
     chip's own ground and the word is coal, 11.8 : 1 or better.
   - Every chip therefore reads on both grounds. `chips.test.mjs` computes it.
2. **"Derived: pale green-grey" names water's pigment.**
   - The palette's own green-grey is `--blue-pale`, whose duty is water.
   - Derived takes the mat with a green-deep edge instead. This is a judgment and is named here.
3. **The handoff note moved, from `docs/design/` to `docs/`.**
   - A glossary basis may quote only top-level `docs/*.md` (`check_openings.GLOSSARY_REC_RE`), and
     the journey marks' records quote the note.
   - Every reference was updated in the same commit.
4. **The faint-ink scanner read the chip table's `edge` key as possible text.** Its owner rules
   know `border` and not `edge`. The key is `border` now. A table entry named for a line is how a
   faint ink would reach text, and the scanner was right to ask.

# WP-13.3 — The prover learns the type, and the elevation draws the plan

*Phase 13, "the coherent sheet". Cite by filename (OQ 90). Two slices, built in isolated
worktrees from `a9f7f77` and merged as `b2bb872` (one bay system) and `bc48e5d` (the prover's
slice); every figure below names the tree, the engine and the load it was taken under. CP-SAT
under a wall-clock budget is not deterministic on this machine — the reference plan produced three
different placements in one afternoon on one tree — so no CP figure is ratcheted and the
deterministic ones are the heuristic's.*

## What this package is

Lucas's 15 Sep ruling: the type's facts become HARD on the prover in a stated precedence, each
downgrade named in the conflict set. Before this package `geometry_cp.py`'s hard set was
no-overlap, containment, a 97% coverage floor, declared walls, doors, sizes, the entrance and the
shape band; stacking was a 40-point soft term the polish never reached at 40 s, tiling was a
floor with a 3% hole in it, bearing continuity and the hearth were absent, and the elevation
divided the outside face evenly and never read a placed window. The gate
(`tests/test_sheet_coherence.py`, `docs/reports/wp-13.1-the-gate.md`) was red on every row that
reads a placement.

## What was built

### The prover's slice (`build/geometry_cp.py`, `build/typefacts.py`, `build/geometry.py`)

- **A seven-rank ladder.** `_RANK = ("wall", "axis", "tiling", "stack", "bearing", "hearth",
  "shape")`, lowest authority first. Every kind is an assumption literal with a typed key; a
  conflict core releases a whole rank; `_reinstate` offers released pins back in two sliced
  phases (every kind whole, then a pin at a time, highest rank first), each inside its own slice
  of `BUDGET_SHARE_REINSTATE`. A released fact keeps the search's own charge in the objective
  (14 for a wall or a hearth wall, `STACK_W` for a stack, `SPAN_W` for a span).
- **The four facts in `_build`.** TILING as a downgradable literal at `TILING = 1.0` above the
  never-released `COVERAGE = 0.97`; DECLARED STACKS as `stacking.lands`' own relation transcribed
  (`_lands_literal`, fraction read off `stacking.LANDS_FRACTION`, one product per claim, domains
  derived from the building frame); BEARING as continuity plus capacity, capacity read the way
  `structure.span_check` decides it; the HEARTH pinned to the face `hearths.flue_walls` puts a
  flue on, ranked above the wall, unjudged by name where the massing cannot be read.
  `solver.facts` is the model's own account — held / downgraded / unjudged per kind.
- **The verifier.** `typefacts.report(plan)` judges the same four on the PLACED record for either
  engine, from `geometry._disclose`, onto `geometry_report.type_facts`, with `status` per fact;
  unjudged is never held.
- **The budget allocation.** `BUDGET_SHARE_FEASIBILITY / _REINSTATE / _POLISH` in `geometry.py`,
  read by `solve_cp` alone; a phase A still UNKNOWN at its share is UNSOLVED; the polish is
  guaranteed its share and hinted first by phase A's own placement; among candidates carrying an
  objective the CP OBJECTIVE ranks them (`_score` is disclosure only). `BUDGET_BATCH_S` stays
  at 40 (see the sweep).
- **The shape pin to the hundredth.** `RATIO_SCALE = 100`: `int(round(1.35 * 10))` is 14, which
  is how `chamber3` was drawn at exactly 1.40 with its pin HELD — main's pre-existing red in
  `test_shape_pins.py`.

### One bay system (`build/elevation.py`, `build/facade.py`, `build/render_elevation.py`)

- The face's openings are the plan's PLACED openings on that wall, read through
  `render_plan.openings_of_level` (the reader the plan sheet, the DXF and the gate share),
  converted from the clear frame to the face's outside datum by the exterior wall thickness,
  one rectangle per placed opening at its own centre and width, on its own storey. A bay the plan
  leaves empty is empty; a refused window reaches `refused` in the placer's words; a placed
  window a stack stands on is refused by name.
- One datum: `_face_bays` takes `facade.rhythm()`'s centres shifted by the wall (bay 1 at 4.5 ft
  clear / 5.792 outside at 9.0 ft pitch, against 4.684 at 9.369). The rhythm is kept for the
  readers that need one and drawn as nothing.
- The three storey-alignment measurements were constants derived from the rhythm; measured now,
  tolerance READ from `faults/storeys-out-of-vertical-alignment.json`. `upper_floor_opening_count`
  is withheld at zero with its reason (a parity test and a division carry no `applies_when`).
- N and W faces read with the plan's axis, unmirrored (`FACE_MIRRORED` is the one switch): the
  scene, the roof's stack axes and the DXF all lay every face out unmirrored, and a mirrored
  draft put the spec Colonial's door 44.0 ft off in the Round.

## What was found

Numbered so a later audit can name a row. The two slices' own findings first, then what the
lead's verification of the merged tree added.

1. **The shape pin was stated to a TENTH, and that is main's pre-existing red.** The band was
   transcribed as `int(round(_ceil * 10))` and Python rounds 13.5 to 14, so a bedroom's own 1.35
   ceiling reached the model as 1.4 and `chamber3` was drawn 21 × 15 — exactly 1.40 — with its pin
   HELD, which is `tests/test_shape_pins.py`'s intermittent red on every solve that landed there
   (`oq/a-held-shape-pin-is-not-held-on-the-hard-only-path`). `_absorb` was the obvious suspect
   and its `_fits` refuses 21 × 15 at 1.35; the grower was the model's own rounding.
   `RATIO_SCALE = 100`, and the test reads the property rather than a literal.

# WP-14.1 — the ink read back

*Phase 14, the ink held to its plates. Built 27 September 2026 in three commits (`19418df`,
`3f41183`, `0fc2735`), before any drawing was changed.*

Lucas asked whether the drawn trim, casings, column and entablature profiles, and every other
drawn figure, reflect the plates and dimensions researched to source them, on every surface that
generates SVG. This package builds the instrument that answers the question and takes the first
reading. It fixes nothing. The fixes are WP-14.2 to WP-14.4's, each landing with the guard that
proves it, and a census that moved before the baseline was taken could not tell them what they
changed.

## I. What was built

**`tests/inkread.py`, a reader of the SVG a reader is served.** Standard library only. It parses
the whole path grammar: relative and implicit commands, compact numbers, arc flags written with no
separator, and smooth-curve reflection. It composes transforms and resolves the computed style
cascade by specificity and source order, with inheritance. It samples, fits circles, and reads a
plate's `data-frame`. `arc_centre()` is now the one independent copy of W3C F.6.5: the copies in
`test_drawn_geometry.py` and `test_sheet_coherence.py` import it. It was proved equal on a seeded
sweep of 10,000 arcs, and the door-arc gate rows pass 4 of 4 on the parent commit and on this one.
Twenty-seven tests hold the reader to values worked by hand, including the trap a single-class
matcher cannot see (`.mt` written after `.w-med` wins on an element carrying both, in whichever
order the element lists them).

**Every plate states what its pixels mean.**

- The 73 profile plates gained a `data-frame`: px per inch, the naked, and the datum each
  assembly was drawn on. The plates changed by that attribute and nothing else. The committed
  files reproduce their 73 manifest digests, and each fresh render with the attribute removed
  equals its committed file byte for byte; only then were the digests moved.
- The elevation's eave inset is a second plate in the elevation's frame, in inches at its own
  scale. It is appended after the face plate, so the Round's `frameOf` still finds the face by its
  id. All 64 elevation sheets are byte-identical to the parent's without the attribute.
- The orders page's SVG states its frame too.

**The two JavaScript surfaces are read without a browser.**

- `tests/js/orders_ink.mjs` runs the COMMITTED `dist/orders.html`'s own three scripts in `node:vm`
  against a stub DOM, for every order pack at 12, 24 and 36 in, pedestal on and off.
- The Proportions plate's arithmetic was lifted out of `Proportions.jsx` into
  `workbench/app/src/proportions/plate.js`, a leaf with no imports. The lift was proved to change
  nothing: every order pack at 6, 12, 24 and 36 in, 104 plates, rendered to static markup before
  and after, and 104 of 104 are byte-identical. `tests/js/proportions_plate.mjs` runs that module
  on exactly what `corpus.proportions_with_members` serves.
- `workbench/app/src/proportions.test.mjs` holds the properties that stay true whatever is fixed,
  on three fixtures trimmed from what the server serves (their generator's `--check` keeps them
  current). The app suite goes from 221 to 238.

**`dist/orders.html` is rebuilt, not remembered.** `build/render_orders.py` computed at import
time and wrote as a side effect, and nothing ran it: the committed page carried three system packs
that had since moved. It is `build_page()` and `main()` now, proved to emit what the old script
emitted. `build.py` regenerates it on every build, as it does `taxonomy.html`.

**`tests/svg_census.py`, the census.** It has a registry of every file that emits SVG, and
`test_svg_census.py` fails on one the registry does not name. It has forty checks, each answering
agrees, disagrees, or could not evaluate, with its population printed. `docs/fidelity.md` is the
layer doc and carries the generated tables. The building sheets are drawn on the deterministic
engine, because a census on a wall-clock solver would move between runs on one tree. Every node
with a kit is also drawn on the Tidewater placement with its style swapped (159 elevations in
10 s). The whole census runs in about 35 s.

**The model draws its record wherever the record has a figure.** Every member boundary is drawn
where its record puts it (P5, 73 of 73). Every published face that sits at mid-height is drawn to
its record (P6, 68 judged, 0 wrong). The orders page's section is the height the engine dimensions
and reaches exactly as far as Python's geometry (O2, O3, 150 of 150). The eave inset draws every
cornice member at its recorded height and face (V13).

**The defects are in the words, the absent figures, the stack, and the frame.**

- **Words that contradict the ink.** 12 Palladio plates print "at a 24 in column" and are drawn at
  12 in (P1). 5 plates draw no relief and print "1.00 in of relief" (P2). 10 capital plates call a
  volute or acanthus "not constructed" and ink a swelling for it (P8).
- **Absent figures drawn as measured.** 11 plates hold members whose record gives no projection,
  and none says so (P7). 6 base plates cut a torus or scotia up to 0.75 in into their own naked
  (P3), and on 2 the ink leaves the viewBox (P4). 65 plates hold medium- or low-confidence members
  and mark none (P10). 9 curved members on 7 plates are drawn straight, unsaid (P11).
- **The stack.** 5 packs draw a column other than the one they state (E1; Benjamin's Corinthian
  120 in against 132). 4 draw an entablature that is not their own (E2). 3 stack Benjamin's
  subplinth on a pedestal, where his own notes offer it instead of one (E3: "on nothing, on a
  pedestal, or on a SUBPLINTH"; "14 diameters = column 11 + entablature 2 + subplinth 1").
  Palladio's Ionic pedestal die is drawn at an invented 1.2 x R (E4).
- **The Proportions plate's frame.** On 12 of 25 plates the ink reaches past the frame (R1;
  vignola-ionic's frame is 11.67 in and its ink 15.33 in). 17 miscount the members "stating no
  projection at all", in both directions (R2). On 14 the caption states the pack's datum while
  assemblies are drawn on the other one (R3). All three have one cause: the plate reads the datum
  at pack level, and the ink is Python's per-group reading (OQ 78).
- **The orders page.** Its flute lines are not the flutes (O6, 20 of 20). Eight lines are drawn at
  even fractions of the radius, where 24 flutes project to six at R sin(theta). An invariant the
  engine cannot judge is printed FAIL (O4, driven, since all 169 hold today). greek-doric and
  moorish-arch can be selected by no button (O5).
- **The elevation.**
  - The weight ladder is overridden on all 44 sheets (V1), because the rung rules are written
    before the class rules.
  - 79 of 159 styles draw something their own resolved kit forbids (V2).
  - 26 styles make a transom or fanlight canonical and none is drawn (V3).
  - On 24 sheets the shutter leaves are 60-80% of what sash-light's own rule gives (V4). The rule
    is "(opening - 1)/2, so that the pair actually covers the window when closed. This is the
    single most-violated rule in traditional building."
  - The lights are counted for the pack's width, not for the width drawn (V5, 27 sheets).
  - Three plans draw a 192 in garage door as a panelled leaf (V6).
  - Every elevation publishes two figures its own rules mark judgment (V8).
  - Every sill and head on the Tidewater sheets misses the brick courses drawn behind it, unsaid
    (V11).
- **The section, the roof, the DXF and the plan.**
  - All 16 sections draw the exterior walls as lines of no thickness, against a stated 8 or
    15.5 in (S1).
  - The roof plan draws the Tidewater stacks 11 in across, against a stated 22 (F1).
  - The DXF omits the sidelights the SVG draws, on 5 plans (X1).
  - 15 of 16 plans name no parti, so the placer writes a 10.0 ft bay module. The working sheet
    draws and labels that grid, and convicts cuts off it, without saying the module is a default
    (PL1).

**What planning suspected and the ink does not bear out.**

- No arched head departs from a circle by more than half a print quantum (V7, 162 sheets). The
  quadratic heads are indistinguishable from the circular segment at the scale drawn, so that item
  leaves WP-14.3's list rather than being "fixed" for nothing.
- The keystone and chimney fallbacks are real code and are reached by no record today (V9, 163
  sheets).
- The Vignola-line Ionic capital's "missing volute" is not missing. Its record carries the
  volute's channel as `volute_gorge`, "the face of the volute channel", which is what a section at
  the axis cuts.

## III. What the instrument got wrong, which is why it is tested

- **The band tolerance was tighter than the print.** Member bands were read at 1e-6 in while the
  plate prints to a thousandth of a pixel (5.7e-6 in at 87 px/in). The first run called 30 plates'
  ovolos "drawn straight" (7 is right) and passed all 10 swellings (all 10 are real). It is 0.001
  in, and every plate read asserts the print quantum sits two orders under it.
- **The emitter scan was blind to a component.** It matched `<svg` alone and could not see a
  component that returns a `<path>` inside its parent's `<svg>`. It now matches a primitive
  followed by an attribute, and a carrier by `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`. That found
  `RoundPlate.jsx`.
- **Holding ids alone was blind.** Corrupting V1's own rung table, and making V5 count the wrong
  muntins, both left the build green: every V1 and V5 row already disagreed, so a change in what a
  row reported could not be seen. The pin maps each id to its figures now, and a moved figure
  fails until a commit re-pins it and says why.
- **Three first readings were wrong.**
  - S1 counted the roof's eave-to-ridge line as a wall body.
  - PL1 first read the presentation register, which draws no grid. It then took "ASSUMED
    PLATFORM-FRAME", a sentence about the walls, as a disclosure about the bay module.

  Nothing in the census reads a sheet's whole text for a word any more.

Every check with no live disagreement is driven with its defect planted in memory, beside a
control showing the unplanted input agrees:

- P5, P6, P9 and P12, on a real plate.
- O1, with a page committed without a build; O2 and O3, with a section at a scale it does not
  state.
- V7, with a head raised into a tall parabola; V9, with a record stripped of its stack size (the
  renderer then draws its own 22 in); V13, with an inset frame at the wrong scale.

Without `node`, every JavaScript row reports COULD NOT EVALUATE and names `node`. Twenty-six
mutations were run in scratch copies of the tree, each asserted to have landed and each restored.
Two were blind at first, and the pinning was changed until they were not:

- **Six on the instrument:** an inverted arc flag, a reversed transform order, a reversed
  cascade, the frame's y-flip dropped, the band tolerance under the print quantum, and the band
  read at float precision.
- **Twenty on the census:** a registry row deleted, the renderer's frame stripped, the scan
  narrowed, P6 blind to the datum, P1 reading the wrong figure, and fifteen on the JavaScript and
  building-sheet rows. Four of the twenty plant a FIX in a renderer rather than a defect (flutes at
  their projection, the rung rules reordered, stacks at their stated size, a widened frame), to
  prove a row can say agrees.

## IV. Two guards the lift would have left behind

- `test_grammar_agreement.py` reads `Proportions.jsx` for re-derived arc arithmetic. That code
  moved to `plate.js`, so the guard would have gone on passing over the file it protects. It reads
  `plate.js` too, and a planted sweep there turns it red.
- `test_modcache.py`'s list of tolerated by-path loaders named `render_orders.py`. It loads
  through `modcache` now, so its entry is gone and a new loader there would fail.

## V. What was deliberately not done

- **No drawing was changed**, beyond the frames that let one be read.
- **The bench sheet (`Sheet.jsx`) has no census rows yet.** WP-14.4 lifts its marks into a pure
  module first, on the plan's own sequencing, and its rows come with that lift.
- **The browser walk's surface-⑩ checks** still read 3 of 26 packs and count the gutter as frame.
  They are WP-14.2's, alongside the frame fix, R1.
- **No plate was read.** Every host the packs cite refused a CONNECT on 26 and 27 Sep 2026. The
  source leg (link 1) is WP-14.5's internal checks and a list for a person to fetch.
- **Decision 4 was not acted on.** The census found that most of what the elevation is forbidden
  to draw is an ancestor's ban, and that one of those bans contradicts the note of the node it
  lands on. That changes what the ruling was taken on, so it is put back to Lucas rather than
  executed: `oq/an-inherited-ban-decides-what-the-elevation-may-draw`.

## VI. Open questions raised

- `oq/an-inherited-ban-decides-what-the-elevation-may-draw`: of 79 styles whose elevation draws

# WP-13.1 — the procedure nobody read, and the verdict that did not carry what the corpus knows

*15 September 2026. Raised by Lucas: "do the faults.detection package next."*

---

## I. What was found before anything was built

`detection` is required on every one of the 210 records in `faults/`. It is **139,070
characters** — nearly ten times the 14,154 of `daylight.orientation`, the field whose zero readers
WP-11.9 closed — and the schema says what it is for: *"How to spot it fast, from the street or from a photograph. Written for both a
person and a model looking at an image."*

**Nothing read one of them.** Every `detection` hit in the tree's `.py`, `.js`, `.jsx` and `.mjs`
is a different sense of the word: datum detection, gap detection, a guard's detection. That is the
shape of `daylight.orientation`'s zero readers (WP-11.9), at ten times the size.

**It is not the largest prose field here, and the first draft of this report said it was.**
`correct_practice` is 191,878 characters over the same 210 records. It is not the same defect —
`tests/test_facade.py` reads it — but the claim was wrong, and it is the third figure in this
package corrected by re-deriving rather than by re-reading: see §IV.2 and §V.

The measured state of the corpus this prose was written for:

| | `tidewater-georgian-careful` | `spec-builder-colonial` |
|---|---|---|
| faults present | 19 | 24 |
| faults clear | 65 | 56 |
| **COULD NOT EVALUATE** | **120** | **126** |
| not applicable | 2 | 1 |

120 of 210 unjudged on this corpus's own most carefully authored plan, naming **299 distinct
missing measurements**, and the verdict says nothing else about any of them.

## II. The finding, in one sentence

**Three records already exist about every one of those 299 names and none of them reaches the
reader**: the fault's own `detection` prose, which is the corpus's written account of how to
obtain the number; the surfaces its tests declare; and — for 23 of the 299 — a refusal taken
deliberately, built, measured and written down.

So a reader is told

> `plant-room-that-was-never-drawn`: could not evaluate — needs `dedicated_plant_room_area_sqft`

about a name WP-9.1 derived, swept, found convicted **both** reference plans, and withdrew with a
forty-line reason ending *"to take this name off the list, give the plan schema a way to say
'services considered, none enclosed'"*. An unjudged whose reason is recorded elsewhere and never
quoted reads exactly like a gap nobody has looked at.

## III. What was built

### `build/detection.py` — the one reader

It supplies no measurement and copies no refusal. `refusals()` reads `arrangement.NOT_DERIVABLE`
and `elevation.NOT_MODELLED` **live**, so a reason corrected there is corrected here and a name
added there is refused here with no edit; a test proves it by moving a table at runtime. Two
spellings of one refusal is the defect `check_addresses.py` exists for.

- `checks(fault)` splits the procedure into the checks its own prose numbers — **123 of 210**
  records number theirs in one of the two ways this reader knows, and the other 87 come back
  whole, which is the honest answer and not a claim that they have one check.
- `surfaces(fault)` reads `measurable_from` over all three test locations — `test`,
  `secondary_tests` and `exceptions[].bounds_test`, the third of which SUBSTITUTES for the primary.
  An empty list is a third state and not a photograph.
- `disposition(name)` returns `refused` (with the module and the reason **verbatim**) or
  `unsupplied`. There is deliberately no third verdict: whether an unsupplied name is an omission
  or an impossibility cannot be decided from the record, and saying more would be inventing one.

### The unjudged verdict carries it

`plan_check`'s `fault_unjudged` rows gain `refused` and `refused_all`. Strictly additive: a row
with nothing refused is returned byte-identical, and no row loses a field. **Thirteen rows are
annotated on the Tidewater plan and eight of them are wholly accounted for** — `duct-soffit-through-the-formal-room`,
`hearth-on-an-interior-wall`, `one-trim-grade-for-every-room`, `plant-room-that-was-never-drawn`,
`powder-room-on-the-dining-room`, `raking-cornice-that-does-not-match`, `stack-with-nowhere-to-land`,
`vestigial-chimney-chase`. Every measurement those eight faults want has already been considered
and declined, and until now the reader had no way to know.

**The prose itself is NOT attached.** 120 rows at a median 639 characters is 77 KB on a result that
measures 110 KB entire, on the one route the infrastructure audit measured as the whole server's
bound. The fault record is served whole by the corpus API and by `tdl_fault`; what is small and
available nowhere else is the disposition. Measured: 110,267 → 118,979 bytes, +7.9%.

### `check_faults.py` gains three checks

The prose is really there (the schema requires the KEY, never its content, so `"detection": " "`
validates); no two records share it verbatim (**0 duplicates, and 0 long sentences shared between
any two records** — this layer is not templated, which the precedent bench found the sources layer
was); and **every refusal names a quantity some fault test actually reads**. All 36 are live today.
A refusal for a name nothing reads is a refusal that cannot fire — this corpus's most-repeated
defect wearing the other face, because it looks exactly like a decision taken.

## IV. Two discriminators were built, measured and falsified

Recorded because each reads perfectly well, and because the first one produced a number that was
published in this session's own working notes before it was checked.

**1. "`measurable_from: photograph` means this compiler can never reach it."** It makes a clean
story: 175 of 210 primary tests declare `photograph` and this system holds no photographs. On that
discriminator the census read *261 off-surface, 23 refused, 15 unclaimed*, and the fifteen looked
like a finishable work list.

**FALSE, and by a factor of eighteen.** **54 photograph-only faults are JUDGED** on the Tidewater
plan, because `build/elevation.py` supplies 184 quantities that a photograph test reads perfectly
well. `measurable_from` says what a photograph SUFFICES for; it does not say a drawing cannot. The
true split is **23 refused and 276 unsupplied**.

**2. "The detection prose names its own surface, so hold it against the tests'
`measurable_from`."** A naive word match reports a large disjoint population — prose and tests
sharing no surface at all. The word `elevation` in this prose means the FACE of the building, not
the drawing: *"count exterior doors on the rear elevation of the main block"*. Excluding
`elevation` and `section` from the vocabulary gives, over the 210 records, **118 agreeing, 35 a
subset, 32 naming no surface, 15 overlapping, and 10 disjoint** — against **22 disjoint** under the
naive vocabulary, so **12 of the 22 are that word sense**, and a checker built on the naive match
would have convicted twelve records of a disagreement they do not have.

**That naive figure is a property of the VOCABULARY and not of the corpus, which is why it is
pinned in full in `tests/test_detection.py` rather than quoted here as a measurement.** This
paragraph first said 21, from a vocabulary written by hand earlier in the session with a wider
`site-visit` arm; the shipped one measures 22. `check_grouping_rules.prose_meter`'s discipline —
*a crude regex is an upper bound and the way to lower it is to author, never to tighten* — reaches
the illustration as well as the meter.

**The other 10 are real and are still not a defect**, which is the sharper half:
`stair-at-the-front-door` describes a photograph procedure (*"measure the distance from the door
face to the first riser against the door's own width"*) for a test stated on a `plan`, and
`stack-with-nowhere-to-land` describes a site visit for a test stated on a plan. A fault may
honestly reach the same defect by two routes. So `surface_words()` ships **reported, ratcheted
nowhere, convicting nothing**, with the exclusion pinned by a test and the reason in its docstring.

**This paragraph said "all 21 are the same false positive" through three readings and died the
first time the shipped function was executed over the corpus** — it is 12 of 22, and the other 10
are not errors at all. WP-9.5's technique, met inside the report that cites it, twice: the
correction's own replacement figure was wrong again until the test derived it.

## V. `--contradictions`: two refusals whose reasons had stopped being true

`detection.contradictions()` asks a question the join makes available for the first time: **is
there a name a refusal table refuses that the measurement layer supplies anyway?** Two, and they
are the same shape.

**`wall_thickness_in`.** `arrangement.NOT_DERIVABLE` refused it saying *"the plan record states no
wall thickness; construction_type says what a wall is OF, not how thick"*. `structure.wall_thickness`
had already falsified that and `footprint.wall` (WP-11.2, plan schema 0.5.1) falsified it again —
and the elevation layer has been supplying the name all along, at **15.5 in** on the Tidewater plan
and **8.0** on the spec Colonial, where **six tests across five faults** read it. WP-6.4's *"until X lands is a lie
the moment X lands"*, in a refusal instead of a comment, and `NOT_DERIVABLE`'s own header says
*"read it twice before taking a name OFF"* — nobody re-read it.

**`net_clear_opening_height_in`.** Refused as *"an egress-sash question; the plan states no sash
operation or clear opening"*, which is true of the PLAN and false of the corpus: the elevation
layer supplies it as `upper_w["opening_height_in"] * 0.5`.

**Both entries are KEPT and both reasons corrected.** Deleting either would license a second
derivation of the quantity in the arrangement layer, which is two numbers under one name — the
OQ 48 error. The reasons now name the supplier and say the entry exists so one quantity keeps one
spelling. `tests/test_detection.py` ratchets the pair **by name**, so a third cannot arrive quietly
and a fixed one cannot be cancelled by a new one (WP-11.7: *a removed serious and an added
duplicate cancelled and 63 stayed 63*).

The `0.5` is **not** invented and nothing was withheld for it: it is the lower sash's travel, and
this fault's own secondary note states that model in as many words. What is wrong is its scope — a
casement's leaf opens whole — and the corpus has the discriminator (OQ 91's sash kind, answered
through the lineage on 119 of 159 styles) and does not consult it.
`oq/the-net-clear-opening-is-half-of-every-sash`.

## VI. The largest finding, and it was not what this package was sent to look at

Chasing `net_clear_opening_height_in` produced a question nothing in the tree could ask, so the
first thing was to make it askable.

**`clear` counts two different things.**

| plan | clear | cleared on a test reading a name `critic_suspects` lists as the generator's own |
|---|---|---|
| `tidewater-georgian-careful` | 65 | **26 (40%)** |
| `spec-builder-colonial` | 56 | **27 (48%)** |

The sharpest are not marginal. `one-bay-symmetry-break` clears on
`count_of_openings_without_a_mirror_twin_about_the_facade_centreline = 0` and
`width_of_the_largest_asymmetric_element_in = 0.0`, both bare literals, on a generator that draws a
symmetric facade by construction: **the fault cannot fire and never could.** `broken-head-datum`
clears on `distinct_head_datums_per_storey_per_elevation = 1`. `condenser-on-the-entrance-elevation`
clears on `equipment_units_visible_on_the_entrance_elevation = 0.0`, on a generator that models no
equipment. `brick-front-vinyl-return` clears on `faces_of_volume_clad_in_primary_material = 4`
against `faces_of_volume = 4`.

**They are not false passes**, and calling them that would be the flattering error in the other
direction: every number is true of the house the generator drew. What they are is **vacuous as
verdicts** — a pass that could not have been a failure, counted in the same bucket and reported by
the same number as a pass that survived a real measurement. A reader told "65 clear" is being told
39 of one kind and 26 of another.

**Nothing saw it because `critique.classify` iterates `findings`, and a fault that passes emits
none.** The critic-suspect machinery — which exists precisely to stop a generator chasing its own
constants — has never been handed one of these. `CLAUDE.md` already records the defect as an
instance (*"A fault can be CLEARED by an invented constant, and `NOT_MODELLED` cannot see it"*,
closed at OQ 89 as one fault). It is fifty-three rows over two plans.

`plan_check` publishes `fault_clear_on_a_generator_constant` — a LIST, naming each fault and the
suspect measurements its passing tests read, because a count cannot be argued with. It is a REPORT
and decides nothing: no severity, no finding, no score term. Reclassifying these would be as wrong
as leaving them, because turning a real pass into an unjudged is the fake-unjudged direction.
`oq/clear-counts-a-pass-and-a-tautology-as-one-thing` sets out the four things that must be ruled,
including where the line falls between `faces_of_volume = 4` (arithmetic about a rectangular block)
and `equipment_units_visible = 0.0` (an absence the generator cannot model), which the instrument
cannot separate and a reader can.

## VII. The guards, and that every one of them bites

Fifteen mutations, fifteen red, each with **the match count asserted before the colour was
believed** — this repository's own *a mutation that silently does not apply looks exactly like a
guard that works*, met while applying it two packages ago.

| mutation | guard that fired |
|---|---|
| `refusals()` carries a copy instead of reading the tables | `test_refusals_read_the_authorities_and_do_not_carry_a_copy` |
| `checks()` consumes its delimiter | `test_the_split_loses_no_text` |
| `surface_words` admits the word `elevation` | `test_surface_words_refuses_the_word_elevation` |
| `measurement_names` drops `exceptions[].bounds_test` | driven three-location test |
| the annotation is not additive (every row gains the key) | `test_a_row_with_nothing_refused_is_returned_unchanged` |
| the annotation has no guard and takes the validator down | `test_a_failure_..._degrades_to_the_rows_as_they_were` |
| `refused_all` is always true | `test_a_refused_row_gains_the_reason_and_loses_no_field` |
| the `wall_thickness_in` reason reverts to the claim WP-11.2 falsified | `test_both_corrected_reasons_say_who_supplies_the_quantity` |
| a refusal is added that no fault test reads | `test_no_refusal_names_a_quantity_nothing_reads` **and** `check_faults.py` |
| the suspect join is inert (no names) | `test_the_count_and_the_four_named_rows` |
| a failed instrument returns `[]` instead of `None` | `test_the_instrument_failing_is_None_and_never_an_empty_list` |
| every clear row is reported, suspect or not | `test_a_clear_row_reading_no_suspect_name_is_not_reported` |
| two tables refusing one name: first wins, second reason dropped | `test_two_tables_refusing_one_name_lose_neither_reason` |
| a truncated `faults_clear` is counted instead of refused | `test_a_truncated_clear_list_is_could_not_evaluate...` |
| `plan_check` stops using `detection.identifiers` | the suspect-count ratchet |

**Four of those fifteen guard branches that are unreachable from the corpus** — two tables refusing
one name, a truncated `faults_clear`, a failed refusal table, a failed suspect instrument — and
each is DRIVEN rather than read off the tree, because a fixture that is accidentally the shipped
corpus is a statement about this tree and not about the rule (WP-8.11, and it has bitten this
repository twice).

Two data mutations were run against `check_faults.py` directly — a record whose `detection` is
whitespace, and two records sharing prose verbatim — and both fail the build. **The first run of
that harness read GREEN for both and the harness was wrong, not the checks**: it tested
`returncode` on a shell pipeline ending in `tail`, so it was reading `tail`'s exit code. The error
text was in the output the whole time. Re-run without the pipe: exit 1 both ways.

`test_the_shipped_plan_is_annotated_and_the_result_does_not_balloon` asserts that no unjudged row
carries a `detection` key, so the decision not to attach 77 KB of prose cannot be quietly reversed.

## VIII. What was deliberately not done

1. **No measurement was supplied.** Fifteen names looked reachable under the falsified
   discriminator and the temptation was real — `main_block_depth_ft` is `footprint.depth_ft`,
   `room_depth` is on every room, `exterior_openings_on_the_passage_axis` is very nearly what
   WP-11.9's `arrangement.passage_ends_with_a_door` counts. **Very nearly is the reason.** That
   fault scores a window at the rear 0.5 and a door 1.0; `passage_ends_with_a_door` counts distinct
   reaches and knows nothing about which. Supplying it would be one quantity under two meanings,
   which is OQ 48 in a new place, and arming a rule that presupposed the number is OQ 89's lesson.
2. **No refusal was deleted.** Two reasons were corrected; the entries stay, for the one-spelling
   discipline set out in §V.
3. **`egress-window-flips-the-proportion` was not moved from clear to unjudged.** Withholding
   `net_clear_opening_height_in` would do it honestly, and it is the elevation layer's call, not
   this one's: the sash-kind discriminator exists and reading it is that layer's package.
4. **No ceiling was moved.** `critic_suspects.LITERALS_CEILING` (44) and `RATIOS_CEILING` (7) are
   ratchets on the instrument and untouched. §VI's 26 and 27 are new ratchets of their own.
5. **The detection prose was not made machine-readable.** The WP-11.9 model — author a reading
   beside each sentence and hold each `basis` to the prose it quotes — is the right shape and is
   roughly six hundred authored entries over 210 records. It is not refused; it is not this
   package, and `checks()` exists so the next one starts from a reader rather than from a regex.
6. **`check_all.py`'s `len(CHECKS)` was not moved, and `detection.py selftest` is not wired into
   it.** The three new checks live INSIDE `check_faults.py`, which already runs, so the published
   total stays at 50 (`TOTAL_CHECKS` 53) and `tests/test_counts_guard.py` has nothing to catch. That is deliberate:
   CLAUDE.md records this number going wrong at five separate merges, always at exactly the point
   a check was added, and the selftest would have gated nothing the fault checker does not already
   gate — it calls `checks()`, `refusals()` and `measurement_names()` over the whole corpus on
   every build.

   (This paragraph first said the total "stays at 45", read out of a CLAUDE.md sentence that is
   the record of an earlier merge rather than of this tree. Derived: `len(check_all.CHECKS)` is
   **50** and `TOTAL_CHECKS` is **53**. Fourth figure in this package corrected by executing
   instead of reading, and the one whose source was a line CLAUDE.md itself warns goes stale
   silently.)
7. **`surface_words()` was not made into a checker.** §IV.2 is why, and the function ships with the
   reason attached so the next reader does not rebuild it.

**And one thing found in passing that is not this package's:** `build/check_frontend.py`'s lazy-tier
block reports a hard `FAIL: three.js is not a separate chunk — round/three-scene.js has been
imported statically somewhere` on this container, with every change of this package stashed. The
cause is not the code: `workbench/app/dist/` is UNTRACKED and was built on 7 September 2026, before
WP-12.4 added `three`, so the artefact simply predates the feature, and the source-level pin in
`round.test.mjs` — that `three` is imported only by `round/three-scene.js` and that module only
through a dynamic `import()` — passes. The check has two states where it needs three: it says
COULD NOT EVALUATE for an ABSENT `dist/` and FAIL for a STALE one, so a build with nothing wrong
with it goes red with a message accusing the code. That is the shape CLAUDE.md records on 4
September — *a red build nobody can act on is worse than no build, because the next real failure
arrives inside it* — and the remedy is a third branch comparing the newest mtime under
`workbench/app/src` against the newest in `dist/assets`. Not done here: it is a different checker,
a different phase, and widening this package to reach it is how a package stops being finishable.

## IX. Verdict

`detection` has a reader. The 23 refusals the corpus had already taken now reach the reader who
needed them, eight faults are wholly accounted for, two refusal reasons that had quietly stopped
being true are corrected and pinned by name, and the largest finding — 26 and 27 clear verdicts
earned on the generator's own output — is measured, published and unruled rather than silently
counted as agreement.

The number this package did not move is 276. That is the honest state and it is the subject of
`oq/two-hundred-and-seventy-six-measurements-nobody-refuses-and-nobody-supplies`. **276 honest
unjudged is not worse than 276 invented numbers**, and this package's own §VI is the measurement of
what the alternative costs.

New questions: `oq/clear-counts-a-pass-and-a-tautology-as-one-thing`,
`oq/the-net-clear-opening-is-half-of-every-sash`,
`oq/two-hundred-and-seventy-six-measurements-nobody-refuses-and-nobody-supplies`.

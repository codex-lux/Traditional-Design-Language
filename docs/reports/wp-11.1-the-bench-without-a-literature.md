# WP-11.1 — The bench without a literature

*4 September 2026. Opens Phase 11, the precedent bench, at Lucas's request: where the research is thin
and where deepening it would change what the machine does; the exemplars of each style researched into
the most beautiful and iconic precedents, with links. Cite reports by filename, never the bare number
(OQ 90). Every figure below was measured in this session by a script over the working tree or by a tool
call whose result is quoted; where a figure came from a subagent's reading it says so and was re-derived
before it was used.*

## The verdict in one line

**The corpus has a grammar, a vocabulary and a critic, and almost no literature** — no period building in
`plans/`, an exemplar that is a name with no locator, and 542 kit figures that claim to have been measured
while nothing on the record says where — and the one meter that policed provenance had counted the other
kind for a year.

## I. The depth profile: templated on the surface, unmetered underneath

Every one of the 132 buildable nodes carries 2–4 exemplars (90 carry exactly 4, 38 three, 4 two, none one
or five), 4 or 5 sources (never 0–3, never 6), exactly 5 constraints, 3 `distinguished_from` (129 of 132),
4–6 diagnostic tells and 388–858 words of long description. A composite of exemplars + sources + words +
distinctions spreads **13.6 → 20.6, a 1.5× ratio**, across the whole set. The 32 family and tradition nodes
carry 0 exemplars, 0 sources, 0 constraints, 0 packs and 85–246 words — and every one is `confidence: high`,
a field whose schema description scopes it to dates and lineage and which therefore does no work at that
rank. Field counts cannot separate a thoroughly researched node from a skeletal one, and no checker had ever
tried.

Four measures do discriminate, and `build/check_research.py` now computes every one on every run:

The kit is the only place with a real depth gradient, and it is a cliff: `georgian-colonial-american` holds
407 of 1,559 parameters (26%), the median kit fewer than ten. The unsourced `measured` figures lead exactly
where the generators read — `height_proportion` 34, `chimney` 24, `ceiling_height_rule` 24,
`window_proportion` 21, `composition_parti` 17, `belt_course` 13 — and `tidewater-georgian`, the flagship, is
31 of 31. The style node cites five books and the slot carries a `rule` and a `note`, so each number is
probably defensible; nothing says which book gives 7:12, so a reader cannot check, and a figure that cannot
be checked is not measured in the sense the schema means.

**The read set is derived, not typed, and the two disagreed.** A hand-typed list of the slots the
generators read gave 230 unsourced figures on read slots; the AST walk `check_research.py` makes over the
generators' and the critic's string constants finds 35 slots and 272. The instrument's number is the one
pinned, and its set is pinned by equality in `tests/test_research.py` so a generator reading a new slot, or
ceasing to, is noticed rather than forbidden. It is an upper bound: a slot named in an error message counts.

## II. An exemplar has no locator

`schema/style-node.schema.json` closed the exemplar at `name/location/year/note`. 482 exemplars, 410 distinct
buildings, **zero URLs anywhere in `styles/`**; archival identifiers appear only inside 26 free-text notes
("National Register 1974."), and exactly one note mentions HABS. `README.md` said "roughly 90 of 299 North
American exemplar rows were individually web-verified" against a corpus of 482, and nothing recorded which
90. Sources are per-node strings — 314 distinct, 219 used once, McAlester's *Field Guide* cited 73 times
under three spellings — never per claim. Buildings are shared across nodes (the Stanley-Whitman House stands
for three, Hammond-Harwood and Drayton Hall for two), so the right grain for a record is the building.

## III. The grammar's own sourcing, and the layers with none

All 57 packs carry an `authority.strength`: **25 canonical** (the twenty treatise orders and `room-harmonic`),
**15 documented**, **1 conventional** (`storey-graduation`: "current professional practice", no author — and
it sets every storey height in the corpus), **16 reconstructed**, nine of them "from this corpus's own
records" (`corbel-course`, `jetty-overhang`, `facade-medieval-english`, `facade-portada`, `facade-picturesque`,
`room-vernacular`, `log-module`, `timber-bay`, `balcony-gallery`). **0 of 761 derived rules carry a per-rule
`source`**; 650 carry an `authority_note`. `greek-doric` and `moorish-arch` have empty `sources[]`.

`massings/catalog.json` has no provenance vocabulary at all — 40 records, no `sources`, no `confidence`, no
`kind` — and it is the skeleton every house is composed on. `groupings/` internal rules (85) carry no
source. `openings/grammar.json` says of itself that its bands were fitted to one plan's 58 hand-authored
doors. All 16 plan records carry no `provenance` block (schema 0.2.0 added one after they were written).

**No period building exists in the data.** The 14 reference plans are modern plan-book screenshots (13 of
the 20 good examples were never opened, WP-2.1); `plans/precedents/` does not exist; the asset manifest holds
0 records of kind `plan` or `measured-drawing`; its 73 `sourced` assets are engine-drawn plates.
`Plan Examples/HABS/WANTED.md` names six buildings and waits on a download; `build/measure_precedents.py` is
unwritten. The study's measured evidence — 15 passages, 27 rooms, 11 of them from one Mount Vernon page —
lived in `docs/reports/wp-9.2-what-the-tradition-actually-does.md` §2 and nowhere in the data. Its §6 lists
eleven things that must not be coded, four cited works "verified as citations, none read by anyone", and its
closing section says the plan layer is "built against two English books, one of them Victorian."

## IV. What the machine reads, and therefore where a source changes a drawing

The operational map, verified against source, decides the leverage ranking:

- **`build/elevation.py:1424-1428` dimensions every elevation from five hard-coded packs** —
  `opening-proportion`, `sash-light`, `facade-classical`, `brick-course`, `gibbs-ionic` — never from the
  style's bindings. Its own comment at `:294-299` says it is blind to bindings, scopes, declines and
  forbids, and reads a forbidden slot on 40 (style, slot) pairs. `structure.py` reads `storey-graduation`
  the same way. Research on the other 51 packs changes what the system SAYS and nothing it DRAWS.
  `oq/the-elevation-reads-five-packs-whatever-the-style-binds`.
- **62 of 97 slots are read by no generator or critic** — all of interior trim, `roof_pitch` as a kit slot,
  `eave_condition`, `primary_cladding`, `wall_thickness_*`, `order`, `pediment`. An editorial parameter on
  one of those changes nothing. `garage_strategy` carries 9, the most of any slot, and nothing reads it.
- `exemplars`, `sources`, `confidence`, `diagnostic_tells`, `distinguished_from`, `description.long` are read
  by display, search and the asset namer only; `confidence: low` gates nothing.
- **17 style ids have ever been a plan or brief style** (`tidewater-georgian` 12 times, `colonial-revival`
  5). 42 nodes appear in no brief, plan or test. `federal-style` is named native by five partis — the most of
  any style — and has never once been composed. Research on a style outside the 17 changes nothing
  observable until a plan or brief exists for it; a transcribed period plan would be both.
- The critic's silence per shipped plan: 120–126 faults unjudged for want of 299–313 distinct measurement
  names, and four of `tidewater-georgian`'s own five constraints unjudged on its own reference plan
  (`chimney_height_above_ridge_ft`, `roof_pitch_rise_per_12`, `water_table_height_in`,
  `service_zone_position`). Those are envelope facts a HABS data page states for the exemplars; the critic
  needs them from the GENERATED house, which is modelling and not research.

**The ranking this report defends.** The test is whether a source on the claim changes a number the machine
produces or a verdict the critic returns.

1. **A sourced precedent dataset the critic is calibrated against.** Every band the critic holds a
   generated house to is either sourced or convicts the tradition. The envelope half is startable today
   from HABS text; the arrangement half still needs the sheets. It also widens the exercised path: a
   transcribed Drayton Hall is the first period plan for `georgian-colonial-american`.
2. **The six packs every drawing is dimensioned from.** One paragraph of authority each, no rule sourced,
   one with no author. Sourcing them per rule has corpus-wide effect on every elevation and section.
3. **The 272 unsourced `measured` figures on read slots**, node by node from each node's own exemplars —
   the OQ 18 discipline applied to the half of the kit that draws.
4. **Precedent links and standing** — the ask. Changes what the system says, makes 482 exemplars
   checkable, turns the image harvest from a name search into an id lookup.
5. The nine self-sourced packs; the 32 exemplar-less higher ranks; the three untestable nodes; `massings/`.

## V. The route, measured

`WebFetch` is egress-blocked for `loc.gov`, `en.wikipedia.org`, `wikidata.org`, `sah-archipedia.org` and
`historicengland.org.uk` (`EGRESS_BLOCKED`, 4 Sep). The Tavily tier — ruled at
`oq/fetching-through-a-tier-the-proxy-denies` — searches everything and **extracts** `tile.loc.gov` data
PDFs, Wikipedia (the infobox carries the NRHP reference and, under external links, the HABS number with
its sheet counts), `npgallery.nps.gov` (National Register nomination PDFs) and Wikidata; it does not reach
`sah-archipedia.org` or `loc.gov/pictures`. Share-alike images are refused by ruling; **a link is not a
derivative**, so the link layer has none of the image layer's rights problem.

**The pilot**, on `tidewater-georgian`'s four exemplars: two searches per building resolve the ids from
two surfaces (Carter's Grove: Wikipedia's *"HABS No. VA-351 … 27 measured drawings, 4 data pages"*; Virginia
DHR 047-0001, NRHP 69000249); one extract of the data PDF returns Part II verbatim and self-identifying —
Gunston Hall: *"a rectangle of h0 feet 11-1/2 inches by 60 feet 10 inches, exterior foundation
measure-ments. From grade to ridge of the roof, the house is 33'-1" high … The walls are about two feet
think … four tall chimney stacks on the house, tvo at each end."* Westover, 1939: *"Brick, Flemish bond;
two stories; hipped roof; two chimneys each endj front seven bays; all sash 9-over-9 lights; five hipped
dormers on roof."* The OCR reads `h0` for 40 and `tvo` for two, which is why every figure is carried
`as_printed`. About four tool calls a building. 235 of the 410 exemplar buildings are in the United States;
83 nodes are all-US, 44 all non-US, 5 mixed.

Two things the pilot found that the corpus should know: the 1939 surveyor records Westover's plan as
*"off center through hall"* — the study's §2f already quotes this against the assumption that a Tidewater
house has a symmetrical centre passage — and Westover carries **five hipped dormers** while the shipped
Tidewater reference plan declares `dormer: none`.

## VI. What was built

- **`schema/precedent.schema.json` and `precedents/`** — one record per building, id == filename, shared by
  every node that names it: `refs[]` (`habs`, `loc-item`, `nrhp`, `nhl`, `state-register`, `wikipedia`,
  `wikidata`, `historic-england`, `institution`, `monograph`), each with `retrieved` and a closed `via`;
  `survey` — the HABS written data quoted verbatim in contiguous spans by the survey's own outline, with
  the page marker each was read beside; `measurements[]` with `as_printed`; `rights_evidence` and never a
  `license`. Seeded with `gunston-hall`, `westover` and `drayton-hall` from the pilot's own extracts.
- **The exemplar's `precedent`, `standing` (`icon` / `canonical` / `regional` / `documentary`) and `why`**
  on `schema/style-node.schema.json`; `name` and `location` unchanged because `check_assets.py` reads them.
- **`build/check_precedents.py`** — both directions between exemplar and record, id shapes per kind,
  `retrieved`/`via` on every reference, no `license` at any depth, `measurements[].from_quote` in range,
  kit `source` pointers of the form `precedents/<id>#survey.<field>` resolved; floors that may only rise and
  ceilings at zero; `--live` returns COULD NOT EVALUATE on a proxy denial. **It earned itself on its first
  run**: the seeded Drayton Hall record said "Ashley River Road" where both exemplars say "Ashley River".
  Mutation-checked three ways (a deleted `retrieved`, a `license` key, a dangling `precedent`) — red each
  time.
- **`build/check_research.py`** — the meter of §I, ratcheted; `check_kits.py`'s census prints
  `measured-bare` beside `editorial-bare`; `tests/test_provenance.py` pins the ceilings;
  `tests/test_research.py` pins the sets by name and proves by mutation that sourcing one figure moves the
  count by one and that the pointer resolves.
- **`check_all.py` runs 49** (46 in the loop, three suites); `TOTAL_CHECKS` and `tests/test_counts_guard.py`
  moved with it, as the trap in CLAUDE.md says they must.
- **`check_counts.py`** now derives and claims the per-rank node counts (`docs/model.md` said 89 styles / 43
  variants against 90 / 42 for as long as the table existed), the exemplar total ("482 exemplars" was
  published with no claim on it — WP-8.14's class), the MCP tool count (pinned in four tests and hand-typed
  in fourteen places, none claimed), and the meter's own figures.
- **`tdl_precedents`**, the 27th tool: by style, with the record joined; walking the membership tree down
  for the 32 exemplar-less nodes and saying it is a reading; by id; by query. `tdl_get_style` joins the
  record's card onto each exemplar and says `precedent_record: null` for one that has none.
  `tdl_list_partis`' docstring said "12" against 21 since WP-4.5 — a hand-typed count in a place no checker
  reads; corrected.
- The workbench `StyleRecord` and the static atlas render standing, why and the record's refs as links.
- `docs/precedents.md`; a row in `docs/README.md`; Phase 11 in `PLAN-OF-ACTION.md`; the state document.

## IX. What was deliberately not done

- **No sheet transcribed, no band changed, no parti edited, no licence written.** The sheets are not here
  and the bands are Lucas's.
- **No kit parameter sourced.** The pointer convention exists and `check_precedents.py` resolves it; the
  D6 table (§VII) lists what a survey would source or contradict, and nothing was applied, because
  `oq/a-surveyors-prose-may-source-an-envelope-figure` is a ruling and not a mechanism.
- **No `precedent:` citation kind** for the palette. `REF_RE`, `CITE_RE` and `parseCite` need no change, but
  `routeCite`, `citeFor`, `SELECTION_KEYS` and `KIND_ORDER` all need a surface that takes a precedent
  selection, and none exists.
- **No harvest workflow** — the register says last, not first.
- **Nothing sourced from a secondary work** for OQ 7–11 or OQ 18's source half.
- **`elevation.py`'s five packs left as they are** — a behaviour change with 40 known forbidden reads
  behind it, and a package of its own.
- **WP-4.7 and WP-5.3 untouched.** Both are named in §IV's ranking with their reason.

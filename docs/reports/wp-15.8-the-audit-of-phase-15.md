### A clear that rests on nothing

The acquittal has a cause older than all of this. `core._judge` returns `clear` when every test
that RAN passed. A test that wanted a measurement nobody supplied did not run, and does not count.
Measured with `tests/fault_clears.py` at `2467585`, on the sixteen plans on the heuristic, as the
bench checks them:

- **550** fault verdicts read clear.
- **142** of them (26%) never ran their governing test, every one for want of a measurement.
  - 48 are on fatal faults, 62 serious and 32 minor.
  - They fall on 19 faults and 15 plans.
  - **70 of the 142 cleared on a secondary that is `build/elevation.py`'s own constant**, so about
    half rest on nothing measured at all.
- **284** (52%) left some applicable test unrun, counting secondaries.

The plainest case is `storeys-out-of-vertical-alignment`. It reads clear on seven one-storey plans,
on a floor on the bay count that says nothing about alignment, where the honest verdict is not
applicable. OQ 84 met one instance of this in August and closed it by supplying the measurement.
The rule that made the instance possible was never raised as a question.

**Three rulings come first**, and the question states them:

- whether a secondary may stand in for the primary; no record says which secondaries are the same
  rule;
- whether a clear needs the governing test (142 move) or every applicable test (284 move);
- what an unjudged fatal costs a candidate.

## IX. Traps worth carrying

- **A gate set before the measurement is what stopped a fake pass.** The front-complete fix read as
  plainly right: one reader, two layers, a contradiction removed. Measured against the gate written
  before it was built, it turned a fatal fault into a clear on four plans. Written after, the gate
  would have been fitted to the result.
- **Withholding a measurement does not unjudge a fault.** The fault is clear on whatever test is
  left. Before withholding a figure, find out which tests read it and which do not.
- **A mutation proves a guard only against the tests it was run against.** Run it against the
  files that hold the property, not the one test written for it.
- **Read a figure off the element that states it, after waiting for that element.** The walk's
  race read a page's text before the span existed.
- **A fix that makes a reader see more can make an older reader see wrong.** Counting a partly
  seated window's sashes made the hyphen a through-axis room, and the element-blind spine convicted
  it. The probe that found it was a per-plan diff of every finding, not the tests.
- **Driven per check is not driven per clause.** Six census clauses could be switched off inside
  checks that counted as driven. Plant the defect each clause exists for.
- **An assumption beside a question can outlive the question's premise.** The Round's camera-left
  reading was harmless while every face was symmetric, and its open question said so. WP-13.3 made
  the faces asymmetric and stated their direction on the record, and nothing went back to the
  question.

# WP-17.0: two grounds, not a theme

**Phase 17, the Workbench redrawn. 29 Oct 2026.** The handoff is
`docs/ui-redesign-2026-10-29.md`.

## I. What was built

**The two ground blocks.** `theme/tokens.css` carries `[data-ground="instrument"]` and
`[data-ground="vellum"]`.
- Each rebinds the semantic duties and nothing else: surfaces, text, borders, the link, the
  focus ring, three severity inks, the refusal's sentence and the receipt.
- The vellum block restores every duty the instrument moves to its `:root` value, so a plate set
  inside the dark instrument reads as the page read before the instrument existed.

**Four primitives, and three new duties.**
- Primitives:
  - `--heavy-2` (a panel on the instrument);
  - `--coal-deep` (an inset field);
  - `--rule-on-dark` and `--rule-on-dark-2` (hairline and border on the dark).
- Duties:
  - `--surface-chrome`;
  - `--surface-lit` (a focused field: paper-lit on vellum, coal-deep on the dark);
  - `--refusal-text` (a refusal's sentence: brick on paper, salmon on the dark).

**`src/grounds.js`** is the one reader of the two blocks:
- `stripGrounds`, `groundTokens`, `rootTokens`, `groundValue`;
- `contrast`, which `inks.test.mjs` now imports instead of carrying its own copy;
- `SHELL_GROUND` and `SURFACE_GROUND`, the table of which ground each page's `<main>` stands on.

`App.jsx` marks `#root` and `<main>` from that table. **Every row is still `vellum`.** A surface
moves to the instrument in its own package's commit, so no surface is shown dark before it has
been redrawn.

**`components/Vellum.jsx` and `components/Plate.jsx`** are the one way content is set on paper.
The plate has a 10 px mat, a double border, a lift shadow, a header slot for the loupe, and a mono
caption.

**The migration: 594 colour primitives in 51 interface files became duties.**
- The mapping: `--ink` to `--text-body`, `--ink-2` to `--text-secondary`, `--paper` to
  `--surface-panel`, `--rule` to `--border-hairline`, and the rest in kind.
- It is done by a script, and comments are left untouched.
- Under the vellum ground every duty resolves to the primitive it replaced, so **the app renders
  the same pixels**. The Front Door was screenshotted and compared.

**What the migration left alone, deliberately:**
- **The drawing files**: `sheet/`, `round/`, `AssemblyPlate`, the atlas, the mark forms, the edge
  glyphs and the relaxation marker. A drawing's ink is a pigment on paper, and a duty there would
  make a line follow the page around it.
- **`surfaces/Proportions.jsx`**, because its order plate carries three rows `inks.test.mjs` holds
  by identity. It is migrated with its own surface.

## II. The guards

`src/grounds.test.mjs` adds eleven tests:
- a ground block declares duties and never a primitive;
- vellum restores every duty the instrument moves, to the `:root` value;
- every text duty reads 4.5 : 1 on every surface of its ground;
- no interface file sets a colour primitive beyond the table, and the table may only shrink;
- the ground table is total over `state/layout.js`'s surfaces.

Each verdict has a driven twin.

**Mutation.** A `var(--rule)` put back into `Splitter.jsx` turned the primitive test red. Restored,
it is green.

`inks.test.mjs` and `marks.test.mjs` read the `:root` answer through `stripGrounds`. Without that,
their last-declaration-wins readers would have judged every duty at its vellum restatement, and
the "declared twice" rule would have convicted the ground blocks for doing what they are for.

## III. What was found

1. **The handoff's 6.3 : 1 is 6.10.** `--ink-4` on `--coal` measures **6.10 : 1** and on `--heavy`
   **5.30 : 1**. Both pass, so the ruling stands; the figure in the note is corrected here, not
   in the note.
2. **Two inks cannot be type on the dark.**
   - `--ink-3` reads 3.81 : 1 on `--heavy`, and `--ink-2` reads 2.47. So `--text-tertiary` takes
     `--ink-4` on the instrument, and `--text-disabled` takes `--ink-2`, because a disabled
     control is what a faint ink is for.
   - `--brick` reads 3.43 : 1 as type on `--heavy`, so a refusal keeps its brick band and its
     sentence moves to `--salmon` (6.49).
3. **`--text-secondary` on `--paper-deep` reads 4.32 : 1.** This was true before the instrument
   existed, and `Eyebrow.jsx`'s own comment records it. The ground test holds it as one known
   row by identity, so it cannot grow a sibling.
4. **The `tokens.css` comment that called the palette single-theme "at the user's direction" is
   rewritten, dated, with the reversal named.** The 29 Oct ruling reverses it. No value changed,
   so `sheet_style.LIGHT` and `svg_theme.HEX_MAP` are untouched.

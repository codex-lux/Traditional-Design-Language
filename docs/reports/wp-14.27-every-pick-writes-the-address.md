# WP-14.27: every pick writes the address, and no surface shows a default record

*25 September 2026. Phase 14, tranche 2. The contract is `docs/prd/phase-14-tranche-2.md` §C.12,
§F and §G; the principles are tranche 1's §E.4 and §F.4 (the URL decides what is read, and memory
only offers links). Cite this report by filename: Phase 14's numbers are this line's, and in this
repository a number does not identify a package.*

## What it is for

Two kinds of screen could disagree with the address above them.

- **A bare address opened a record nobody had named.** `#/faults` drew the card of
  `porch-too-shallow-to-inhabit` because that id was typed into `FaultCorpus.jsx` as
  `DEFAULT_FAULT`. `#/phylogeny` opened `tidewater-georgian`'s record the same way
  (`DEFAULT_TAXON`). The address named no record, so a reader could not share what they saw, and
  the walk's own checks had come to rely on the constant.
- **Some picks did not write the address.**
  - The family tree's descent buttons moved the record pane and left the hash behind.
  - A candidate's column was component state.
  - The Drawing Set's sheet and face were `useState`, so a reload or a shared link always drew
    the plan at the entrance front, whatever the reader had been looking at.
  - Export had no face at all. Three of the four elevations the Drawing Set shows could be looked
    at and never taken away.

WP-14.25's report also named two defects that are this package's: the candidate strip read
*"returned 2 of 1 asked for"*, and a lineage column read *"NOT native to this style — native to
an ancestor or relative of the style"*.

## I. What was built

### No default record

- **`DEFAULT_FAULT` and `DEFAULT_TAXON` are gone.** Both surfaces read their selection or
  `null`, in the initial state and in the reset effect. A bare `#/faults` lists the corpus, and a
  bare `#/phylogeny` draws the tree. Neither draws a record card.
- **The empty pane is worded by a record.** `components/NoRecordChosen.jsx` renders the
  `no-record-chosen` glossary record: its term as a `Term` under an eyebrow, and its definition.
  The app writes none of it.
- **A hollow hearth on the map is a target across its whole disc** (`pointer-events: visible`
  in `surfaces/phylo/MapView.jsx`). A click in the middle of a country-precision mark used to
  fall through to the map; §II.1 has the measurement.
- **The family tree with nothing chosen dims nothing.** `bright(id)` is `!sel || lit(id)`, so a
  bare tree is not greyed out around a record that is not there. It draws no lineage edges.
  WP-14.26's compare code (`lit`, the shift-click `pick`) is untouched; `bright` is a separate
  line beside it.

### Every pick writes the address

- **Descent buttons.** They carry `data-descent` and call `setSelection({ style })`. A descent
  therefore pushes history like any other selection, and Back returns to the taxon you came from.
- **Candidate columns.** A column writes the numeric `candidate` key, which the router already
  held in `SELECTION_KEYS` and `NUMERIC_KEYS`. The column is the candidate's index in the server's
  own list (`c.n`), not its rank on screen, so re-sorting the columns cannot change which
  candidate an address names. `CandidateSet`'s local `sel` state is gone.
- **Drawing Set.** The sheet and the face are `useSurfaceFilters` axes named `sheet` and `face`
  (`widens: true`, on the `view` pattern), read through `drawingFaces.parseSheet` and `parseFace`.
  A malformed value reads as the model or as no face; it is never guessed.
- **Export.** Export has a face picker in its strip, headed by the `elevation-face` record.
  - Its chips are the four face records (`face-south`, `face-north`, `face-east`, `face-west`).
  - A fifth chip, **"the entrance front"**, worded by `entrance-front`, is the unpicked state and
    means the server's default. That spares this surface deriving the front's compass letter,
    which only the server knows.
  - The face is sent to `/api/drawings/elevation` (the SVG) and to `/api/export/dxf` with
    `kind: "elevation"` (the CAD sheet), through one spelling: `drawingOpts` and `cadOpts` in
    `surfaces/drawingFaces.js`, which the Drawing Set uses too.

### The Fault Corpus read for one style

With `?style=` the list groups three ways, in the dossier's order. Each group is headed by its
record (`fault-group-here`, `fault-group-lineage`, `fault-group-universal`) and a count.

- **The partition is the server's.** It is `corpus.dossier_faults`, served on the dossier route,
  and nothing in the app re-derives it. A source guard refuses `applies_to`, `severity_by_style`
  and `inverted_by` in `FaultCorpus.jsx`.
- **The universal group is a set difference, not a rule.** The partition names the first two
  groups and only COUNTS the third. The third group is therefore the style's own
  `/api/faults?style=` list, less the two named groups, taken at the same limit the partition
  was.
- **It is held to the served count.** `faults/groups.js::faultGroups` returns `unjudged`, with
  the disagreement stated, in three cases: the universal count differs from `universal_count`,
  the list's length differs from `matches`, or a named id is missing from the list. The page
  then shows that sentence and an ungrouped list. A group whose membership and served count
  disagree has stopped meaning what its heading says.

### WP-14.25's two findings

- **The strip.** `candidateOrder.setSize` counts the set as the candidates less the one the
  composer says it appended (`named_parti.returned && named_parti.appended`). The appended one is
  named beside the count by the `named-by-the-brief` record. The strip reads *"returned 1 of 1
  asked for · named by the brief · <name>"*. It is computed from `named_parti` and holds no count
  literal.
- **The nativity word.** `candidateOrder.nativityLine` words the column from the served
  `nativity`, through `NATIVITY_TERMS`, the one map the Brief's select and the dossier already
  use. A lineage column reads *"lineage parti — native to an ancestor or relative of the style"*. Only
  a borrowed column drops the composer's own "NOT native" clause, because there the record
  already says it. Where no nativity was served, no record is named and the composer's sentence
  is printed whole.

### Records, all new files on 0.1.0 fields

There are ten records: `no-record-chosen` (product), `fault-group-here`, `fault-group-lineage`
and `fault-group-universal` (fault), and `elevation-face`, `entrance-front` and the four
`face-*` records (house).

- Every one is `kind: editorial`.
- Every basis quotes at least 25 characters verbatim from an admissible file: the fault schema's
  `exceptions`, `inverted_by` and `severity_by_style` descriptions, `core.py`'s *"Faults are
  element-first: most are universal, and style is a facet"*, and `build/compass.py` and
  `build/axis.py` for the faces.
- `check_glossary.py` returns **214 records, 678 quotations verified**.

No schema, checker or `fields.js` was touched, which is PRD §A.2's lane rule.

## II. What was found

1. **The walk's map-click check (W6) could not fail, and replacing it found a map mark you
   could not pick by clicking its middle.**
   - **The old check.** It clicked the first hearth in document order with `force`, then asked
     only that the page's text contain a rank word (`/VARIANT|STYLE|FAMILY|TRADITION/`). The rank
     chips in the strip always carry those words, and `DEFAULT_TAXON`'s record carried one
     besides. So the check passed whether or not the click did anything.
   - **The re-cut check.** It needs no record before the click. After the click the address must
     name a taxon, and that taxon's record must be the one on screen. It went red on its first
     run.
   - **Cause one, in the instrument.** The first hearth in document order sits beneath the record
     pane. `elementFromPoint` at its centre returns the pane, so the click never reached the map.
     The check now clicks a mark whose centre is its own, measured the same way.
   - **Cause two, in the product.** A country-precision hearth is drawn hollow (`fill="none"`).
     SVG's default `pointer-events` is `visiblePainted`, so the disc took a click only on its
     dashed 1.2 px stroke. Measured at the map's opening view: **three of the six hollow marks on
     screen passed a click at their centre through to the coastline beneath**, and the map
     panned instead of picking. Mark 27 is one of them: clicking its centre leaves the address
     bare without the fix, and names `colonial-iberian-americas` with it.
   - **The fix.** `MapView.jsx` gives the disc `pointer-events: visible`. After it, all six are
     their own target at the centre.
   - **The guards.** A node source test holds the attribute (it goes red with it removed). The
     walk checks every hollow mark on screen by geometry, then picks one with a click in its
     middle.

   **The instrument had the defect's own shape**: a default record makes a pick look as if it
   worked, and it hid a real pick that did not work.
2. **Four elevation SVGs of one house saved as one file name.** Export and the Drawing Set both
   saved `{plan}-elevation.svg` whichever face was drawn. `sheetFileName` puts the face in the
   name for an elevation: the face picked, or else the front the server reports. The CAD half was
   **not** a defect, and I nearly reported it as one. `export_dxf.export_elevation_dxf` already
   returns `sheets: "elevation-<face>"`, and `corpus.export` builds the filename from it. I
   suspected otherwise, then read the writer before writing the sentence.
3. **The dossier's own Faults section still types its three group eyebrows** (*"written for its
   lineage"*, *"universal"*) in `app/src/dossier/Faults.jsx`. The three records exist now, so
   that section can read them. It is the dossier owner's file and is **not edited here**.
4. **The search index grows by exactly the ten records**, from 839 to 849 entries. I measured it
   with one instrument (compact JSON of `corpus.search_index()`) on both trees: **259.9 KB at 839
   on the base `5aa8041` and 263.0 KB at 849 here**. `docs/workbench.md` had said ~258 KB for the
   base tree, which is this instrument within 2 KB. That line and `workbench/README.md` are
   corrected. **`CLAUDE.md` still says 839**: this lane may not edit it, so `check_counts.py`
   reports one stale claim until the integration package takes it.

## III. What was deliberately not done

- **Phylogeny's shift-click compare** is WP-14.26's and is untouched.
- **No router change.** `candidate` was already a numeric selection key. `sheet` and `face` are
  surface filters and need no router entry.
- **No server change.** The dossier route already serves the partition, and both drawing routes
  already took `face`.
- **The entrance front is not derived in the app.** Export's unpicked chip is worded
  *"the entrance front"* rather than given a compass letter, because the front belongs to the
  record and only the server resolves it.

## IV. Guards, and the mutations that prove them

`src/everyPick.test.mjs` holds sixteen tests, and each join is also asserted in the source it
guards. Sixteen mutations were run through a harness that asserts each one **landed** before its
result is believed, and restores the file byte for byte, checked by hash. All sixteen went
**red**. The tree's diff hash was identical before and after the run.

| mutation | result |
|---|---|
| restore `DEFAULT_FAULT` in the initial state | red |
| restore `DEFAULT_TAXON` | red |
| the Fault Corpus reset falls back to a record | red |
| drop the face from `cadOpts` | red |
| drop the face from `drawingOpts` | red |
| Export sends no face on the DXF | red |
| Export sends no face on the SVG | red |
| the strip counts every column (`setSize` without the subtraction) | red |
| only `native` is worded by a record (the lineage word lost) | red |
| lineage prose keeps the NOT-native clause | red |
| a column pick writes nothing | red |
| a descent writes nothing | red |
| the universal count is not held to the partition | red |
| the Drawing Set's sheet goes back to component state | red |
| a face chip given another face's record | red |
| the hollow hearth loses `pointer-events: visible` | red |

**The walk** (`e2e/walk.mjs`) now covers each pick, as follows.

- **Fault Corpus.** A bare page shows no card and the `no-record-chosen` word. Picking the list's
  first row puts that fault in the hash; a reload keeps it; Back takes it away.
- **Family tree.** Same check on a bare `#/phylogeny`. Then a taxon is picked by name, one of its
  descent buttons is clicked, the hash and the record must agree, a reload keeps it, and Back
  returns to the first taxon. W6 is re-cut as described in §II.
- **Drawing Set.** `sheet=elevation&face=<not the front>` must be in the hash, and after a reload
  both radios must still be checked.
- **Export.** The face chip is picked by its record's word, the hash must carry it, and a reload
  keeps it. The **POST bodies** of `/api/drawings/elevation` and `/api/export/dxf` are captured
  (the routes are answered by a stand-in, since what is under test is what the page sends) and
  must carry `face`, with `kind: "elevation"` on the DXF.
- **Candidates.**
  - The strip's count is held to the job's own `named_parti`, and the appended candidate must be
    named apart by its record.
  - The named column's nativity must be the served one, worded by its record, with no "NOT
    native" on a lineage column.
  - Picking the column must write the server's index into the hash and survive a reload.
  - On the runs measured here the composer ranked the named lineage parti INTO the set (asked
    for one, returned one, nothing appended), so the walk judged the strip's plain branch. The
    appended branch is held by the node test on `setSize`, whose mutation goes red.
- **Fault Corpus with `?style=tidewater-georgian`.** The groups' order and counts are held to the
  dossier partition, fetched from the same route, and each heading to its own record.

Where the walk cannot judge a check, it reports it by name as could-not-evaluate and never passes
it. That happens when the elevation buttons are not live on the bench, or when no taxon has a
descent to click.

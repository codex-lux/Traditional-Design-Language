# WP-17.8 to WP-17.13: the library surfaces on the instrument

**Phase 17, 29 Oct 2026.** The handoff is `docs/ui-redesign-2026-10-29.md`, boards 6 to 13.

Details & Export, the Style Dossier, the Kit, the Fault Corpus, the family tree, the Elements and
Compare stand on the dark instrument now. Each one's row of `grounds.js`'s `SURFACE_GROUND` moved in
this commit, and its cards and plates are on vellum. **Proportions is the one surface of WP-17.13 not
moved**: it is still a vellum ground and still holds the ground ratchet's last 43 rows. That is the
next commit, because its order plate's drawing rows are held by `inks.test.mjs` by identity and have
to be moved one by one rather than by a sweep.

This one report covers six packages. They share the ground table, the glossary and
`docs/workbench.md`'s Phase 17 section, and they were verified by one walk.

## WP-17.9: the Style Dossier

**The period is drawn** (`dossier/periodBar.js`, drawn by `dossier/PeriodBar.jsx`):
- origin to floruit as a lead-in;
- the floruit as the body;
- the decline as a tail;
- each revival as its own bar.

**A date the record does not state is not drawn, and the bar says which.** `origin` is null on 27
styles and `decline_end` on 52. Interpolating either would be inventing a date the corpus declines to
give. Four records name the segments: `period-origin`, `period-floruit`, `period-decline` and
`period-revival`.

**The tells and the distinguished-from come before the description**, as the board orders them.

## WP-17.13: Elements and Compare

**Who binds a slot across every style** (`elements/bindingBar.js`, drawn in
`dossier/SlotPanel.jsx`).

The server change: `corpus.slot`'s `bindings` gains `extends` and `open` beside `specified` and
`forbidden`, and the total as `of`.
- `extends` is the specified styles whose slot a later node extended, read off the resolved kit.
- `open` is every style the other two do not name.

`test_slot_binding_bar.py` asserts on three slots that the parts partition all 164 styles. The bar
counts nothing itself.

| slot | specified | extended (of the specified) | forbidden | open | of |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cornice | 86 | 7 | 23 | 55 | 164 |
| dormer | 73 | 14 | 9 | 82 | 164 |
| shutter | 94 | 2 | 2 | 68 | 164 |

**A payload missing any of the four parts draws no bar**, because three parts of four would read as
the whole.

**Where two styles part** (`compare/parting.js`).
- Two styles part at the nearest node both cascades hold.
- A pair sharing none part at no node, and the page says so.
- The cascades ride on `/api/compare` (`compare_kits`'s new `cascades`). A source guard forbids
  Compare a second fetch, and `test_compare_route.py` asserts the payload's cascades equal the cascade
  route's.

## What was found

1. **The chronology exception had a reader and no surface.** The validator has warned about one edge
   on every build, and the tree drew it like any other. The fix is a port held to the original by a
   test rather than a second rule. Two spellings agreeing by test is this repository's standing
   compromise across a language boundary.
2. **The slot route's two parts were not a partition.** `specified` and `forbidden` left the open
   styles implicit, so a bar drawn from them would have needed the client to count styles. The route
   now states the whole, and the test is the partition.
3. **Compare could not fetch the cascades it needed.** An existing source guard refuses a second
   request from that surface, so the data went into the one payload it already reads.

# WP-12.5 — Overlays and modifiers, and a defect I reported that was not there

*9 September 2026. Brief: `docs/prd/phase-12-the-sheet-in-the-round.md` §§7.5, 7.6.*

The Round can carry the six analytic overlays and the three modifiers now, each computed in a
pure module, each composing with any view, each stated in the caption when active and
addressable in the URL.

**The finding worth reading first is the third one, because it is about this project's own
highest-yield technique producing a wrong answer.**

## II. The lift, and the two defects it found

Three rules lived inline in `Sheet.jsx`'s JSX as expressions inside the elements that drew them.
Surface ⑦ was the only reader, so that was tenable; a second surface transcribing a wash rule is
this repository's most-repeated defect. Both defects below are **real in the code, authorised by
the corpus's own records, and unreachable from it** — so both guards are driven by hand.

### 1. `daylight_multiplier || 2.25` turned a stated zero into the default

Three records — `closet`, `cold-room`, `linen-press` — state `daylight.depth_multiplier: 0`, a
**measured zero** meaning the room takes no daylight depth. `0 || 2.25` is 2.25, so a closet
would have been washed to 2.25 × its head exactly like a parlour.

**Measured rather than published at its alarming size.** Those types are placed **12 times across
6 plans** including both shipped ones — and **0 of the 12 draw a wash**, because none of them
declares a window and the overlay is gated on `litWalls`. Latent, not drawn. Publishing 12 would
have been three times its true size in the flattering direction for the fix, which is WP-11.14's
rule about the sixteen refused windows.

### 2. The privacy ramp is unbounded in both directions — and my first version of this was wrong

I wrote that `0.04 + ((rank - 1) / 4) * 0.20` drew rank 0 at an opacity of −0.01. **A mutation
proved it false**: reverting the whole function left the suite 24 of 24 green, because
`if (!rank) return null` catches 0 as falsy before the arithmetic runs and a test on the *value*
could not tell the two versions apart. The guard was about nothing.

Derived rather than re-read:

```
rank  −1  →  −0.06   a NEGATIVE opacity              not authorised
rank   0  →   null   dropped as though unranked      AUTHORISED by PRIVACY_BANDS
rank 1-5  →   0.04 … 0.24                            authorised, and unchanged here
rank   6  →   0.29   darker than the deepest room    not authorised
```

The falsy guard covers exactly one of the three by accident. The out-of-band ranks are what a
mutation can see, and they are what the test drives now. Rank 0 is the other half — authorised,
carried by no record, silently dropped as though unranked — and is told from a genuinely
unranked room only by `privacyRefusal` naming it, which is three states rather than two.
`oq/the-privacy-ramp-is-unbounded-and-does-not-cover-the-rank-its-own-band-admits`.

## III. The finding: LOOKING AT THE SHEET FOUND A DEFECT THAT WAS NOT THERE

CLAUDE.md records *"render the sheet and open it"* as the technique that found what nothing else
did, seven packages running. This package is the first time it produced a **false positive**, and
the correction is worth more than the feature.

I turned the privacy overlay on, took a screenshot, looked at it, and saw every room in the same
tone. I concluded the overlay drew nothing, and **committed and pushed that conclusion as
`46f612f`, whose subject reads "AND THEY DRAW NOTHING".** That claim is false.

What settled it was not a better look. It was two measurements:

**The instrument.** `setOverlays` returns what it built and the canvas states it as
`data-round-overlays`. The walk came back `privacy:24, wet:4` — and those reconcile exactly with
the record: 25 rooms carry a `privacy_rank` and one of them is the terrace, which takes no
rectangle (WP-11.10) and therefore stands in no `space`. Four rooms are wet. The geometry was
there all along.

**The control.** Sampling a horizontal band of pixels across the model, with the overlay off and
on:

```
overlay off : ONE floor tone      (236, 228, 207) × 254
overlay on  : THREE floor tones   (232,222,199)  (226,214,190)  (221,207,181)
```

and those three sit on the sepia ramp composited over that base, to within two values of eight
bits. The wash is 4% to 24% of `--sepia` over a warm cream ground; at plan scale in a downscaled
screenshot I could not see it, and I read "I cannot see it" as "it is not there".

**The rule this yields**, and it is WP-11.8's *a number read off the deliverable is not a
measurement of the change* wearing the other face: **looking at the sheet FINDS defects; it does
not ADJUDICATE them.** A picture is evidence that something is worth measuring. It is not
evidence of what is wrong, and on a translucent mark it is barely evidence at all — the absent
overlay and the faint one are the same picture. Measure against a control before naming a cause,
and never before publishing one.

**The half of the original finding that stands.** My six walk checks asserted the URL and the
caption and never that anything was drawn — so they would have passed over an overlay that was
genuinely absent. That was true when I wrote it and is still true; it is why the count attribute
exists. What was wrong was the diagnosis, not the gap in the guard. A translucent wash that draws
nothing looks exactly like one drawn faintly, and only a count separates them — which is exactly
why my own eyes could not, and is the same fact from the other side.

## IV. Three of the PRD's sentences the tree has overtaken

Corrected in the module rather than followed, as WP-12.4 had to correct two:

| §7.5 / §7.6 says | the tree |
|---|---|
| the grid draws bay lines from `scene.grid` | `scene.grid` is `{bays, module_ft}` — a count and a module. The lines are `derive.js::bayLines` |
| the drop-line count "matches `geometry_report.vertical`" | that field is a list of **English sentences**; `disclosures.transfers` regexes the number out of one. A count cannot match prose. `transferCount` reads the disclosure and never recomputes — WP-11.12's rule |
| an element explodes "along the axis of its `attached_to` face" | a scene element is `{id, role, rect}` and carries no `attached_to`. The direction is derived from the rects, and an element concentric with the main block is **refused by name** rather than sent somewhere plausible |

`oq/the-transfer-count-lives-only-inside-an-english-sentence` records the third reader that field
now has.

## V. What was deliberately not done

- **The daylight, wet and datum overlays are drawn but not measured against a control.** Only
  privacy was, because it is the one with a ramp and therefore the one whose *values* can be
  wrong rather than merely present. The counts are asserted for all of them.
- **`explode · elements` is inert on every shipped plan**, because no plan carries a `block` tag —
  `explodeOffsets` returns `one element — nothing to separate` and the caption says so. The
  multi-element path is driven by a hand-built fixture at negative x, on WP-11.15's precedent.
- **The cut handle is a chip, not a drag.** §7.6 asks for a handle on the plate's margin with a
  keyboard nudge; the axis chips select a plane and `x`/`y` take a fixed 20 ft. Dragging it is
  real work in the pointer layer and it is not here.
- **No overlay is drawn on the flat plates.** They remain what they were.

# WP-14.5: the source leg, the record held to its own notes, and the plates wanted

*Phase 14, the ink held to its plates. Built 27 September 2026. The code is one commit, and this
report and its verification are the commit after it.*

WP-14.1 through WP-14.4 held the ink to the record. This package holds the record to what it was
transcribed from, as far as that can be done from here.

Every host the order packs cite refused a CONNECT from this container when it was re-probed today:
archive.org, loc.gov, HathiTrust, Wikimedia and polito.it, 403 from the proxy each time. So no
figure can be held against its plate. Decision 3 (27 Sep) follows from that: **internal checks
now, and a list of the plates a person must fetch.**

The rule is the phase's, applied one link upstream: **say what can be said without the plate,
name the plate for what cannot, and approve nothing.**

## I. What was built

### 1. The figures the notes state, held to the figures the records carry (N0, N1, N3)

**The population is every note that states a number:**

- the 293 member notes in the 26 order packs that state one;
- the 107 notes on measured kit parameters that the elevation reads and that state one;
- the module notes of all 18 overlays.

**How they were read.** Six agents, working to one protocol, read them. For each note an agent
wrote either of two things:

- what the note states about which recorded figure, as an expression over the record's own names,
  with the verbatim quote it rests on and a sentence saying how it was read;
- or why it states nothing about a recorded figure (a date, a plate number, or a member the record
  does not carry).

**How the lead verified them.**

- Every quote was checked to be a verbatim substring of its note. That check is now census N0,
  run on every build.
- Every expression was evaluated over the record's own numbers.
- Every disagreement was read against its note by hand.

That review changed eight rows:

- **Dropped from `benjamin-corinthian`: "thirty four".** The note corrects its own misprint.
- **Dropped from `gibbs-ionic`: "a third larger".** It is a loose gloss beside the stated 18
  against 12.
- **Dropped from `greek-classical`: its examples as bounds.** They are examples, not bounds.
- **Dropped from `new-england-colonial`: the ratio.** It needs a pairing the note leaves open.
- **Dropped from `mission-revival` and `pueblo-revival`: the literal-label claims.**
  - A "1:X (height:width)" label reads X as a ratio of width to height, which is backwards.
  - The notation turned out to be consistent across three kits, so no kit note was edited and the
    claims were dropped instead.
- **Added to `palladio-corinthian`: two claims about other packs.** Its base note states what
  Palladio gives the Tuscan and the Doric base. Each claim is judged over its target's own record
  and units.
- **Dropped from `storybook`: "6".** It is one half of an either-or, so a single value would keep
  one alternative and silently drop the other. **The row then kept the other half, "7", which is
  the same defect.** WP-14.6's audit found it, and this report's commit makes the row a stated
  non-claim.

The result is `build/note_figures.json`. `build/plate_review.py` judges it, and it asserts nothing
except the reading.

- **Member notes (N1).**
  - 668 claims, of which 652 agree.
  - 265 notes carry a claim, and 14 of them disagree.
  - 28 notes state a number that is not about a recorded figure, and each says why.
- **Kit notes (N3), after the storybook correction.**
  - 99 claims, of which 90 agree.
  - 65 parameters carry a claim, and 6 of them disagree.
  - 42 state a number that is not about the parameter.
  - At the package's own commit these read 100, 91, 66 and 41.

**Two figures agree** when they are within 0.01 of a part (or of the parameter's unit) or half a
per cent, whichever is larger. That is the notes' own precision, not a fudge: a note states a figure
to a sixteenth or a fraction of a part, and the record carries four decimal places.

**N1 says "states", not "quotes from its authority".** Many of these figures are the transcriber's
own arithmetic on the author's words. Palladio's Ionic architrave *"three of which are given to the
first fascia"* is read as 3/12 of four fifths. A note is not a plate. An `agrees` says the record
and the sentence beside it agree. Whether either agrees with the plate is `SOURCE: COULD NOT
EVALUATE`.

**One record was corrected, because its own note proved it.**

- `craftsman`'s window casing carried 4 in for the leg and 6 in for the head, which are the
  nominal sizes.
- Its note cites the trim-craftsman pack, which states the dressed sizes: *"1x4 (3 1/2), 1x6
  (5 1/2)"* and *"HEAD CASING HEIGHT. Five and a half inches."* Both parameters now carry 3.5 and
  5.5, sourced to that pack.
- The two leave `build/measured_unsourced_grandfathered.json` through its one-way door:
  `measured_unsourced` 536 → 534, and `measured_unsourced_read` 304 → 302.

**The 14 member disagreements are not fixed, and each is pinned by identity.** They are set out in
`oq/a-members-note-states-a-figure-its-record-does-not-carry`, in five shapes:

1. two authorities in one note presented as agreeing (Vignola's Doric base and capital, his Tuscan
   capital);
2. a note whose own arithmetic contradicts its words (Vignola's Corinthian bell in "three equal
   sixths" of a diameter);
3. a division the note quotes and the stack does not carry (Gibbs's Ionic capital);
4. a projection rule quoted and not followed (Gibbs's architraves and pedestal caps);
5. a figure stated in words over an illegible plate (Benjamin's Doric annulets).

The fourteenth, Palladio's Doric base, has its own question:
`oq/four-attic-bases-project-a-third-of-a-diameter`. Four records carry a plinth projecting a third
of a diameter where the corpus's account of Palladio gives a sixth.

**None of them meets OQ 81's bar**, which is that the corpus's own quotation proves the fix. Each
rests on a reading:

- which member a phrase names;
- what a projection is measured from;
- whether a sixth is of the lower diameter or the upper;
- whether a phrase is the author's words or the transcriber's slip.

**The six kit disagreements are one thing.** A note quotes millimetres, and the parameter carries
the conversion rounded to a tenth of a foot or a whole inch.
`oq/a-metric-source-figure-is-carried-rounded` asks whether that is a transcription to correct or a precision to state. The tolerance was
not widened to make them agree, because loosening a guard until the reds go away is the move this
corpus refuses.

### 2. The conversion an overlay applies, held to the factor its own module note states (N2)

**Each overlay's module note states the conversion in words.** For example:

- Chambers: *"PART figures must be multiplied by 30/18"*.
- Palladio: *"multiply any inherited vignola-ionic part-value by 10/6"*.
- Palladio: *"a module figure here is half the Vignola figure"*.

**The engine never reads those sentences.** It takes its factor from a different record: the
module's numeric `diameters` on 25 of the 26 packs, and the module's name only on `moorish-arch`,
which states no number. The two are therefore independent. (The first version of this paragraph
said the engine reads the NAME, which is true of one pack; WP-14.6's audit read the function.)

**The check reads the engine's output, not its formula.** For every assembly an overlay inherits
whole, it compares each figure against the base's figure times the stated factor. **All 18 overlays
agree, over 968 figures.** No test held that population when this was committed, so dropping a
field from the comparison could halve it with the census unmoved; WP-14.6 pins it.

A shaft re-sized to the overlay's own column (WP-14.2) is a derivation, not a conversion. The engine
marks it, and its body's height is not read.

### 3. The plates wanted (`Plan Examples/Plates/WANTED.md`)

`build/gen_plate_wants.py` generates it, and `--check` holds it current. It lists five classes, in
the order a person's time is best spent:

| class | what it is | count |
|---|---|---:|
| 0 | a figure a note in the corpus disputes (census N1's pinned disagreements, one row per figure) | 15 |
| 1 | an assembly with no projection published at all, drawn as a ghost at the naked on every surface since WP-14.2 | 21 |
| 2 | a member with no projection, in an assembly that publishes the others | 23 |
| 3 | a height the transcriber apportioned (the note's own word) | 110 |
| 4 | another low-confidence member | 14 |

**Where each row is listed.**

- A row goes under the pack that STATES the assembly, so a gap an overlay inherits from Vignola is
  listed once, under Vignola.
- A note's claim about another pack's record is listed under that pack. Palladio's Corinthian note
  on the Doric base is listed under the Doric.
- A summary entablature whose members are the pack's own detailed assemblies is not a gap. This
  rule is read from the RESOLVED pack, because Palladio states his entablature's division and
  inherits the detail it divides.

**The books** are ordered by their gaps. Palladio comes first, with 13 wholly unpublished
assemblies.

**What each book's section gives:**

- the scan URL the packs cite;
- the folder to put the plates in;
- the open questions that plate closes (OQ 7–10);
- the rights discipline, on the HABS list's precedent: the holding library's rights statement is
  copied verbatim, and no `license` field is ever written.

**Nothing in it claims a host was reached.** It names the five hosts and the day they refused.

### 4. Each plate's review note says what was checked (P14, P15)

**What the note used to say.** It read *"NOT reviewed: nobody has looked at this plate"* and nothing
else. That was true, and it gave a reader nothing to act on.

**What `render_profile.py --write` now files on each of the 73 plates:**

- **The internal verdict:**
  - the members sum to the assembly;
  - the figures notes state about the record the plate draws;
  - the conversion of anything it inherits;
  - the pack's invariants.
- **`SOURCE: COULD NOT EVALUATE`**, with the plate named.
- **`NOT approved`.**

The status stays `sourced`, because approving a plate is a person's act.

**57 plates read AGREES and 16 read DISAGREES.**

- Seven of the 16 draw a disputed figure of their own pack.
- Nine are overlays that draw one they inherit. Benjamin's Tuscan capital draws Vignola's abacus,
  and its note says so in Vignola's name and parts.

**Census P14 holds the manifest to what `render_profile.review_note` composes NOW.** A note that
says `agrees` is therefore the verdict the record earns today, not the one it earned when the plate
was last written.

**Census P15 holds each plate's alt text to its record.** `gen_assets.py` writes that text once,
from the engine, and nothing rewrites it. All 73 agree.

## III. What the instrument got wrong

- **A claim counted where its note sat, not on the plate whose record it judged.** This was found
  by reading the notes the first `--write` produced.
  - Palladio's Corinthian base note disputes the Doric base's 20 minutes.
  - The Corinthian plate read DISAGREES over a record nobody disputes.
  - The Doric plate, which draws the 20, read AGREES. That was a false `agrees` in the one field a
    reader is told to trust.
  - A claim now counts on the plate whose record it judges. Two mutations guard it, one for each
    direction.
- **An inherited figure's detail was in another pack's parts and did not say so.** "The record 5",
  on Benjamin's Tuscan capital, was Vignola's 5 parts. The detail now names the stating pack.
- **A quote was cut mid-phrase and nothing marked the cut.** `_clip` cuts at a word and marks the
  elision, which is WP-11.5's rule for a quoted basis.
- **The wanted list's owner filter could never be false.**
  - `rows()` iterates each pack's own file, and `assembly_owner` returns the pack for every assembly
    in it. The mutation deleting the filter was green.
  - The filter is gone, and the guard is now on the real route: iterating the resolved pack, which
    lists an overlay's inherited gaps, turns three tests red.
- **A summary entablature was read as a gap.** Palladio states his entablature's division and
  inherits the detailed architrave, frieze and cornice from Vignola. Reading only his own file
  called the division four wholly unpublished assemblies. It reads the resolved pack now.
- **A cross-pack claim's judgment had no driven test**, and the mutation judging it over the
  wrong pack was green. There is a driven test now.
- **The first draft of the four-Attic-bases question carried a superlative its measurement did not
  support.** My probe had misread `palladio-ionic`'s "no relief drawn". The superlative was removed
  after re-measuring.
- **One mutation run reported nothing.** The spec gave its command as a string where the harness
  wants a list, so it raised before running anything. It restored the file byte for byte, as its
  `finally` guarantees. That was a harness error, not a result, and the run was repeated.

## IV. What was deliberately not done

- **No plate was approved.** `sourced` stays, because approving a plate is a person's act.
- **No figure was re-kinded or re-sourced on the strength of a note**, except craftsman's casing,
  whose note cites the pack that states the dressed size.
- **None of the 14 member disagreements was fixed**, and none of the six roundings. Both are open
  questions, and all 20 are pinned.
- **The Ionic's and Corinthian's Gibbs pedestal caps are not claimed.** Their notes do not quote the
  two-thirds rule, and a claim written into the table for them would be the table's reading passed
  off as a note's. The question records them.
- **No host was reached, and nothing claims one was.**

carries WP-14.4 as well: **3 of 53 checks failed, 78 min 25 s. None of the reds is this

- The server suite's third 429 is WP-14.4's too.

## VI. What the phase's adversarial audit found in this package

WP-14.6 audited this package before this report was committed. What it found is stated here and
not folded silently into the text above. The data and prose corrections are in this report's
commit, and the guards in WP-14.6.

- **Three of the plate reviewer's verdict branches were not driven**, against this report's and
  the test file's claim that every branch was. The corpus reaches none of them, so mutations
  making each wrong were green, and the digest of all 73 review notes was unchanged under each:
  - an invariant the engine cannot evaluate counted as held, which is the unjudged-as-passed
    collapse in the note P14 files on every plate;
  - a stale table entry that stopped counting against its plate;
  - a members-sum check that always agrees.

  WP-14.6 plants each.
- **Four of the 57 AGREES review notes carry a check that could not evaluate.** The headline reads
  AGREES whenever one check agrees and none disagrees. A check that could not be judged is not a
  check that agreed, and the headline must say so; WP-14.6 makes it.
- **N2's 968 figures were held by nothing.** A mutation dropping `projection_parts` from the
  comparison cut them to 541, with all 18 overlays still agreeing and the census unmoved. WP-14.6
  pins the count.
- **"Palladio comes first" was held only by `--check`.** A generator listing the books fewest gaps
  first failed the currency test and passed all nine semantic ones. WP-14.6 adds the test that the
  first book has the most wholly unpublished assemblies.
- **Corrected in this report's commit:**
  - the storybook row that kept the "7" of an either-or it had dropped the "6" of;
  - N2's account of where the engine gets its factor (the module's numeric `diameters` on 25 of 26
    packs, not its name);
  - the claim that only the Composite's note quotes Gibbs's two-to-three rule (the Tuscan's does
    too, and follows it; the three short caps are short by 1.1, 1 and 1 parts);
  - the 536 still standing in CLAUDE.md, the plan, `docs/precedents.md` and the question it
    bounds. Each is 534 now, and `check_counts.py` reads all four, 119 -> 123 claims.

## Correction (WP-14.6, 27 Sep 2026): the kit population was six short

`plate_review.kit_params` chose which measured kit notes state a figure by `DIGIT.search`. The
member notes were read by `NUMBER`, which also matches a figure written in words. Six kit notes
state theirs that way ("one-third", "two feet", and so on) and were never read. WP-14.6's audit
found the difference between the two readers, and the kit reader takes `NUMBER` now. The six were
read into `build/note_figures.json` by the same protocol, and the figures above move as follows.
They were re-derived from `plate_review.kit_entries()`, not added up.

- **The kit population:** 107 notes → **113**. N0's rows: 418 → **424**.
- **N3:** 65 parameters carrying a claim → **68**, of which 59 → **62** agree. **The 6
  disagreements do not move.** The three new claims all agree:
  - `andalusian-spanish-revival`'s one-third reveal ratio;
  - `monterey-colonial`'s 24 in reveal;
  - `swiss-chalet`'s three to six lights a band, read as two claims, lo and hi.
- **Claims:** 99 → **103**, and 90 → **94** agree.
- **Notes stating a number that is not about the parameter:** 42 → **45**, each with its why. The
  three are `georgian-colonial-american`'s dormer sash pattern and two of `tidewater-georgian`'s
  reveal parameters.

The mutation that puts `DIGIT` back is red: the six table rows it orphans read as stale in N0.

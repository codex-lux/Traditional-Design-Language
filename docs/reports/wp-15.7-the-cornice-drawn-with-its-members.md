# WP-15.7 — the cornice drawn with its members

*Phase 15, the plates drawn. Built 28 September 2026.*

Lucas, of the drawn Tidewater front (27 September): **"the cornice not being represented on this
export."** The face drew the frieze and the cornice as ONE rectangle, 36.8 in deep and 10.5 in proud
of the wall at every height, with a single line inside it. The DXF drew the same rectangle. So:

- the eight members `elevation.eave_cornice` dimensions were drawn on the inset beside the face and
  nowhere on the face itself: bed mould, fillet, modillion band, its cap, corona, listel, cymatium
  and fillet;
- the FRIEZE stood 10.5 in out from a wall its own record says it is flush with. facade-classical's
  `elevation` assembly states the frieze `projection_parts` 0.0, as it states both storeys, and the
  one rectangle drew it at the cornice's projection.

**Now the face draws the eave as its record states it.** The frieze is drawn flush. The cornice's
box is drawn at the one reading of the band's projection. Inside the box, a line marks every member
division the record states, each naming the member above it. The sheet and the DXF draw these marks
from one function, and a census row holds each to the record.

## I. What was built

### 1. `elevation.cornice_marks`, the one spelling

It follows the precedent of `stack_marks` (WP-15.5): face feet (`u` from the face's left edge, `h`
above grade), so both surfaces draw the same numbers.

- **`frieze`**: the band from the wall head to the cornice's springing, at `frieze_projection_in`.
  That is a new field on `eave_cornice`, dimensioned by the engine off facade-classical's own frieze
  member at the function's own module, so it converts exactly as the members do. Where no record
  states it, it is None, and the band is drawn flush and SAID (`FRIEZE DRAWN FLUSH WITH THE WALL`).
- **`cornice`**: the box from the springing to the true eave, at `cornice_band_projection_in`, the
  reading the audit of Phase 14 made one.
- **`members`**: each member's own band inside the box, end to end from the springing to the true
  eave.
- **`teeth`**: the toothed band's layout (`profiles.repeat_positions`), or the band drawn solid with
  the reason.
- **`notes`**: the sheet's sentences, which the DXF now writes too. The modillion one is kept byte
  for byte.

### 2. The sheet (`render_elevation`)

- The frieze is its own rectangle (`bd fz`), and the box keeps its class (`bd w-prof`) so census V2
  reads it as before.
- A line marks each division (`cm`), carrying `data-member`.
- Both rectangles are written from their rounded EDGES (`_rect_edges`, WP-14.3), because the
  frieze's top IS the cornice's soffit.
- The shade line under the cornice now falls at the cornice's own soffit, where its shadow lands on
  the frieze. It used to fall at the frieze's foot.
- The inset's caption now says which surface draws which projection: *"THE FACE DRAWS THE ENVELOPE'S,
  THIS PROFILE THE ORDER'S (OQ 79)"*.

### 3. The DXF (`export_dxf`)

- The frieze gets its own layer (`TDL-ELEV-FRIEZE`), carrying its projection and the reason where
  it is unstated.
- The box stays one polyline on `TDL-ELEV-CORNICE`, carrying its projection and its order pack.
- A line marks each division on `TDL-ELEV-CORNICE-MEMBER`, with the member's id and profile in
  XDATA.
- The teeth, where they can be laid, go on `TDL-ELEV-CORNICE-TOOTH`.
- The cornice's sentences are written with the sheet's others. This file had written only the
  flush-band one, on a line of its own.

### 4. `profiles.repeat_positions`: two interleaved rows, refused

This function was found by reading at WP-15.6 and measured here before it was changed. Given the bay
centres as anchors, it filled both ways from every anchor and stopped only near another anchor.
Between two anchors that are not a whole number of pitches apart, it therefore laid two rows. On
the Tidewater front at the record's own 25.58 in pitch, it laid 33 teeth where one row holds 22,
in pairs 5.68 in apart.

It now lays the row between two anchors only where they stand a whole number of pitches apart,
within a hundredth of an inch (`_PITCH_TOL_IN`; Gibbs's rule says "exactly"). Otherwise it
REFUSES, with the figure: *"its anchors 108.00 in apart are 4.22 pitches of 25.58 in, so no tooth
can centre on each at the stated pitch"*. Meeting the rule there would mean changing the pitch the
record states, and that is a judgment. Anchors that all fall off the run now lay a centred band;
before, they returned `solid: False` with no teeth, a band claimed as laid and drawn empty.

### 5. Census V25 and X4 (74 checks)

- **V25**, on every face of every plan and every style's front (85 rows), reads each band back to
  the face's own feet. It holds:
  - the frieze's box against facade-classical's frieze member, READ OFF THE PACK FILE, not
    `eave_cornice`'s new field (R4's trap);
  - the cornice's box against the stated band projection;
  - every division line against the member it names, and no line the record does not state;
  - the legend's two FLUSH sentences, both directions;
  - PAINT ORDER: a stack that overlaps the cornice is painted over it where it stands in front of
    the face and under it where it stands behind. On the Tidewater gable ends the exterior stack
    stands in front of the wall and the cornice returns against it; on the front, the same stack is
    behind the cornice's return at the corner.
- **X4**, on every face (44 rows), holds the DXF's frieze, box and division lines to the SVG's ink,
  member by member. Where the SVG draws no division the record states, X4 reports COULD NOT
  EVALUATE, because two drawings that both draw nothing are not in agreement.

## II. What was found

1. **The frieze was drawn 10.5 in proud of a wall its record says it is flush with**, on every face
   the elevation draws. facade-classical states it flush, the order pack's own frieze records a
   projection of 0, and the 3D scene has drawn it flush since WP-12.6 (*"the Gibbs frieze really is
   flush with the naked"*). The sheet and the DXF had one rectangle for two bands.
2. **The members were drawn on one surface of the sheet and not the other.** V13 held the inset to
   the eight members. Nothing held the face, so the census read the flat band exactly as it read the
   inset's profile. **V25 disagrees on 85 of 85 rows on the parent's code, and agrees on 85 of 85
   here.**
3. **X4 AGREED on 44 of 44 rows on the parent's code**, because both surfaces drew the same one
   box. Parity between two drawings that draw nothing is not agreement, so X4 reports COULD NOT
   EVALUATE there now (44 of 44) and V25 is the row that says why. It is the shape of *"a guard's
   reference must not be what the subject serves"*, met from the other side: two subjects agreeing
   with each other.
4. **A test certified the interleaving.** WP-14.3's
   `test_the_modillions_centre_on_the_bays_they_stand_over` drove a 6 in tooth at the record's pitch
   over bays 4.22 pitches apart, and asserted more than five teeth and a tooth over every bay. Both
   held, over two interleaved rows. It is re-cut:
   - a whole-pitch case (a quarter of the bay), holding every gap to the pitch;
   - a refusal case at the record's own pitch.

   **Under the parent's layout verbatim, the whole-pitch test stays GREEN**: at an exact pitch the
   old code's de-duplication does lay one row. Only the refusal tests go red, which is why the pair
   exists.
5. **No face this corpus draws has its bays a whole number of modillion pitches apart.** Measured
   over all 44 faces, the ratio of bay spacing to pitch runs from 3.886 to 6.989, and the nearest to
   a whole number are 5.987 (bad-03's long faces) and 6.989 (bad-02's gable ends). So Gibbs's rule
   (*"always the centre of a Modillion exactly over the centre of each column"*) cannot hold at the
   stated pitch on any shipped front. It is moot today, because no pack states a tooth width and
   every band is drawn solid and said. *(Corrected 28 Sep 2026, §V.1: 26 of the order packs' 39
   toothed members do state a width. The one every drawn elevation reads, gibbs-ionic's
   `corn_modillion`, states none, and that is why it is moot.)* It will not stay moot: a width read
   off Gibbs's plate (WP-15.3) will meet every front at a pitch that does not fit.
6. **The first draft's member lines were in the file and not on the sheet.** The `cm` class had no
   stroke rule, and a line with no stroke draws nothing. Looking at the rasterized eave found it.
   The assertion that a division line carries ink (a stroke and a width) is in the tests now, and
   the mutation removing the rule is red on it.
7. **The gable end draws a full cornice whatever the kit says of the return.** All 41 drawn styles
   draw the band the whole width of the gable end:
   - 11 styles' resolved kits FORBID `cornice_return`, two by their own ban and nine inherited
     (decision 4's shape);
   - 3 make `none` canonical;
   - 12 state a return 12 to 24 in deep, whose own note adds that Southern brick houses often
     return the cornice all the way round.

   Raised as `oq/the-gable-end-draws-a-full-cornice-whatever-the-kit-says-of-the-return`, with the
   rake, which is not modelled at all.
8. **"The cornice returns against the stack" is served by paint order on the one plan that has a
   gable stack.** The Tidewater stacks stand 6 to 7 ft from the corners, beyond the kit's 12 to
   24 in return. On the gable face the band runs behind the stack and the stack is painted over it,
   which V25 now holds in both directions.
9. **OQ 79 is unchanged, and the drawing is honest about it.** The face draws the members' HEIGHTS
   and not their projections, because the projections are that question. Ten of the 159 resolved
   kits bind the envelope's rule as their own `cornice.projection_in`: the American Georgian and
   Federal line, Tidewater's among them, and none of the English Georgian nodes. That is evidence
   for the reading OQ 79 calls most likely, and it is recorded in OQ 79's amendment. It is not a
   ruling.
10. **CI has been red on the fidelity doc since at least the merge, and no attribution saw it.**
    Reading run 123's reds by id, rather than by shard colour, found
    `test_svg_census.py::TestTheDocIsTheCensus::test_the_tables_in_docs_fidelity_are_current` red
    in CI on runs 121, 122 and 123, and green in every local build. The doc is generated where
    `ezdxf` is installed; CI's corpus shards do not have it, so the X rows read one "not installed"
    row there. Every earlier attribution of PR #40's CI mapped the local reds onto the red shards,
    and each shard that carried this one was already red for another test. It is CLAUDE.md's *"a
    test already red for cause A cannot report cause B"*, one level up. Fixed in its own commit:
    - a check this environment cannot run at all, every row could-not-evaluate for want of a tool,
      keeps the doc's own line (`svg_census.unevaluable_here`);
    - a second test names those checks as a SKIP, never a pass.

    With `ezdxf` withheld to stand in for a CI shard, in a git worktree, the fix passes. The line
    removed, it fails on exactly CI's X1 and X2 lines. Removing the skip is not caught, and
    cannot be: a skip is the report, and no test can hold a sibling to having been skipped.

## III. What was deliberately not done

- **The order's profile is not drawn at the face's corners.** That would choose between the two
  projections OQ 79 records, and the box keeps the envelope's figure, as it always has.
- **No modillion is drawn.** The record states the pitch (27 parts, *"three quarters of a
  diameter"*) and not the width. Gibbs's note divides the SOFFIT between modillions and does not
  size the modillion, so the width is a plate figure, WP-15.3's.
- **The gable end is not changed**, and the rake is not drawn. Both go to the new question.
- **Decision 4 is not applied.** The eleven styles whose kits forbid the return still draw one.
- **The 3D scene is not touched.** It builds its roof at the roof record's eave with no frieze or
  cornice band, which is census V19's standing disagreement and a ruling
  (`oq/the-elevation-stands-its-roof-on-a-cornice-band-no-other-surface-draws`).

## V. Corrections, 28 September 2026 (the audit of Phase 15, WP-15.8)

1. **"No pack states a tooth width" is too broad.** Over the 25 order packs with a column, resolved,
   39 members carry a toothed profile (dentil, modillion, mutule or triglyph, the elevation's own
   `TOOTHED_PROFILES`), and 26 of them state `width_parts`: vignola-corinthian's modillion at 8
   parts, benjamin-ionic's at 10.5 and gibbs-composite's dentil at 2.571 among them. (The audit
   published 13 of 29. That figure could not be reproduced under any population tried here, so it
   is not repeated.) What is true, and what the
   "unreachable" conclusion needs, is narrower: all 52 elevations this corpus draws (11 plans and the
   41 swept styles) read `gibbs-ionic`'s `corn_modillion`, and it states no width. The refusal becomes
   reachable the day an elevation resolves another order pack, as well as the day a width is read off
   Gibbs's plate. The same sentence stood in CLAUDE.md and PLAN-OF-ACTION.md, and is corrected
   there too. Commit `6f2291d`'s message carries it and cannot be changed.
2. **The CI run that carried this package.** `6f2291d` was pushed with `3b083d9`, so PR #40's run
   124 is its first CI. Read by test id, every corpus red there was red before this package: the
   gate's rows, `test_solver`, the three family-georgian composer rows, `test_parti_composability`,
   the native-set score row and `check_partis.py`, all of which the WP-15.6 whole build attributes;

# WP-4.2, wave B — filling all 132 style/variant kits, and closing WP-4.2

*24 August 2026. Closes WP-4.2 (opens Phase 4, WP-4.1 committed f5ee927). Companion to `docs/reports/wp-4.2-family-kits.md` (wave A: the family cascade mechanism and the 27 family kits), `docs/inheritance.md` (the resolution-order rules this wave exercised at scale), and `docs/open-questions.md` OQ 20 (updated by this pass with the data the plan asked for).*

## Scope and method

Wave A built the mechanism (family nodes participate in the cascade) and authored the 27 family kits. Wave B's job, per `PLAN-OF-ACTION.md`'s own task text, was the remaining two tiers: "the 90 styles (using `extends` against the family wherever the style adds rather than restates), then the 42 variants as 10-30 overrides" — 129 files, since 3 (`georgian-colonial-american`, `tidewater-georgian`, `english-georgian`) were already populated exemplars predating this work package.

Run as two sub-waves of parallel batches, mirroring the grouping WP-1.1 and WP-4.1 already established for this corpus (batched by family, one batch per family or small cluster of related families):

- **Sub-wave 1** (7 batches, 72 nodes, committed `f459742`): eclectic-revivals, american-colonial, victorian, romantic-revivals, early-republic, english-classical.
- **Sub-wave 2** (6 batches, 57 nodes, committed `0076db6`): american-arts-and-crafts, american-folk-vernacular, continental-baroque-neoclassical, british-picturesque, low-countries-vernacular, tudor-jacobean, renaissance-classical, medieval-british, mid-century-traditional, french-vernacular, contemporary-traditional, germanic-vernacular, nordic-alpine-vernacular, antique-classical, british-vernacular, spanish-classical, mediterranean-vernacular, iberian-islamic, colonial-iberian-americas, british-arts-and-crafts, iberian-vernacular.

Every batch was given the same instruction, in this order, for every assigned node: run `resolve_kit.py <id> --verbose` first to see what the family-plus-lineage cascade already resolves (wave A's own report showed this can be 91 of 95 slots before a style writes a single delta of its own); read the node's own `styles/<id>.json` text, not the family's summary prose, which several batches in both waves caught overclaiming a trait one of its own members contradicts; add `extends`/`specified`/`forbidden` deltas only for content genuinely new or different from what already resolves; `forbidden` where the node's own text rules something out; every dimensional parameter carries a `unit` and a `kind` (`measured`/`derived`/`editorial`/`invented`/`code`); `garage_strategy` specified on every contemporary-buildable node, honestly `invented: true` where (as is universal) no historical precedent exists; no fabricated sources, dates, or measurements — an unsupported call is `kind: "editorial"` and/or `judgment: true`, stated as such in the note, never presented as documented fact.

Before this work package, the overwhelming majority of that chain would have resolved to nothing: an empty kit contributing zero content, present in the chain but invisible in practice. `resolve_kit.py`'s own provenance trace now shows real multi-level attribution the whole way down, which is the acceptance criterion PLAN-OF-ACTION.md names directly ("`resolve_kit` shows multi-level provenance on every variant").

## OQ 20, answered with data

OQ 20 asked whether the cascade actually works three levels deep, and — the harder question — how many merge problems appear once real content is present at every level, not just an empty skeleton sitting in the chain. Wave A answered the first half with zero merge problems found, but at only one real chain (`tidewater-georgian → georgian-colonial-american → american-colonial → english-georgian`) and explicitly flagged the fuller answer as still open. Wave B ran the same question corpus-wide — 129 nodes, thousands of cascade positions — and found three real, distinct kinds of merge problem, all fixed, none requiring a change to the mechanism itself:

**1. Deep classical content leaking into non-classical branches via a real but narrow lineage edge.** A `composition_parti` claim of `strict-bilateral-symmetry` (from `antique-classical`, at the root of the classical lineage) and a `ceiling_height_rule` derived-from-proportion rule (from `renaissance-classical`) each reached more than a dozen nodes across five unrelated families — vernacular, Gothic, Moorish, log-construction branches with no classical pretensions at all — via a real `descends_from`/`regional_of` edge that exists for a legitimate, narrow reason (a shared construction technique, a documented historical influence) but, once populated, carries the *donor's entire specified content* for a slot, not just the aspect the edge was drawn for. Found and fixed on: `italian-villa-vernacular`, `tuscan-vernacular`, `moorish-andalusian`, `mudejar`, `andalusian-courtyard-vernacular`, `mexican-colonial`, `mexican-hacienda`, `arts-and-crafts-british`, `tudor`, `english-gothic`, `norman-romanesque-english`, `italian-baroque`, `palladian`, `elizabethan`, `jacobean` — fifteen nodes, one recurring root cause.

**2. `hybridizes_with` edges transmitting a donor's whole kit instead of the one trait they were drawn for.** The corpus uses `hybridizes_with` to record a real, narrow influence — Continental Baroque/Neoclassical's massing owing something to French vernacular manor forms; British Picturesque's construction-trades descent from English Georgian; Low Countries Vernacular's thatching technique or ornament pattern-books shared with Germanic Vernacular. Because the cascade mechanism (correctly, by design) has no way to partition a donor kit by *which* aspect of practice an edge was meant to carry, a populated donor kit now transmits everything it specifies — a full classical-order forbid, an entire timber-frame construction system, a Mediterranean daylight strategy — regardless of how narrow the edge's real justification was. Found on 9+ nodes in the `continental-baroque-neoclassical` / `british-picturesque` / `low-countries-vernacular` cluster alone (`beaux-arts-french`, `french-baroque`, `french-neoclassical`, `french-renaissance-chateau`, `gothic-revival-british`, `cape-dutch`, `dutch-urban-gable-house`), and independently in `north-german-hall-house` and `swiss-chalet`. Every instance fixed on the affected node's own kit file.

**3. A cross-batch cascade-position collision — real, and specific to running kit-fill in parallel.** `french-eclectic` (sub-wave 1) used `extends` on `garage_strategy`, assuming `tudor-revival` was the nearest ancestor that specifies that slot — true when sub-wave 1 wrote it. Sub-wave 2, running concurrently, gave `beaux-arts-french` (also in `french-eclectic`'s cascade, and nearer) its own `specified` `garage_strategy` binding. `check_kits.py`'s dangling-diff check correctly caught the resulting broken `replace` op after both sub-waves landed. This is not a version of merge problems 1 or 2 above — it is a structural property of authoring `extends` deltas against a specific ancestor's content while other batches are populating other ancestors in the same cascade at the same time, and it is the reason this pass re-verified every batch's self-report against a corpus-wide `check_kits.py`/`validate.py` run rather than trusting any individual batch's "0 errors" claim. Fixed by converting `french-eclectic.garage_strategy` to a full `specified` override that does not depend on which ancestor happens to be nearest.

None of these three required a mechanism change. All three are documented here as real content facts about a corpus this densely cross-referenced, not defects in the family-cascade design wave A built — the same distinction wave A's own report drew between "the mechanism is sound" and "the corpus's existing lineage edges may still contain provenance the mechanism now makes visible for the first time." Wave B is the second half of that sentence, delivered with real numbers: **~26 real merge problems found and fixed across 129 style/variant kits (roughly one in five), zero of them mechanism failures, all traceable to specific lineage/hybridization edges whose narrow original intent the cascade's populated-content model cannot express.**

## What's still open

- The `hybridizes_with`-transmits-everything limitation (merge problem 2) is a real, general property of the mechanism, not fixed at the mechanism level in this pass — only patched node-by-node where it was found. A future pass could give `hybridizes_with` edges (and possibly `descends_from`/`regional_of` too) a slot-scope allowlist, so an edge drawn for one narrow reason cannot silently carry unrelated bindings. Not attempted here because it would be a schema/mechanism change affecting every populated kit's resolution, and this work package's job was to fill kits against the existing mechanism, not redesign it mid-fill.
- `english-georgian`'s two pre-existing `code_conflict` warnings (cornice, window_type — no `code_ref`) remain unaddressed; out of scope for this pass, unrelated to the family cascade.
- `mexican-colonial`'s date-predates-ancestor warning remains unaddressed; a genuine content question (is the lineage edge or one of the two dates wrong?) for a future pass, not a WP-4.2 concern.
- WP-4.3 (garage strategy as its own work package) is substantially pre-empted by this pass — every contemporary-buildable node touched by wave A or wave B already carries a `garage_strategy` binding, honestly flagged `invented: true` where no precedent exists. A future WP-4.3 pass should audit for completeness (any contemporary-buildable node wave B didn't touch) and build the `garage`/`garage-and-hyphen` catalog entries PLAN-OF-ACTION.md's WP-4.3 task text also asks for, rather than re-author strategies that already exist.

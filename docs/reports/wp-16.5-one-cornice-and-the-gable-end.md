# WP-16.5 — One cornice, and the gable end draws what the kit says of the return

*Phase 16, 30 September 2026. R9 and R9a (OQ 79): the envelope's depth in the order's shape, with
the bed mould held at 2½ in. R8 and R8a: the gable end draws what the resolved kit says of
`cornice_return`, and a stated return runs as far as the cornice is tall.*

## I. What was asked

Lucas's rulings of 29 September 2026, each asked as a direct question and recorded in its
question's own file:

- **R9, on OQ 79.** The envelope's depth (facade-classical's module/14) with Gibbs's shape. The
  member heights are kept and the projections scaled, so the face and the inset draw one cornice.
- **R9a.** The bed mould is held at 2½ in, the bed-mould fault's own figure after Benjamin, and
  labelled a judgment. The members above share the rest of the depth in Gibbs's proportions.
- **R8, on `oq/the-gable-end-draws-a-full-cornice-whatever-the-kit-says-of-the-return`.** The gable
  end draws what the resolved kit says of `cornice_return`:
  - forbidden, or `none` canonical: no band crosses the gable end;
  - a stated return runs out, then stops;
  - silence keeps the band, and the sheet says the return is unstated.
- **R8a.** A stated return is as long as the cornice is tall, the pork-chop fault's own rule:
  24.6 in on Tidewater. It is labelled a judgment, and the kit's 12–24 in band is noted as written
  for an 11.7 in cornice.
- **Read with the ruling, not ruled:** cape-cod-colonial's plain 6–12 in return is licensed by the
  stunted-return fault's own exception, so it keeps its own band, drawn at the band's middle and
  labelled a judgment.
- **Left open by the ruling:** the rake.

**3. What each face draws: `elevation.gable_faces`, `elevation.cornice_return`,
`cornice_marks`.**

- **Which faces are gable ends** is read off the roof record's own form and ridge. A hip has none;
  a form the generator does not model is `None`, unjudged and never "no gable". The axis-to-walls
  mapping is `threshold.gable_end_walls`', its one spelling.
- **An eave face** carries the cornice along it. It turns each corner where the gable end carries
  something round it (a return or the band), and stops at the corner where the gable end draws
  none. The band's extension past a corner IS the return seen end on.
- **A gable end** carries what `cornice_return` says:
  - `returns`, each with its frieze box, its cornice box, its members and its teeth;
  - or `end_profiles` at the corners;
  - or the band.

  `wall_to_rake` is the run between the corners where the wall goes up to the rake.

On the Tidewater W face the returns stand at u −0.877 to 2.046 ft and 37.773 to 40.697 ft, each
24.558 in long, with the wall running to the rake between them.

**4. The gable wall and the rake.**

- **On the sheet.** A gable end with no band across it draws its wall as one polygon from the
  ground to the rake (`data-wall-to-rake`). The coursing runs up the gable to the rake.
  - The triangle keeps its `rf` mark and is filled as wall (`.gw`). The rake is drawn over it as
    the roof's edge (`.rk`).
  - No shingles are drawn on a gable end.
  - Returns carry `data-return`, and end profiles carry `data-end-profile`, constructed by

  - Each member line carries its return side.
  - The end profiles have a layer of their own (`TDL-ELEV-CORNICE-END`), because a section is not a

## IV. What was found

1. **The rake overhang was invented, and a serious fault convicted every drawn house on it.** Since
   WP-3.2 (`f3d89f6`, 23 Aug 2026), `rake_overhang_in` published `cornice["cornice_projection_in"]`,
   the EAVE cornice's projection, as the rake's overhang. No record models a rake overhang, and
   every surface draws the roof stopping at the gable wall. The figures ran 8.612 to 11.481 in, and
   `flush-rake` holds the figure to 4–8 in in a secondary test. Since R4 a failed test makes a fault
   present, so The Cardboard Gable convicted all eleven drawn plans on a number nobody measured.
   Its governing test reads the raking member's thickness, which nothing supplies. So the verdict
   is could-not-evaluate now, which is the truth about a member no layer draws.
2. **A count of profiles carried onto a return was invented too.**
   `count_of_moulding_profiles_carried_around_onto_the_return` published the cornice's member count
   on every house with a cornice, since WP-3.2. That includes the gable ends crossed by a band,
   where nothing returns. It reached no verdict, because the pork chop's governing test never ran.
   It is published only where a full return is drawn now.
3. **A band was credited to the wrong node.** `return_at` first named the slot's binder as the
   writer of the return's depth band. greek-revival-upland-vernacular restates the band at 10–18 in
   from its own `typical_ratios`, in an `extends` delta over georgian-colonial-american's record,
   and its words would have read *"GEORGIAN-COLONIAL-AMERICAN'S KIT STATES 10–18 IN, A BAND WRITTEN
   FOR AN 11.7 IN CORNICE"*. Both clauses were false. `_extends` records only the last merge step,
   so it could not say which delta stated a figure. `_param_writers` does. The "written for an
   11.7 in cornice" note is added only to the band R8a is about, 12–24 in. Three mutations of the
   writer bookkeeping are each red (§VI). greek-revival-upland-vernacular is outside the
   elevation's gate, so no drawing reaches it today; the words are driven by a test.
4. **good-05 is convicted for a gable end its style says it does not have.** A4 bound
   italian-renaissance-revival's return forbidden because *"This style's own roof leaves a return
   nowhere to stand"*: its kit's roof is *"Low-pitched hipped roof with no gable facing any
   principal elevation"*. good-05 declares no roof form, and `threshold.roof_form_for` falls back to
   side-gable without reading the style's kit.
   - So the elevation draws two gable ends, honours the ban with end profiles, and
     `return-that-never-returns` (serious, `applies_to: universal`) convicts the house.
   - The fault excepts five styles whose kits refuse a return, not the Italianate family.
   - Twelve of the sixteen shipped roofs are drawn in a form their style's kit does not make
     canonical: nine by the fallback, and three (Tidewater, the spec Colonial, bad-04) because the
     record declares that form.

   Neither the fallback nor the exception is this package's to change:
   `oq/the-roof-is-drawn-side-gabled-whatever-the-style-says`.
5. **The gable triangle was drawn as roof.** Seen end on, both roof planes are edges, so the
   triangle above the eave is the gable wall and the roof is its rake. The sheet filled it as roof
   and coursed it with shingles. The wall stopped at the roof record's eave, drawing a line across
   the gable at a height where nothing is. On a gable end the band crossed, the band hid both.
   Once R8 took the band away, the drawing showed what the band had covered.
6. **The ruling's question quoted the cyma, and the bed mould's outer face is the fillet.** The
   control published `bed_mould_projection_in` as the first bed member's projection, the cyma's:
   2.842 in on Tidewater at the order's own relief. The question put to Lucas said a uniform scale
   makes the bed mould 1.2 in on Tidewater and 1.0 on the spec Colonial. Those are the cyma's
   figures.
   - Under a uniform scale the group's outer face, the fillet, would stand at 1.33 to 1.77 in over
     the eleven drawn plans. That is under the fault's 1.5 in floor on seven (the spec Colonial's
     1.33, bad-05's and good-02's 1.48) and above it on four (Tidewater's and good-03's 1.62,
     good-05's and good-07's 1.77). The cyma would be under the floor on all eleven, 1.00 to 1.33.
   - The ruling holds the outer face at 2½ in, and the cyma stands at 1.875 in beneath it. The
     ruling stands either way. OQ 79's closing section records the correction to the question's
     figures.
7. **One record sentence contradicted itself on bad-03.** `projection_ruling` read *"The order's
   own cornice projects 26.79 in at this height (Gibbs: projection equals height)"* over a cornice
   20.09 in tall. WP-16.4 refused new-urbanist-traditional's modillions and read the six members
   left to the envelope's height, and their projections grew with them. The parenthesis is said now
   only where it is true, and bad-03's names the members that left. A test holds both.
8. **The caption's wrap width was an estimate, and R9's longer line was the first to reach the
   frame.** `_wrap` took 4.3 px a character. Measured in Chromium, 51 characters of the caption's
   own 7.5 px monospace lay at 229.55 px, 4.5 a character, so a full line ran 7.5 px into the plate's
   right margin. It is 4.5 now. `test_unpublished_projections`' ghost-caption test, which read a
   caption sentence out of the raw SVG, then failed, because the sentence now breaks across two
   `<text>` elements. It reads the joined text now, as the re-cut OQ 79 test does.
9. **A dead local was quoted as the assumption.** `render_elevation`'s `is_gable_end = face in
   ("E", "W")` had been read by nothing since WP-14.6. The gable-end question quoted it as the
   assumption about which faces are gable ends. It is removed, and `elevation.gable_faces` reads
   the roof record.
10. **Two mutations were equivalent, and the filter was why.** Publishing the return's length, and
    re-inventing the rake overhang, both left the suite green. `NOT_MODELLED` filters those names at
    the point measurements are returned, so neither mutation could reach a fault. Each was re-run
    with its `NOT_MODELLED` entry removed as well, and both went red. The filter is the guard, and
    it is exercised.
11. **OQ 79's open state was written into five comments and two drawn sentences.** Two comments in
    `elevation.py`, two in `render_elevation.py` and one in `tests/test_cornice_face.py` said
    nothing chooses between the two figures. The inset's caption and the DXF's text said both were
    sourced, and that the face drew the envelope's. Each is corrected in the commit that made it
    false.
12. **The sheet said colonial-revival's kit permits a return, and its own condition says it does not
    on six of the seven plans.** colonial-revival's `cornice_return` permits the full return, with
    nothing canonical, and states `min_cornice_projection_for_return` as 10 in from its own c04:
    *"A cornice return is permitted only where the eave carries a full classical cornice of at least
    10 in. projection"*. The drawn cornice is the envelope's: 8.6 in on four of those plans, 9.6 on
    two, 11.5 on good-07. The words said *"COLONIAL-REVIVAL'S KIT PERMITS ONE AND SETTLES NONE"*
    over all seven. `return_at` reads the condition with its writer now, and the sheet says it
    beside the drawn figure. **The drawing is unchanged**, because a failed condition read as a
    ban is A3's reading carried to another axis, and it would convict six plans:
    `oq/a-return-permitted-only-over-a-deeper-cornice-is-drawn-over-a-shallower-one`. It was found by
    reading the words the package had printed against the record they claimed to summarise, after
    the rest of the package was built.

## V. What was not done

- **The roof-form fallback is not changed, and the fault gains no exception** (§IV.4). Both are
  rulings: `oq/the-roof-is-drawn-side-gabled-whatever-the-style-says`.
- **The rake is drawn as the roof's edge and carries no member.** No record dimensions a gable's
  rake, and the ruling left that half open:
  `oq/the-rake-is-drawn-as-an-edge-and-carries-no-member`.
- **`elevation.stack_outline` and `near_end_last` still take E and W to be the gable faces.** That
  is true of every shipped plan: all sixteen are side-gabled with the ridge on x. So no plan can
  drive the case, and it is named here rather than changed. The scene reads the ridge's axis.
- **No tooth width is stated for gibbs-ionic's modillions**, so on the shipped returns the band is
  drawn solid and says so, as on the eave (WP-15.7). A return lays whole teeth along its own run
  where a width is stated, and a test drives that.
- **cape-cod-colonial's plain return is drawn by the census sweep and by no shipped plan.**
- **A return a kit permits only over a deeper cornice keeps the band over a shallower one**, and
  the sheet says so (§IV.12). Whether the failed condition is a ban is
  `oq/a-return-permitted-only-over-a-deeper-cornice-is-drawn-over-a-shallower-one`.
- **`bed_mould_projection_in` is held absent by census V8, on every drawn plan, and by
  `TestOneCornice`**, and not by `tests/test_forbidden_slots.py`'s `WITHHELD_EVERYWHERE`, which the
  plan named. That list is a two-style differential about forbidden slots, and V8 is the general
  guard.

**Answered the same evening (30 September 2026).** After this package's code had landed, the three
questions this section names were put to Lucas as direct questions, and each answer took the
recommended reading. They are B1 to B4 in `PLAN-OF-ACTION.md`, and each question's own file
records the question as put and the answer as given:

- **B1:** a record that names no roof form takes its style's canonical form, after the inherited
  roof records a style contradicts are corrected in its own kit;
- **B2:** `return-that-never-returns` gains no exception and is gated on a drawn gable end;
- **B3:** the rake is drawn to The Cardboard Gable's own figures, as a judgment withheld from the
  fault;
- **B4:** where a kit states a minimum cornice for its return, the cornice is drawn at least that
  deep.

WP-16.9 executes them. Nothing in this package was changed by them.

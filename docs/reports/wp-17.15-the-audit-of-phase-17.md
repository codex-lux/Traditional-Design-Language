# WP-17.15: the audit of Phase 17

**29 Oct 2026.** This audit asks what is wrong with the redrawn workbench, not whether it looks done.

## How it was run

Three independent auditors each worked from the code and not from the reports, and the lead did one
sweep of their own. Every finding was reproduced before anything was touched.
- **Claims (read-only).** Re-derived every figure and behaviour the seven Phase 17 reports, the board
  and `docs/workbench.md` state, against the tree.
- **Mutation (its own worktree).** About 75 mutations of the code each new guard holds, each asserted
  to have landed and restored byte for byte.
- **Rendered contrast (read-only, its own server).** Read the computed colour of every text element
  on 24 routes, in two passes: no plan, and the walk's drawable record on the bench. Each was measured
  against the background actually painted behind it.
- **The lead's sweep.** The naive comment regex against the shared lexer, over every source file.

## I. The finding that decides the phase: the dark ground did not read

**Phase 17's central claim was that no word reads below 4.5 : 1 on either ground. On the rendered
page it was false.**
- 407 words failed on 18 routes with no plan on the bench, and about 400 more with a plan on it.
- The worst was 1.26 : 1: `--ink`, the paper ground's body ink, set as type on the dark ground.
- The token-table guard was green throughout.

**It read the tokens the guard chose, and words reach the page by four other routes:**

1. **A pigment set as a word.**
   - `--gilt-deep` (2.93 on `--heavy`), `--brick` (3.43), `--green-deep` (3.17) and `--salmon-deep`
     (4.43) read on paper because they are dark, and fail on the instrument for the same reason.
   - The primitive ratchet polices neutrals by design, because a pigment is ground-free, so these
     passed it.
2. **A severity duty that was never rebound.** `--sev-fatal` and `--sev-serious` resolved at `:root`,
   to their paper pigments, on every ground. Those were 324 of the failures on the faults pages alone.
3. **A drawing file's HTML caption.**
   - The drawing exemption excused whole files, so an `AssemblyPlate` member key and an `EdgeGlyph`
     lineage label set `--ink` as HTML text on the dark page.
   - A drawing's ink is its `fill` and `stroke`; its `color` is type.
4. **A word on its own severity field.** A finding row is its severity's wash over the panel. The
   minor word read 3.89 on its own row, and the secondary grey 4.47.

**The fix:**
- **Three lit pigments**, each its pigment mixed toward paper by the least step that reads 4.5 : 1 on
  every dark surface and on its own field. The steps are computed, not chosen: `--salmon-lit` (salmon-deep at 0.20),
  `--gilt-lit` (gilt at 0.16) and `--green-lit` (green-deep at 0.33).
- **On the instrument the severity duties take them**, and a fatal takes `--salmon`, so fatal and
  serious stay two inks. The vellum block restores every value.
- **The severity fields are 0.08 on the instrument**, against 0.11 and 0.12 on paper.
- **82 `color:` sites in 29 files moved from pigments to duties**:
  - `--gilt-deep` became `--text-accent`;
  - `--brick` and `--refusal` became `--refusal-text`;
  - `--green-deep` became `--sev-cleared`;
  - `--ink` and `--ink-2` became the text duties.

  The duties are those pigments on vellum, so paper reads exactly as before.
- **Re-measured with the auditor's own scripts on the first rebuild** (before the last three fixes
  below): 407 to 37 failing words with no plan on the bench, and about 400 to 32 with one on it.
  - On the instrument, what remained was disabled controls (`--text-disabled`, an inactive control),
    and the cascade's `✕` in `--bind-forbidden`, which §8 then caught.
  - A toned chip's word, and the bench's green disclosure line through a `TONE` map, were fixed in
    the last three.
  - On vellum, everything that remained is the paper rows below: the open question's.
  - **Re-measured on the final build: on the instrument, no live word reads below 4.5 : 1.**
    - 6,301 text elements in pass 1 and 2,877 in pass 2.
    - The seven left on the dark ground are all inactive controls: "fit" while the plate already
      fits, "−" at the smallest zoom, and Compose before a brief is ready.
    - The other 48 are on vellum, and each is a paper row named below: gilt-deep, salmon-deep and
      green-deep words on the fault card and the export cards, and export's disabled chips.

**Four guards hold it now (`grounds.test.mjs` §3, §6, §7, §8):**
- every duty a word is set in, on both grounds (was four on vellum and seven on the instrument);
- no `color:` names a raw pigment or neutral, in any file, drawing files included;
- every severity word and the text beside it reads on its own field;
- every token any `color:` names reads 4.5 : 1 at its instrument value.
  - That last one is the probe that found the cascade's ban mark set in `--bind-forbidden`, a ladder
    token, after the pigment sweep.
  - It names four exceptions by identity, each with its reason.

**The paper ground is not changed, and its rows are named.** Widened to every duty, the guard measured
the full set on vellum. Every row is older than Phase 17, and the phase moved none of them:
- `--sev-serious` 2.71 on paper;
- `--text-tertiary` and `--sev-info` 3.15;
- `--refusal-text` and `--sev-fatal` 3.50;
- `--sev-cleared` 3.78;
- `--text-accent` and `--sev-minor` 4.09.

They are held by identity (`KNOWN`), and `oq/two-inks-set-as-small-text-read-below-aa` carries them
in a dated amendment. Darkening a paper pigment moves the colour of every fatal finding, which is
that question's ruling to make.

## II. Three claims that were false of the tree (blocking)

1. **The binding bar's parts wore the kit's binding words.**
   - `binding-specified` says "the style fixes this slot itself", over a count of resolved kits. Of
     the cornice's 86 styles in that count, 57 inherit the slot.
   - `binding-open` says "takes whatever its ancestors specify", over the styles where no ancestor
     specifies anything.
   - Four records now name the resolved parts (`slot-reach-*`), and the route's own note is printed
     under the bar.
2. **A "placed" candidate was defined as one whose type's facts all held.**
   - `typefacts.refusal` refuses on a broken fact only. So a placement with every fact unjudged is
     placed, as `bad-06` is.
   - The disc says what is true: the placement was judged and refused nothing, and an unjudged fact
     is counted as neither held nor broken.
   - `candidate-placed` and `docs/workbench.md` say so now.
3. **"Where two styles part" depended on which style was named first.**
   - It took the first shared id in A's flattened cascade, and the auditor measured A→B and B→A
     disagreeing on 1,003 of 1,500 random pairs.
   - It is the shared node with the least summed depth, ties broken by name. 0 of 400 measured pairs
     disagree.
   - "Above it they share everything" is false of a graph and is withdrawn. Each style's own levels
     are the ids the other's cascade lacks.

## IV. The guards that could not fail

Of the mutation auditor's runs, 19 landed on a real defect and left their guard green. Each guard has
an assertion now, and each was re-run against its own mutation (below).
- **The paper rules matched by prefix**, so `.tdl-plate-caption`, which stands on the instrument, was
  exempted as `.tdl-plate`.
- **Nothing held every surface on the instrument.**
- **The datum overlay's fixture put its origin at grade.** `render_elevation` puts it at the top, so
  dropping `at_origin_ft` from the arithmetic would mis-place every datum on every real plate.
- **The binding bar's fixture had `open` equal to the remainder**, so a bar that counted the styles
  itself was indistinguishable from one that read the route.
- **The slot's `extends` was held only as a subset of the specified**, and the variant bans were not
  held at all.
- **The chronology rule's corpus population is one edge, 150 years early**, so the slack and the
  `regional_of` kind were free. Both are now driven at the boundary.
- **The chips' struck and hatched forms, the sidebar's compact pages and its open Styles, the front
  door tile's own mark, the module order against an already-alphabetical fixture, and the site
  diagram's east, west and corner edges.**

Three mutations proved equivalent and are named as such:
- the elevation plate is always the frame's first;
- the unstated lot's dimensions are never read;
- dropping `compare` from the sidebar's Styles pages changes nothing, because Styles also opens
  whenever one of its own items is current, and Compare is one. That was measured when its new
  assertion stayed green under the mutation.

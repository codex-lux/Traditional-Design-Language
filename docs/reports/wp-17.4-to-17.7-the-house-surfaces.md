# WP-17.4 to WP-17.7: the house surfaces on the instrument

**Phase 17, 29 Oct 2026.** The handoff is `docs/ui-redesign-2026-10-29.md`, boards 2 to 5.

The Candidate Set, the Plan Workbench, the Brief and the Drawing Set stand on the dark instrument
now: each row of `grounds.js`'s `SURFACE_GROUND` moved in this commit. Their drawings, columns and
receipts are on vellum.

This one report covers four packages. They share every file they touch (the ground table, the
glossary, `docs/workbench.md`'s Phase 17 section), and they were verified by one walk.

## WP-17.4: the Candidate Set

**Each candidate is a vellum column**, and the picked one takes the gilt.

**The column opens with how the type's own facts stand on it**, in three forms that
`candidateStanding.standingOf` decides:
- refused: a brick band with the facts that could not hold;
- placed: a green disc, with the new record `candidate-placed`;
- unjudged: the hatched ring and *may be refused*, with the new record `candidate-may-be-refused`.

**The score says its own denominator.**
- It reads `66.3 of 100 · 94 of 100 weight judged`, every figure from the payload.
- `scoreOf` is `score_model.of`, passed through the adapter.
- The column used to type "out of 100", a literal the corpus did not state.

**The decision log is a vellum receipt** between dashed rules.

## WP-17.5: the Plan Workbench

**Findings are pinned where they fall** (`sheet/pins.js`).
- There is one pin per room, at the room's north-east corner, clear of its label.
- It carries the count and the worst severity's ink.
- A pin is the room's own filter, the same act as clicking the room.
- A finding naming no room is never placed; it stays in the list.

**The style control is labelled as what it does**: *Judge this plan as*, a new record.

**The sheet marks itself vellum.** The list was already grouped by layer, with the severity tally
and one card open at a time, and none of that was rebuilt.

**Not done, and why: the relaxation marks stay triangles.** The board draws them as gilt diamonds.
But the bench's mark is held to the Python plate's by the frozen sheet-symbol contract
(`tests/fixtures/sheet_symbols/`), and changing one renderer's mark would part the two. That is a
ruling about both renderers, so it is not this package's to make.

## WP-17.6: the Brief

**The site is drawn** (`brief/siteDiagram.js`, drawn by `brief/SiteDiagram.jsx`):
- the lot to scale, or a dashed square that says it is unstated, never a default lot;
- the street on the side the entrance faces, or the corner for a diagonal;
- the compass's eight points as the control. Each is a button reachable by keyboard, and it
  replaces the row of chips.

The feasibility and the count of silences were already in place.

## WP-17.7: the Drawing Set

**The one-building banner** carries the placement's input digest and its engine, so two sheets can
be seen to be of one house.

**The datum overlay.**
- The server forwards the datums the elevation states (`corpus.elevation_datums`): grade, the
  floors, the water table, the eave, each with the field it came from.
- A leaf maps them through the plate's own `data-frame` (`sheet/datumOverlay.js`), so they
  register by construction.
- Where the record states none, the control is absent. *(Corrected 29 Oct 2026, WP-17.15: grade is always stated, as the plate's own datum, so the control is never absent; the other datums appear only where the record states them.)*

**The evaluated count** says how many faults ran (present or clear) out of every fault, with the
unjudged and the not-applicable in the total and never among the evaluated.

**The plate and the Round are vellum.**

## What was found

1. **The hero's cost order made a bad example the front door.**
   - `corpus.first_drawable` searches cheapest first, and the cheapest drawable record is "Bad
     Example 6". The front door now takes a separate hint, `HERO_ORDER`, under the same rule.
   - Measured: a good-first order reached `good-03` in 118 s cold, because `good-01` and `good-02`
     each spend a whole proof budget before being refused. With `good-03` asked first it takes
     37 s.
   - The sweep is warmed in a background thread at startup and serialised behind a lock, so a
     first visitor and the warm do not solve twice. *(Corrected 29 Oct 2026, the second audit: the startup hook never ran -- Starlette ignores `on_event` beside a `lifespan` -- and was removed; the first request starts the sweep.)*
   - The test re-derives that every record asked before the hero's is refused by the same verdict
     the tests read.
2. **A failed hero request had printed "refused".** A failure is not a refusal, so it says
   unjudged now.
3. **The Faults page's filter strip overflowed by 14 px at 1280 px.** The rail's stub grew from
   26 to 52 px, which the walk's width block measured. The strip takes the reflow ruling's `wrap`.
4. **The site drawing set pigments in an interface file, and the ground guard caught it.** A
   drawing's ink is a pigment, so the drawing moved to its own file and joined the named drawing
   files.

# WP-17.2: the shell on the instrument

**Phase 17, 29 Oct 2026.** The handoff is `docs/ui-redesign-2026-10-29.md`, "The shell".

## I. What was built

**The sidebar** (`Chrome.jsx` `LeftRail`, shaped by the new leaf `nav/sidebar.js`) sits on the
dark instrument, coal and 236 px. From the top:
- **The wordmark.** It is the site map's front-door item and carries that item's `data-nav`.
- **The house on the bench.** The plan's name and its counts, then the five steps as stations.
- **The library.** Five rows: Styles, Proportions, Faults, Elements, Glossary. Styles' own places
  (the style in hand, the family tree) are its sub-rows only while the reader is among them.
- **Search and the keys**, at the foot.

`navModel` is unchanged, ids and all. The sidebar reshapes its answer and decides nothing about
the places.

**The journey bar is retired into the spine** (`components/JourneySpine.jsx`).
- The house is the sidebar's own block on every page: stations on a vertical rule, each carrying
  the journey mark `journey/spine.js` gives it, with its state in italic prose. Next and the
  origin come below.
- On a library page the five stations shrink to five marks in a row, still links.
- What is a link and what is blocked is still `journey/bar.js`'s `barView`. A blocked step is a
  `<span data-blocked>`. Every hook the bar carried stays on the spine.
- `oq/the-journey-bars-next-jumps-rows-when-the-strip-wraps` is CLOSED: Next has its own line now.

**The top bar** (`TopBar`, 48 px, coal):
- the crumbs as an eyebrow;
- the address in the corpus's grammar, in mono;
- one *Ask the corpus* button that opens the assistant.

While the sidebar is folded, the wordmark, search and the keys stand in the top bar, so none is
ever off the screen.

**The assistant opens only when summoned.**
- `NARROW_FOLD_PX` is `Infinity`: the pane starts folded at every width unless the reader has
  stored a choice.
- Its stub is 52 px with a vertical label.
- Its self-description is deleted. The "assistant is off" disclosure is kept, because it says
  something a reader cannot otherwise see.

**The state words became glossary records.**
- Ten journey state words are now records (`journey-state-*`), each quoting the table in
  `docs/workbench.md`'s new Phase 17 section.
- `ask-the-corpus` is a record too.
- The words are set in italic serif, not mono.

**The shell stands on the instrument** (`SHELL_GROUND = 'instrument'`). Each surface's `<main>`
stays on vellum until its own package.

**The page head's eyebrow is gilt**, because it names the page's subject, and its sentence is
set larger.

## II. What was found

1. **The stylesheet's own class rules named pigments, and the first ratchet scanned JavaScript
   only.**
   - The first dark render drew the page head's sentence in `--ink` on `--heavy`, because
     `.tdl-page-head-what` named the pigment.
   - 27 class rules moved onto duties. `grounds.test.mjs` now reads the stylesheet's rules too.
   - Five rules are named as paper by construction: the vellum box, the plate, `body`, the Term's
     popover, and a vellum `<main>`.
2. **A vellum `<main>` painted no paper.** Rebinding the duties is not painting a ground, so
   `main[data-ground="vellum"]` paints its paper now.
3. **The wordmark is one word to the line-breaker.** The middle dots join "Traditional·Design·
   Language" into a single unbreakable word, which overflowed 236 px. It takes `<wbr>` after each
   dot.
4. **The walk's site-map check had to move with the sidebar.** The walk built its expected rail
   from `flatItems(navModel)`. The sidebar draws the front door as the wordmark, omits blocked
   steps as links, and opens Styles' sub-rows only in place. The walk reads `drawnItems(sidebarView(...))`
   now, the module the sidebar draws from.
5. **A station is marked current as a step of the journey (`aria-current="step"`), not as a
   page.** The walk's every-item-reaches-its-surface loop accepts either now.

# WP-13.4 — refuse to draw

*16 September 2026. Branch `claude/chomsky-plan-coherence-u6s9wv`. Two slices in isolated
worktrees against one written contract — the server (`61bd54f`) and the app (`85c5792`) — then
the lead's pass. Cite this report by filename (OQ 90).*

Lucas ruled on 15 Sep: **a placement that breaks a hard fact of the type is REFUSED, not drawn.
The bench shows the conflict set; the brief or the parti is what changes.** This reverses, for
every user-facing surface, the 25 Aug ruling pinned at `tests/test_solver.py` that a partner
hears the refusal and still sees a drawing. The RECORD keeps the least-bad placement — it is the
conflict set's explanation and the wall-drag's working sketch — and no surface draws it.

## I. The contract, and that it was not moved

`typefacts.refusal(plan)` returns `None` or one shape: `kind` (`infeasible` |
`type-fact-downgraded`), `facts`, `conflicts`, `lines`, `engine`, `status`. `lines` is always a
flat list of sentences so a plate, a bench panel and a CLI print the same thing; `conflicts` is
the evidence. **Unjudged does not refuse** — a house stating no hearth is not refused for it —
and unjudged is not held either.

`corpus._placed` returns `{"error", "refused_placement", "unsolved"}` and **never the key
`refusal`**, which `app.py` maps to a 501 "missing library". Routes answer 422 with the typed
dict; `/api/plan/evaluate` answers 200 with `placement_refused` and no `placement`; the
wall-drag path, which asks for the search by name, returns `placement.sketch` and may be drawn
as a WORKING sketch and never exported.

Three additions are declared rather than smuggled: `typefacts.judge` writes the measurement and
the verdict together so no caller can take one without the other; `geometry._refuse` sits beside
`_disclose` because the infeasible block lands *after* it at two sites; and `export_ifc` gains a
`geometry_result` parameter. One deviation from the brief's wording is stated: `refusal()` does
not import `geometry.conflict_lines`, because `typefacts` is a leaf and that would close a cycle
— the two readers are held to one sentence by a test instead, which is this repository's own
answer where an import is not available.

## II. What the packages found

1. **`/api/export/ifc` never went through `_placed`.** It handed the DECLARED plan to
   `export_ifc`, which called `structure.build_section` with no `geometry_result` and took that
   function's heuristic default — the one its own comment reserves for *"INTERNAL callers
   ONLY"*. The IFC a reader downloaded was a placement of a **different house** from the plan
   sheet beside it. That is WP-6.4's *one drawing set is one building* surviving in the single
   export branch nobody moved, and it was found because the refusal could not reach it.
2. **The Drawing Set was still certifying a proof, and it is the THIRD spelling WP-13.2 did not
   find.** Its elevation caption decided *"placed by proof (CP-SAT)"* from a ternary on the
   engine's NAME, which cannot tell OPTIMAL from a FEASIBLE truncation. Measured on a running
   server, that plate printed a proof over a placement the API reports FEASIBLE.
3. **Nine rewraps flattened a refusal to its sentence one frame above where it was made** — four
   in the drawing and scene paths, and, found by mutation rather than by reading, one in
   `export_plan_dxf` and three in `export_all`.
4. **The bench's status line read "placing…" for three different states**: a refused placement, a
   200 carrying neither placement nor error, and a placement with no relaxations. A reader
   watched a spinner over a house the server had already turned down.
5. **A button that appeared to work and did not.** `saveSvg` was a raw fetch with `if (r.ok)` and
   no else, so a 422 downloaded nothing and said nothing; `saveCad` painted every failure in the
   token that surface reserves for *designed and not built*, so a refused house read as a missing
   feature.
6. **`_disclose` is not idempotent**, measured rather than reasoned: the obvious re-judge would
   have silently deleted the at-grade disclosure, because it rebuilds `lot_extent` from scratch
   while `_disclose_at_grade` writes into that row afterwards.

## III. The honest cost of the ruling, and the ruling it produced

Swept with the cache cold, **before** the capacity ruling below: on `auto`, **15 of 16 shipped
plan records refuse** and **19 of 21 partis** instantiated against their own first native style.
Every one of them was refused for `bearing`.

**And the dominant cause was a fact the corpus itself grades serious rather than fatal.**
`typefacts.bearing` returned one verdict for two halves, continuity and span capacity, and the
capacity half alone refused **33 of the 37** records and partis swept. Neither slice narrowed it
— the written contract said refuse on a downgrade, and a contract is not narrowed unilaterally —
so the measurement was published and the question put.

**Lucas ruled the same day: "capacity shouldn't refuse a drawing — continuity only".** That is
executed in `typefacts.bearing`: the status is continuity's alone, capacity is still measured and
still reported, and `capacity_refuses` is on the row so no reader infers it. Re-measured
afterwards on `engine="heuristic"`, the deterministic one, both columns from one run:

| | before the ruling | after |
|---|---|---|
| shipped records with no type fact downgraded | **2 of 16** | **12 of 16** |

Eleven records move, and **eight of them have continuity UNJUDGED** — a one-level house, or a
record stating no bay module — which had been refused a drawing by capacity alone. The cp figure
is deliberately not re-quoted: CP-SAT under a wall-clock budget is not deterministic here.

**The shipped Tidewater record is now refused for one fact only: `stacks`.** All five declared
stacks are broken on the search engine, the landing sitting at 0% of the stair it names. That is
Lucas's own complaint 1 of 15 Sep, so the refusal that remains is the house's and not the
instrument's.

## IV. Guards

`tests/test_refusal.py` (13), `workbench/server/tests/test_refusal_routes.py` (16) with a shared
`drawable.py` fixture, and `workbench/app/src/refusal.test.mjs` (18). The node suite goes
191 → 209. Six of the app tests read the callers AS SOURCE and hold the one-spelling rule — every
surface imports the leaf, not one counts `"downgraded"` for itself — and each asserts its own
PREMISE first, so a pattern that could never match cannot pass as a guard.

Pins were rewritten and not loosened. `tests/test_solver.py`'s 25 Aug pin now asserts all three
of conflict set, least-bad placement and `geometry_report.refused`, with the split between record
and surface written down. Two face tests in `test_drawing_set_one_building.py` are NAMED and skip
with the refusal's own words rather than being substituted: four distinct drawings for four faces
is a property of a house with four different elevations, and turning that into a green tick on a
one-elevation record would convert a real guard into a true statement about nothing.

**36 mutations across the two slices, all red from a green baseline**, each asserted to have
LANDED before its colour was believed and each file restored byte-for-byte. Four were blind on
their first run and each was a different failure of the harness rather than of the code: one
matched two occurrences and never landed; one went green because its target test SKIPS without
the MCP SDK, and a skipped guard is a could-not-evaluate and not a pass; one mutated a frame the
guard could not see; and one matched a string that also appears in the line it was not about —
the shape WP-12.8 records, met again in a second saver's identical copy.

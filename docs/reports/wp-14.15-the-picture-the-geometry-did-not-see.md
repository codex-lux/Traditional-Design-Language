# WP-14.15: the picture the geometry did not see

*25 September 2026. Integration of Phase 14's first tranche, on `claude/fervent-babbage-pgi95c`.
Scope: the package text in `PLAN-OF-ACTION.md`, and the findings the fourteen packages above it
routed here. Cite this report by filename: Phase 14's numbers are this line's, and a number does
not disambiguate a package in this repository.*

## What it is for

Lucas read the Proportions surface on `trim-classical` and wrote that the workbench had become
*"all just a bunch of text, and I'm not sure how it all relates to each other."* Phase 14's first
tranche rebuilt the navigation around two spines, one style and one house, and put a definition
under every word. WP-14.0 through 14.14 each built one part in isolation, and each was verified in
isolation. This package puts the tranche in a real browser at three widths and looks at it. It
fixes what the looking found, and names what it did not fix.

**The headline is a defect no check could see, found by looking.** The loupe every drawing sits in
painted its text from a layout it had already left. That covers the Proportions plates, the Plan
Workbench sheet and the Drawing Set. Every geometry call in the browser reported the true layout,
so every check in the walk passed over it (§II.1).

## II. What was found

### 1. The loupe painted its text from a layout it had already left

`PlateViewer` measures its pane in a layout effect. Before that measurement, the stage is
`width: 100%` at `scale(1)`. The next commit gives it its measured width and its fitted scale
together. Chromium then **painted every SVG text from the first layout**, and every geometry call
returned the second: `getBBox`, `getBoundingClientRect` and `getScreenCTM` all agree with each
other, and with the paths, which painted true.

On `trim-classical` at 1440 px the fit is 0.83. The labels were painted 1.2× out from the plate's
left edge, which is 1/0.83:

- in the first section, the labels sat clear of their leaders' ends;
- the third section's names ran off the frame (*"Frieze, plain a"*, *"Architrave, or"*);
- the section titles ran into the next section's wall strip.

**The Plan Workbench sheet had the same defect.** On `good-03-parlor-drawing-room-house`, the
figures of the bay grid along the top were painted at 127, 193, 260, 326 and 393 px. The same
sheet laid out afresh puts them at 117, 173, 229, 285 and 341. The furniture keys and the side
dimension moved with them.

**How it was found, and what was ruled out on the way:**

- It was not any check. The walk's own assertion on that plate, *"every label inside its frame
  and none on another"*, reads `getBBox`, and it was green throughout.
- A probe of every text's box against its SVG's box found nothing outside, at all three widths.
- The screenshots showed the defect.
- The first hypothesis was hinted glyph advances. Canvas measures *"Wall field - the column of the
  order"* at 156 px and the SVG reports 171 px. A `textLength` on every line was built and
  measured, and it changed **nothing** in the picture, so it was reverted.
- A minimal page, with an SVG under a CSS `scale()` and the app's `text-rendering:
  optimizeLegibility`, painted correctly.
- In the app, pressing 1:1 and then fit painted correctly. The fault was only the first fit.

**The fix is one line in the shared loupe.** The stage's content is keyed on the measured pane
width. The plate is mounted afresh once the width is known, and again only when the pane is
resized. The text is then laid out under the width and scale it is painted at. Every surface that
uses the loupe gets the fix: the Proportions plates, the sheet and the Drawing Set.

**The guard compares pixels.** A check that reads geometry cannot see paint. The walk takes the
plate at its first fit, presses 1:1 and then fit, which lays it out fresh, and requires the two
screenshots to be byte-identical. Driven with the key removed:

| state | Proportions plate | plan sheet |
|---|---|---|
| without the key | differs, 2 of 2 runs | differs, 1 of 1 |
| with the key | identical, 2 of 2 | identical, 1 of 1 |

The sheet has no walk check of its own. Reaching it costs a minute of solving, and the fix is the
same line.

### 3. Filing does carry something, and three sentences said it did not

`README.md:24` said `member_of` *"exists for browsing and nothing else"*, and `docs/model.md:16`
said it *"does not carry inheritance"*. Both were written before WP-4.2 spliced a style's family
into its kit cascade, so a family's own kit reaches the styles filed under it. WP-14.11 found the
sentences false, and did not correct them, because three glossary records quoted them verbatim
and `check_glossary.py` verifies those quotations.

The sentences now say what filing carries: one thing, and only through a family. The records
`member-of` and `section-members` quote the new clause instead of the old ones. `surface-style`
quotes the new sentence. `docs/inheritance.md`, which had quoted the README's old words in its
own prose, quotes the new ones.

- Quotations verified: 482 → 480, because two old quotations came out of each of two records and
  one new one went in.
- Driven: changing *"through a family"* to *"via a family"* in the README turns
  `check_glossary.py` red on both records.

### 4. The pack's sources were not served

`GET /api/proportions/<id>` served every field the plate and the rules table read. It did not
serve `sources`, which 55 of 57 packs carry: eleven on `trim-classical`, from Vignola and Palladio
onward. The sources section printed only the authority line. The route now serves the resolved
pack's own list, `proportions/page.js::sourceLines` takes it in the record's order, each once and
never twice beside an authority line, and the page draws it under the glossary's
`bibliographic-source` word.

The field stays out of `core.get_proportions`, which is the MCP payload and is held byte-stable,
and `test_pack_plates.py`'s leak check says so. Driven:

- serving one source instead of the list turns the new route test red;
- dropping the dedupe turns the page-module test red.

### 5. `478d2c0`'s message makes a claim that measurement contradicts

The message says the 401 a gated hook got behind the Gate *"stood as a failure after the reader
signed in"*. The new walk check signs in on the page the Gate was on, with no reload, and on the
pre-fix code it **passes**. The readers that mount after the Gate opens call `load()`, and
`fetchOnce` retries a failed read.

The defect was the three signed-out calls, and only those. The walk's signed-out check catches
them: it is red on the pre-fix code. A pushed message cannot be rewritten, so the correction is in
`410ffa4`'s message and in `useGlossary.js`'s own comment, which had repeated the claim.

What the sign-in check does catch, driven: `Gate.jsx` not calling `onUnlocked` turns it red, with
0 rail items and the glossary never asked.

### 6. Two things that looked like defects and are not

- **The walk after 14.14 had one red: the rail's in-hand item marked nothing current.** It did not
  reproduce in six runs then, and it passed on the walk of the 14.12 merge and on this package's
  walk. It is recorded as unexplained. It ran under load from two agents' walks at once.
- **`#/brief?example=family-georgian` ends at `#/brief?style=tidewater-georgian`.** WP-14.10 built
  it this way on purpose: `?example=` is an act and not a place. It loads the example once. The
  address then names the example's style, so that a refresh keeps the reader's edits rather than
  loading the example over them.

## III. What was deliberately not done

All of these are tranche 2's, or need a ruling first.

- **The fault card's section labels** — *the right way*, *how to spot it*, *symptom*, *cause*,
  *cost saved*, *rule violated*, *fix*, *test*. They are app-written, and eight labels are one
  change. Two of them from glossary records and six from the app would be half a fix. The copy
  ratchet reads one of them, *"three tiers"*, as a count; the rest are short labels it does not
  read. They belong with "the copy ratchet to zero".
- **The copy ratchet itself** is at its floor. It fails when a baseline row no longer exists, so
  every package deleted its own rows as it went. Eight counts and fourteen long titles remain.
- **`--ink-2` is 4.32:1 on `--paper-deep`**, under 4.5, and 119 readable `--ink-4` uses remain.
  Changing an ink is a change to Graphic Standard No. 1, not an integration fix.
- **The signed-out boot still asks `/api/overview`**, and it answers 401. WP-14.13 left it
  because the obvious fix changes `/api/health`, which tranche 1 holds byte-stable.
- **The front door's inventory and *is not* sections carry no heading**, because no glossary
  record names them. The front door carries no `PageHead` (WP-14.14's stated deviation).
- **Smaller items, each named where it was found:**
  - The list route serves a pack's authority as an object and the detail route as a string.
    `authorityWords` reads both.
  - Five packs state their author as *"compiled"*.
  - The server accepts `term:about-tdl#x`, which `check_glossary.py` refuses.
  - `/api/phylogeny` serves no edge note.
  - A paste, an undo or a traced DXF records no plan origin.
  - Three faces reach behind the wall plane. They are WP-14.4's named exception, asserted
    exercised by the walk.
- **No new open question.** Every finding above was fixed, belongs to a named tranche 2 item, or
  was already filed.

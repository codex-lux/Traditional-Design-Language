# WP-17.3: the front door redrawn

**Phase 17, 29 Oct 2026.** The handoff is `docs/ui-redesign-2026-10-29.md`, board 1. Lucas's
words were *"extraordinarily bland, dense and impossible to intuitively navigate."*

## I. What was built

The front door stands on the instrument and reads, top to bottom:

1. **The thesis**: `about-tdl`'s definition at display size, under its term as a gilt eyebrow.
2. **One primary act and one secondary.**
   - *Continue the house* goes to the step the journey resumes at.
   - *Start a new brief.*
   - Both are new glossary records quoting `docs/workbench.md`.
3. **The hero plate** (`GET /api/front-door/hero`): the plan sheet of the first shipped record the
   product may draw, served whole so its disclosures come with it, in a vellum `<Plate>`.
4. **The five stations** on a rule (`frontDoor.stationTiles`): done is a vellum tile, the step in
   hand a dark tile with a gilt border, not yet a dashed outline. A blocked step is a tile and not
   a link.
5. **The library as five vellum cards** (`frontDoor.libraryCards`), which are the sidebar's own
   library rows. Each carries a drawn glyph (`frontdoor/glyphs.js`), its surface record's line and
   the API's count.
6. **The closing furniture**: the worked example, what the corpus holds, and what it is not.

**Deleted:**
- the three readers' paragraphs, which stay `about-tdl`'s;
- the two-spine map (`components/TwoSpineMap.jsx`, `frontdoor/spineMap.js`), because the sidebar
  is the map and the page no longer says it a second time;
- the two entrance boxes.

The entrance hooks the walk reads (`data-entrance-link`) ride on the new act and on the Styles
card.

## II. What was found

1. **The hero rule was a test helper's.** `workbench/server/tests/drawable.py` found the first
   drawable record for the server tests. It moved to `corpus.first_drawable()` and the helper
   imports it, so the hero and the tests cannot disagree about which house draws. *(Corrected 29 Oct 2026, WP-17.15: they share the RULE and ask it in different orders, so they find different records -- bad-06 for the tests, good-03 for the hero -- and agree only on the verdict of each record both ask.)*
   - It is memoised for the process, and `corpus.invalidate` clears it.
   - The route is metered only on the call that computes it.
   - On this tree it finds the open-concept render in one solve, and the route answers in about
     2 s cold.
2. **The hero is never assumed to be the Tidewater record.** That record is refused, and a front
   door that showed it as the product's drawing would claim what WP-13.4 forbids.
   - A guard asserts the hero's id is the one the tests' rule finds. *(Corrected 29 Oct 2026, WP-17.15: it asserts the hero is what the rule finds in the hero's own order; its loop over the records asked earlier ran over nothing, because the hero is the first asked, and the skip is now driven by a test of its own.)*
   - Where nothing is drawable, the plate says so as a refusal with the count it swept.

  journey's steps with its words and marks, linked exactly where the bar says; the cards are the
  sidebar's rows with the API's figures and a glyph each; and a source guard on the page. The
  copy guards no longer read the deleted map file.

  it is memoised and a reload clears it; and the rule lives in the server once.

  journey's five steps, the cards are the sidebar's rows, and the hero is drawn or refused by

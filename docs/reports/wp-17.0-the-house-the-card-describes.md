# Phase 17, first tranche — the house the card describes (WP-17.0 through WP-17.7)

*29 October 2026. One report for the first tranche of Phase 17, because its packages were built and
measured together against one complaint and one acceptance figure; each package has its own section.
Cite it by filename.*

## I. The complaint, and what it turned out to be

Lucas composed a brief on production — tidewater-georgian, 3,200 sf, four bedrooms, three and a half
baths, four candidates — and every candidate came back refused a drawing ("stacks, tiling not held").
Reproduced locally with CP-SAT available: **4 of 4 refused**, and across the whole thirteen-parti pool
every two-storey diagram was refused on every engine and budget tried. Earlier packages had fixed the
surfaces that SAY a house is refused (it ranks last, the strip names it, the tally counts it) and had
filed the cause in six open questions. This tranche goes after the cause.

**The cause is one thing.** The type's facts are enforced at the end (WP-13.4's refusal) and demanded
inside the prover (WP-13.3), but nothing upstream built a house to them, and the prover could not decide
what it was asked:

- the **composer** posed programmes the facts cannot hold — the foursquare's upper gallery corridor at
  13.5 x 87.6 ft, 1,166 sf, the midpoint of a 150-3,000 sf band and 57% of the upper programme; rooms
  sized from band midpoints, never from the room they stand over; seventeen plumbing claims (a bath over
  a pantry, a powder room, a kitchen) filed as STRUCTURAL stacks; and no `parti` on the plan record, so
  every later re-solve built the house on the placer's 10 ft bay instead of the diagram's;
- the **prover** asked bearing WITH capacity, posed every fact as an assumption literal (which forces
  CP-SAT onto one sequential worker), and tiled an inward-rounded integer box while the gate measured the
  float box;
- the **gate** read bearing continuity by line position only: 4 of 5 placements reading "held" carried
  an upper bearing wall standing over open floor, one of them the shipped `good-05`;
- and the **product** could carry one house as three placements — the composer's, the bench's re-solve
  at the interactive budget, and the Drawing Set's.

A read-only feasibility spike (every fact, band, size and door hard on CP-SAT, four workers, 120 s) is
what separated the instrument from the house: the foursquare was drawable with every room in band once
its corridor was sized from its own record; the side hall was PROVEN infeasible on its own parti's
stacks; the centre passage was infeasible as stated. *(Corrected 30 Oct 2026, the audit of Phase 17,
finding 42: the side hall's proof was of the parti BEFORE ruling 5 re-filed its bath-over-powder claim
as a wet stack. On the parti as it stands the prover places it at 110 s on Lucas's brief, drawable where
judged (hearth unjudged), measured twice; at the 60 s compose budget it is UNDECIDED, not proven.
`oq/the-side-hall-partis-stacks-cannot-co-hold-in-band` carries the amendment.)*

## II. The rulings (Lucas, 29 October 2026)

Put as direct questions in this session, each answered with the recommended option, and recorded here
as the act they are. The questions and the answers are two acts; the answers arrived on 29 Oct.

1. A placement that holds the type's facts outranks one that releases them, whichever engine
   (re-rules 4 Sep) — subject to ruling 6.
2. The prover's hard bearing fact is continuity plus at least one upper bearing line; capacity is a
   soft charge, still reported.
3. A short set is filled from the ranked pool; every passed-over diagram is named.
4. Derived massing elements are stated on the whole-foot grid; a wing is sized for its door set;
   `_absorb` runs per single-storey element.
5. Bath-over-wet-room `stacks_over` claims in the partis are reclassified as `wet_stack_with`.
6. Two placements are compared on the never-downgrade facts first (sizes, doors, rooms in band), then
   the type's facts.
7. The filled set keeps the 16 Oct key (judged fatals, standing, unjudged fatals, score).
8. Bearing continuity covers a wall's WHOLE run, within the 0.75 ft across-tolerance.

**A count was wrong when ruling 5 was asked for.** The question said fourteen claims in eleven partis;
the class is **seventeen in thirteen**. The ruling was executed on the class, and that is said here
rather than left for a reader to find.

## III. What each package built

**WP-17.0 — the instrument.** `build/drawability.py` reports, per placed record, each fact held /
downgraded / unjudged, the refusal CLASS of each released fact (PROVEN by the prover's own phrase,
UNDECIDED at budget, MISMATCH where the prover held it and the gate refused it, SEARCH), rooms drawn past
their band, unplaced door pairs, and the whole-run audit. Its first version read every carried release
as PROVEN because the carried note contains "re-proven"; the exact phrase is the reader now.
`tests/test_solver.py`'s restore count read every restore kind where it means wall pins; re-cut.

**WP-17.1 — the partis say what they mean (ruling 5).** Seventeen claims in thirteen partis moved to
`wet_stack_with`, each with a dated note; `compose.instantiate` copies the field; `stacking.wet_judge`
judges a wet stack as a shared chase (across levels, overlapping plans; on one level, a shared wall over
a foot); `plan_check`'s drawn layer reports `wet-stack-shares-no-chase` (minor); `check_plans` holds a
plan to its parti's wet claim. **The shipped Tidewater record is NOT re-filed**, on purpose: the placer
reads `stacks_over` while it slices, and re-filing it took the build's reds from the known 20 to 108 —
almost all of them pins on that one placement. It is reported as `wet-stack-filed-as-structural` under
its own ceiling of one (`oq/a-shipped-record-files-a-wet-stack-as-structural`).

**WP-17.2 — the composer poses a programme the type can hold.** `compose.compose_to_the_type`:
an open-band circulation room is sized from its record's working width and the wall its doors need
(`openings.required_wall_ft`, the one spelling); a room with `stacks_over` takes the plan of the room it
stands over, inside its own bands (editorial, and the decision log says so); the area that gave back —
about a thousand square feet on the foursquare — is grown back toward the brief in the rooms the pass
did not size, each inside its own area band, each stacked room following its target; the brief's
bathrooms are read and a shortfall SAID, never invented; the plan names its parti; and the entrance
front is read off the diagram's own entry (a porch declared S/E/W into a passage declared N/S is entered
on S), stated where the readings agree on one face and disclosed where they do not. The first version
wrote the front as a list; the schema states one compass point and the search raised on every composed
plan, which is why a composed plan is now validated against the plan schema in the test.

**WP-17.3 — one house, one placement.** Every placement carries `solver.input_digest`, the digest of the
declared record it was placed from (`geometry.declared_digest`, placement keys and the revision report
excluded). The bench's evaluate keeps a carried placement where that digest matches what the record
declares now and re-judges its verdict server-side (a forged `refused` on the posted record is not
read); the Drawing Set keeps one on the same rule and re-solves a record edited since; the wall drag
asks for the search by name and is never carried. Before this, a candidate the composer found drawable
was re-solved on the bench at 25 s and could come back refused.

**WP-17.4 — the prover asks the gate's question.** Bearing is continuity over the whole run plus at
least one upper bearing line, hard; capacity is the soft `SPAN_W` charge in both branches; derived
elements are stated on the grid and centred exactly (even depth differences); `_absorb` runs only in
single-storey elements where a bearing or stack fact is held; the dead band exemption is deleted.

**WP-17.5 — the prover can decide.** A hard-mode portfolio on as many workers as the cgroup quota allows
(`portfolio_workers`, `TDL_CP_WORKERS` to override), one solve at a time holding it; the footprint grows
2 and 4 ft in depth before a room is compromised (decision 11), because the derived footprint holds its
programme with no slack at all; the assumption ladder runs only where the portfolio does not decide, for
its cores. The undecided-scout policy sweep is NOT done and is not put to Lucas yet.

**WP-17.6 — the set pursues drawability (rulings 1, 3, 6, 7).** `geometry.BUDGET_COMPOSE_S = 60` per
placement, passed by name from the composer; the set's budget derived (a placement per asked candidate
and per pool-fill, bounded at 600 s); no candidate's loop may take more than a placement and a half;
the pool-fill places down the ranked pool, at most `POOL_FILL_PLACEMENTS = 4`, and every diagram it
places and finds refused is named with its reason, every one it did not reach is named; the job TTL is
counted from a job's FINISH and a running job is never reaped. `solve(engine="auto")` draws the search's
placement over a REFUSED proof only where the search holds every fact AND is no worse on each of the
three never-downgrade counts, compared one by one and never summed; the bench says so in its own words
and no longer calls a proof that answered "attempted and did not answer". The Candidate Set counts a
house filled from the pool out of the set's own number and names it, and names the passed-over ones.

**WP-17.7 — the gate reads whole runs (ruling 8).** `typefacts.bearing` downgrades a positional HELD

## VIII. What the attribution found in this tranche's own code, and fixed

The re-pins were split over three forked agents by file, each told to run its failing ids on the parent
first, to re-pin only against a harness that reproduces the old value, to drive any premise the corpus
stopped reaching, and to report a genuine regression rather than re-pin it. They reported four, and all
four are fixed here, each measured:

1. **The assumption ladder was starved by the portfolio** (`geometry_cp`, WP-17.5's split). After an
   undecided portfolio the ladder's deadline was `max(started + 0.45T, now + 0.1T)`, so a portfolio that
   spent its 0.75 share undecided left the ladder a tenth of the budget, and on the multi-element fixture
   `solve_cp(40 s)` returned UNKNOWN after 28.6 s with 11 s unused where the parent's prover closed
   FEASIBLE. The ladder keeps its own 0.45 share measured from when it starts. **Cost, stated:** on a
   house the prover cannot decide, a solve may now run to about 1.2 times its budget, which
   `geometry.solve`'s docstring already allows ("a target, not a hard wall").
2. **The retry after the type's facts are released ran sequentially under assumptions**, the
   formulation the portfolio exists to avoid, and timed out on the composer's own Tidewater candidate
   (`r1+ UNKNOWN`): the gate row "the sheet is drawn at all" went red on it. The retry is wanted as a
   placement, so it runs hard on the portfolio's workers first (`r1+ H:OPTIMAL`, the candidate placed)
   and falls back to the assumption form only for a core.
3. **A relaxation on a wing was never drawn on its wall.** Both spellings of the relaxation-mark rule
   (`render_plan.relaxation_marks`, `derive.js::relaxationMarks`) clipped every run to the main block,
   so the Tidewater record's wing relaxation (on the pantry's face) clipped to nothing and was listed as
   unlocated. Both clip to the building's extent now (`building_extent` / `buildingExtent`, one rule in
   lockstep); a one-rectangle house's extent is its block, so every one-element sheet is unmoved, and
   the harness with the extent disabled reproduces the previous sheet digest to the character.
4. **The decision classifier had no patterns for the type pass's own log lines** (31 of 40 live lines
   classified against a 0.8 floor). Patterns added, keyed on the composer's own words; and the stacked
   rooms' log lines were moved after the growth, because they reported a size the room held for the
   length of one loop.

**Costs carried forward, measured and not netted off:** the Tidewater record's search placement counts
six relaxations where it counted one (corpus 81 -> 86, fifteen plans unchanged), stated beside its fatal
findings 10 -> 6; `docs/fidelity.md` regenerated with `ezdxf==1.4.4`, the version it is generated with in
CI (a regeneration without it turned the four DXF rows into could-not-evaluate and was discarded), every
census row agreeing and six populations one sheet smaller -- the Tidewater W face, which draws no window
now that both west fires and their stack take that wall. *(Corrected 30 Oct 2026, the audit of Phase 17,
finding 52: it is six, not five -- V5, V7, V11, V14, V16 and X2. V11 was undriven before this tranche and
lost a third of its population here with nothing driven in its place; §X drives it.)*
